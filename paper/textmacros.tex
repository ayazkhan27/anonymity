\newcommand{\machineMethods}{\paragraph{Human and machine writing}
The ten measures are: hedons, dolors, net charge and total charge per thousand words; the percentage of words that are charged; the percentage of charge that is negated, intensified, and after a contrast word; ending lift, the mean of the cubic arc over the last fifth of the text minus the text's density; and sentence clustering, the between-sentence variance of density divided by its expected value if the text's words were dealt to its sentences at random, $\sigma^2(m-1)/L$ for $m$ sentences, $L$ words and word-level variance $\sigma^2$ of net charge. From HC3\cite{guo2023} I took the first human answer and the first ChatGPT answer to each question and kept the \hcN{} questions where both have at least 30 words; contractions split by the corpus's tokenisation of human answers were rejoined. From PERSUADE~2.0\cite{crossley2024} I drew 20 essays of 150 to 800 words for each of the eight assignments that need no source text (\esN{} essays, seed 0). Each essay was matched by two machine essays written to the same assignment and word count, one with no further instruction and one with the instruction to write as a student of the same grade would in a timed test. The writers were subagents of the coding assistant, one essay per agent, running Claude Fable~5.2, Opus~5.5, Sonnet~5.5 and Haiku~5.5 in rotation at low reasoning effort. Each agent received only its task; none was told of the study or its hypotheses. Two of the 320 requests produced no essay. Differences are paired by essay or question and standardised by the human standard deviation; intervals resample assignments (eight) for the essays, as planned, and questions for HC3; results that resample essays are given where they differ. The classifier is a logistic regression on the ten standardised measures, scored out of fold with all texts of an assignment or question in one fold. For the tone check, 60 plain-instruction drafts (15 per model) were each revised by the model that wrote them under four instructions: the control instruction, to revise the draft so that it reads as a student of that grade wrote it; the control instruction plus a report listing each measure on which the draft lay outside the middle 80\% of student essays on other assignments; and the control instruction plus an instruction to do the rewrite with one of two published prose skills for removing the marks of machine writing. Distance is the mean over measures of the squared standardised difference from the mean of student essays on other assignments. The model declined \rvDeclined{} of the 240 revision requests, stating that making machine text read as a student's work would misrepresent authorship; essays with all four revisions (\rvN) enter the comparison.}

\newcommand{\dialParagraphs}{Can the measures steer a revision? For \rvN{} plain machine essays, the model that wrote each draft revised it under four instructions: a control instruction, to make it read as a student of that grade wrote it; the control instruction plus a report naming each measure on which the draft lay outside the middle 80\% of student essays on other assignments; and the control instruction plus one of two published prose skills written to remove the marks of machine writing (Methods). I score a text by its mean squared standardised distance from the centre of student essays (\figref{fig:machine}c). Drafts average \rvDraft{} and control revisions \rvControl. Revisions made with a skill average \rvHumanizer{} and \rvSepia, neither distinguishable from the control (differences \dRvHC{} \dRvHCCI{} and \dRvSC{} \dRvSCCI): the skills work on wording and sentence shape, and these measures show no trace of them. Revisions that saw the report average \rvReport. Their advantage over the control is \dRvRC{} with an interval of \dRvRCCI{} when assignments are resampled, as the plan specified, and \dRvRCCIessays{} when essays are, so the planned test is undecided. The report moved what it named. It cut the mean ending lift from \rvLiftDraft{} to \rvLiftReport{} standard deviations, against \rvLiftControl{} for the control, and left intensifier use where it was (\rvBoostDraft{} to \rvBoostReport), because a draft low on intensifiers is still inside the wide human range and was not flagged.

A report of ranges is a weak instrument. It acts only on drafts outside a wide band, and if it worked perfectly it would pull every draft to one centre. A dial is stronger. Each of \dlRequested{} further drafts was assigned the measured profile of one real student essay, drawn at random from other assignments, and was revised twice by its author model with a sheet giving the current and the target value of each measure; a control arm revised the same drafts twice with the plain instruction. The dial was followed. Across the \dlN{} essays with both arms complete, achieved values correlate with assigned targets at a mean of \dlCorrDial{} over the ten measures in the dial arm and \dlCorrControl{} in the control arm (difference \dDlCorr{} \dDlCorrCI), from \dlCorrMax{} for the best-followed measure to \dlCorrMin{} for sentence clustering. The mean squared gap to the target fell from \dlGapDraft{} to \dlGapDialOne{} after one round and \dlGapDial{} after two, while the control arm stayed at \dlGapControl.

Following the dial did not make the essays more like student writing. The classifier's mean machine score is \dlScoreDraft{} for the drafts and \dlScoreHuman{} for the matched student essays; two plain revisions bring it to \dlScoreControl{} and two rounds with the dial to \dlScoreDial{} (difference \dDlScore{} \dDlScoreCI, undecided). A judge model that read both revisions of each draft without knowing which was which chose the dial revision as the better written in \dlJudge\% of \dlJudgeN{} pairs \dlJudgeCI{} and as the one that reads more like a student's own work in \dlJudgeStudent\% \dlJudgeStudentCI. Extended Data Table~\ref{tab:excerpt} shows the essay at the median improvement, chosen by rule and not by eye. To raise its positive charge the dial revision closes on \emph{a fun, wonderful summer}, and its ending lift moved away from the target.

The measures therefore give a model a working control over how its text carries feeling, as far as a target can be reached by word choice in two passes. That is narrower than writing as a person does. A plain instruction to write as a student did as much for the classifier and far more for the judge.

One more observation belongs here. These experiments ask a model to make machine text resemble student writing, and the model sometimes refused: \rvDeclined{} of 240 requests in the first revision experiment, and \dlMissing{} of \dlRequested{} essays in the dial arm were left unfinished for that reason, \dlMissingHaiku{} of them by the smallest of the four models. The refusals said that passing machine text off as a student's work misrepresents authorship. The tools released with this paper report tone against a human range for writers who disclose their use of a model, and the separations measured above are too weak for these measures to serve as a detector or to defeat one.}
