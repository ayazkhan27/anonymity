\newcommand{\machineMethods}{\paragraph{Human and machine writing}
The ten measures are: hedons, dolors, net charge and total charge per thousand words; the percentage of words that are charged; the percentage of charge that is negated, intensified, and after a contrast word; ending lift, the mean of the cubic arc over the last fifth of the text minus the text's density; and sentence clustering, the between-sentence variance of density divided by its expected value if the text's words were dealt to its sentences at random, $\sigma^2(m-1)/L$ for $m$ sentences, $L$ words and word-level variance $\sigma^2$ of net charge. From HC3\cite{guo2023} I took the first human answer and the first ChatGPT answer to each question and kept the \hcN{} questions where both have at least 30 words; contractions split by the corpus's tokenisation of human answers were rejoined. From PERSUADE~2.0\cite{crossley2024} I drew 20 essays of 150 to 800 words for each of the eight assignments that need no source text (\esN{} essays, seed 0). Each essay was matched by two machine essays written to the same assignment and word count, one with no further instruction and one with the instruction to write as a student of the same grade would in a timed test. The writers were subagents of the coding assistant, one essay per agent, running Claude Fable~5.2, Opus~5.5, Sonnet~5.5 and Haiku~5.5 in rotation at low reasoning effort. Each agent received only its task; none was told of the study or its hypotheses. Two of the 320 requests produced no essay. Differences are paired by essay or question and standardised by the human standard deviation; intervals resample assignments (eight) for the essays, as planned, and questions for HC3; results that resample essays are given where they differ. The classifier is a logistic regression on the ten standardised measures, scored out of fold with all texts of an assignment or question in one fold. For the tone check, 60 plain-instruction drafts (15 per model) were each revised by the model that wrote them under four instructions: the control instruction, to revise the draft so that it reads as a student of that grade wrote it; the control instruction plus a report listing each measure on which the draft lay outside the middle 80\% of student essays on other assignments; and the control instruction plus an instruction to do the rewrite with one of two published prose skills for removing the marks of machine writing. Distance is the mean over measures of the squared standardised difference from the mean of student essays on other assignments. The model declined \rvDeclined{} of the 240 revision requests, stating that making machine text read as a student's work would misrepresent authorship; essays with all four revisions (\rvN) enter the comparison.}
