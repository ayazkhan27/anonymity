Dear Principal,

I heard that you are thinking about making a rule that students need a B average to play sports or do other activities. I think this is a bad idea and I hope you will change your mind after reading my letter.

First of all, a lot of kids at our school have a C average. A C is still passing. If you take away sports from all of those students, half the teams wouldn't even have enough players. Our basketball team only has eleven kids on it right now, and I know at least four of them would be cut. That is not fair to them or to the kids who do have B's and still want a team to play on.

Second, sports and clubs are the reason some kids come to school at all. My cousin was not good at school, but he loved football, and he kept his grades up enough because he wanted to stay on the team. If the rule said he needed a B, he probably would have just given up, because a B felt impossible to him. When you take away the thing a student cares about, they don't always work harder. Sometimes they just stop trying.

Also, activities teach things that classes don't. In band you learn to practice every day. In sports you learn teamwork and how to lose without getting mad. Those are important for life too, not just grades.

I understand that you want students to do better in school, and that is a good goal. But instead of a B rule, maybe students with a C could go to tutoring once a week to stay on the team. That way they get help and not a punishment.

Thank you for listening to what students think.

Sincerely,
A student
