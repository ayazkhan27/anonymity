Today most people have a cell phone, and many of them use it every day. Because of this, people often ask whether drivers should be allowed to use their phones while driving. I believe drivers should not be able to use cell phones in any way while they are operating a vehicle.

The main reason is safety. When a person is driving, they need to pay full attention to the road, other cars, pedestrians, and traffic signals. Even a few seconds of looking at a screen can be enough for a crash to happen. A car going 60 miles per hour travels almost 90 feet in just one second, so looking down to read a text can be very dangerous.

Some people say that hands-free devices solve this problem. However, research has shown that talking on the phone, even without holding it, still takes the driver's mind off the road. The brain can only focus on so much at once, so the driver may miss something important.

Cell phones are useful, but driving requires full concentration. If drivers could use phones in any way, more people would get hurt or killed. For this reason, drivers should not use cell phones at all while operating a vehicle.
