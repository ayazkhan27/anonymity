Dear Principal,

I heard that you are deciding if all students should have to do community service. I think you should not make it required. I know helping the community is a good thing, but forcing kids to do it is not the right way.

My first reason is that a lot of students don't have the time. I have soccer practice three days a week and games on Saturday, and I still have homework every night. Some nights I don't finish my math until ten. My friend Marcus has to watch his little sister after school until his mom gets home from work, so he can't go anywhere. If you add hours of community service on top of that, some kids are going to fall behind in there classes or get really stressed out. School is supposed to come first.

My second reason is that it doesn't mean as much if your forced to do it. My friend Kayla tutors second graders at the elementary school and she loves it. She talks about the kids all the time and she even makes flash cards for them at home. She does a good job because she wants to be there. Now imagine a kid who hates it and is only there because he has to be. He is probably going to sit around and not really help. That isn't fair to the little kids who need a good tutor. Also when we picked up litter at the park for a class once, half the people just stood around and talked.

Some people say that requiring it will teach us responsibility. I get that, but I think we learn responsibility in other ways already, like chores and sports and taking care of family.

Instead of making it a rule, I think the school should make it easier for kids who want to help. You could have a sign up sheet in the office, or give extra credit, or have a club that does a project once a month. Then more students would probably try it and maybe even like it.

Thank you for reading my letter and I hope you think about it.

Sincerely,
A student
