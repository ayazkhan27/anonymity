Dear Principal,

I think students should be required to do community service, but not a crazy amount. Maybe like ten hours a year. Here is why I think that.

First, community service helps people who really need it. My friend Maria tutors second graders after school at Lincoln Elementary. She says one boy couldn't read at all in September and now he can read short books by himself. That is a big deal for that kid. If more students did stuff like that, a lot more kids could get help.

Second, it teaches us things we don't learn in class. When my class cleaned up litter at the park last spring, I was surprised how much trash was there. It made me think twice before I throw a wrapper on the ground.

I know some of my friends say they don't have time because of sports and homework. That's why it should only be a few hours. Students could also pick what they want to do so it's not boring.

In conclusion, I think required community service would make our school and our town better.

Sincerely,
A student
