Almost everybody I know has a cell phone, and most of them can't go ten minutes without looking at it. That is fine when you are sitting on the couch, but it is a different story when you are behind the wheel of a car. I believe drivers should not be able to use their cell phones while driving, except for a few situations like an emergency or using directions that are set up before the car moves.

The biggest reason is safety. When you are driving, you are in control of something that weighs thousands of pounds and is going fast. It only takes a couple seconds of looking down to miss a stop sign or a kid running into the street. People always think they are the exception and that they can text and drive just fine, but nobody really can. Your eyes can only be in one place at a time. My cousin got rear ended last year by a lady who admitted she was reading a message. Nobody got hurt bad, but his car was totaled and it could have been a lot worse if he had been on the highway instead of at a red light.

Another reason is that driving affects other people, not just the driver. If someone wants to take a risk that only hurts themself, that is one thing. But on the road there are other drivers, passengers, people walking and people riding bikes. They did not agree to be put in danger because someone wanted to answer a text that could have waited. A message that says "lol" is not worth somebody's life.

Some people would say that phones are needed in the car, and I do partly agree with that. A lot of us use our phones for GPS, and getting lost can be dangerous too. Also if there is an accident or your car breaks down, you need to call for help. That is why I don't think phones should be banned from cars completely. Drivers should type in the address before they start driving and let the phone talk to them. If they need to make a call, they can pull over first. Hands free calling is better than holding the phone, but even then your mind is not all the way on the road.

In conclusion, drivers should not be using their phones while the car is moving. The text, the song or the notification will still be there when you park. Laws against it might be annoying to some people, but they are there to keep everybody alive, and I think that matters more than being able to reply right away.
