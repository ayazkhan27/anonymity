Dear Principal,

I heard that you are thinking about making a new rule that students need at least a B average to play sports or do other activities. I think this is a bad idea and I am against it. I have three reasons why.

First, a lot of students at our school have a C average. A C is not failing. It means you are doing average work and passing your classes. If you take away sports from all those kids, you are basically punishing them for being normal. My friend Marcus has a C average and he works really hard in math, it is just hard for him. Basketball is the thing that makes him want to come to school every day. If he couldn't play, I honestly think he would stop trying in class at all.

Second, sports and clubs teach things that you don't always learn in a classroom. When I was on the soccer team last year, I learned how to work with other people and how to not give up when we were losing. Kids in drama club learn to speak in front of people. Those skills are important for getting a job someday, maybe even more important than getting a B in science.

Third, being in activities can actually help your grades go up. Most coaches already check grades and make kids do homework before practice. There is a study hall for the football team after school. If you kick the C students off the team, they lose all that help and structure. They will probably just go home and play video games instead of studying.

I understand that you want students to do better in school, and that is a good goal. But instead of taking away activities, maybe the school could make a rule that you need a C average, or that students with lower grades have to go to tutoring to stay on the team. That way kids still get pushed to do better but they don't lose the thing they love.

Please think about all the students this rule would hurt. Thank you for asking what we think.

Sincerely,
An 8th Grade Student
