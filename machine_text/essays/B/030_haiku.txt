Dear Principal,

I think all students should be required to do community service. I know some people think it takes up too much time, but I believe the benefits are worth it. Helping others teaches us things that we cannot learn from a textbook.

First, community service helps students understand people who are different from them. For example, my friend Maria volunteers at the elementary school on Tuesdays. She helps a second grader named Jake with his reading. At first Maria said it was boring, but now she says Jake is one of her favorite people. She learned how to be patient, and Jake learned that someone cares about him. If every student did this, our school would have more friendships across different groups.

Second, community service makes our town better. Last spring my class spent a Saturday cleaning up litter at the park near the river. We filled twelve garbage bags and found an old bicycle that was stuck in the mud. The park looked so much nicer afterward, and families were able to have picnics there again. It felt good to see the results of our hard work right away.

I understand that some students have busy schedules. They have sports practice, jobs, music lessons, and homework. However, community service does not have to take a huge amount of time. Students could volunteer for just two hours a month, and the school could help find opportunities that fit around sports seasons and other activities. Also, many students say they feel less stressed after helping others, so it might even make them feel better.

Finally, community service teaches responsibility. When we show up on time and keep our promises to people who depend on us, we learn that our actions matter. These are skills we will need for the rest of our lives, whether we become doctors, teachers, or store owners.

For these reasons, I believe students should be required to perform community service. It helps the people around us, it makes our community cleaner and kinder, and it teaches us to care about more than ourselves. I hope you will consider this idea.

Thank you for reading my letter.

Sincerely,

A Concerned Student
