Dear Principal,

I think Policy 1 is the better choice for our school. Students should be allowed to bring their phones and use them at lunch and free times, as long as they are turned off in class.

First, phones are important for safety. If there is an emergency, like a lockdown or someone gets hurt, students need a way to contact their parents. Also, a lot of kids have parents who work late, so they need to text them about rides after school. If phones are banned, nobody can do that.

Second, using phones at lunch gives students a break. School can be stressful, and checking messages or listening to music for a few minutes helps us relax. Then we can come back to class ready to focus.

Third, Policy 1 teaches responsibility. When we get older, we will have jobs where we have to put our phones away during work. Learning to turn them off during class is good practice for that.

Some people might say phones will distract students, but if they are off during class, that problem is solved. Teachers could take phones away from students who break the rule.

For these reasons, I hope you will choose Policy 1.

Sincerely,
A Student
