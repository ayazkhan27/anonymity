Dear Principal,

I am writing to tell you that I disagree with the idea of requiring a B average to play sports or do other activities. I think this rule would hurt a lot of students more than it would help them.

First of all, many students have a C average, and they are not bad students. Some of them work really hard but a subject like math is just hard for them. If they cant play sports because of one or two grades, they might feel like giving up. Sports and clubs are sometimes the only reason kids want to come to school at all.

Also, activities teach us things that classes dont. On a team you learn to work together, to be on time, and to keep trying when you lose. Those are skills we will need when we grow up. Taking that away would not make us better students.

Some people say the rule would push kids to study more. I think a better idea is to offer free tutoring or a study hall for students who are struggling. That way they can get help and still stay on the team.

Thank you for listening to our opinions. I hope you will think about this before you change the policy.

Sincerely,
A concerned student
