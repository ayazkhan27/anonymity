Dear Principal,

I am writing to tell you my opinion about the possible new policy that would make students have at least a B average to play sports or do other activities. I think this is a bad idea and I disagree with it.

First of all, many students have a C average, and a C is not a bad grade. It means the student is passing and doing okay. If you take away sports from all of those kids, a lot of them will lose something they really care about. Some students only come to school excited because of soccer or band or drama club. If they cant do those things, they might stop trying at all.

Second, activities actually help students with school. When I am on a team I have to manage my time, so I do my homework right after school instead of waiting. Sports also teach you to work with other people and to never give up. Those skills help in classes too. A kid who is on the basketball team might get better grades because he wants to stay on the team, but only if he gets the chance to join in the first place.

Also, some students are not good at tests but are really talented at something else. Maybe a student struggles in math but is the best runner on the track team. That student should still get to show what they can do. Not everybody is good at the same things, and activities give those students a way to feel proud of themselves.

Instead of taking away activities, I think the school should offer tutoring or a study hall for students who need help. Then they could raise their grades and still be part of the team. Maybe a C average could be the minimum, so students still have a reason to work hard.

Thank you for listening to my opinion. I hope you will think about it before you decide.

Sincerely,
A concerned student
