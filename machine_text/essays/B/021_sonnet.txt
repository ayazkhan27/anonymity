Dear Principal Johnson,

I am writing to tell you my opinion about requiring all students to do community service. I think that students should not be forced to do it, but the school should encourage it in other ways.

First of all, some of my friends already do community service and they love it. My friend Maria tutors third graders after school, and she says it makes her feel proud when a kid finally learns to read a hard word. Another friend, Jake, picks up litter at the park every month with his soccer team. They do these things because they want to, and that is why they work so hard at it. If the school makes it a rule, some kids will just do the bare minimum so they can check it off a list. That is not really helping anybody.

Also, many students are already very busy. I have a lot of homework every night, and I also have a younger brother I watch while my mom is at work. Some kids have jobs or play sports or take care of family members. If they had to add a certain number of service hours, they might get stressed out and their grades could go down. One of my friends already stays up until eleven finishing assignments, and adding more required hours would be too much for him.

However, I do think community service is a good thing, so I have a suggestion. Instead of making it a requirement, the school could set up a volunteer club and give students a certificate or a mention in the announcements when they take part. The school could also plan a few service days where everyone can join in if they want. That way kids who have time can help out, and kids who are too busy will not get in trouble.

In conclusion, helping the community is important, but it means more when people choose it themselves. I hope you will think about my ideas before you make your decision.

Sincerely,
A student at your school
