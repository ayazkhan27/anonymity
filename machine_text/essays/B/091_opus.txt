I agree with my principal that every student should have to do at least one extracurricular activity. I think it would help kids make friends, learn new skills, and stay out of trouble.

First, activities help you make friends. When I joined the soccer team in sixth grade I didn't really know anybody because I had just moved here. After a few weeks of practice I had like five new friends and we still hang out at lunch. If I didn't join I probably would of just sat by myself.

Second, you learn things you can't learn in class. My cousin works on the yearbook and she learned how to use a real camera and design pages on the computer. She says she wants to be a graphic designer now. Student council teaches you how to talk in front of people and be a leader, which is important for jobs later.

Some people might say kids are too busy with homework. But there are lots of choices, and some clubs only meet once a week, so it's not that hard.

Finally, activities keep kids busy after school instead of just playing video games all day. That is why I agree with the principal's decision.
