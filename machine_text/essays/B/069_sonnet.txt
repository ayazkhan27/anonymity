Dear Principal,

I am writing to tell you that I disagree with requiring a grade B average to play sports or join activities. I think this policy would hurt more students than it helps.

First, many students have a C average, and sports and clubs are often the reason they come to school and try. A kid who loves basketball might work harder in class just to stay on the team. If you take the team away, that kid might stop caring about school at all. Activities give students a reason to be here.

Second, activities teach things that grades do not. Sports teach teamwork and discipline, and clubs teach leadership. A student with a C can still learn a lot from these things. Also, being busy after school keeps students away from trouble.

Some people say the policy would make students study more. But a better idea would be to ask students with low grades to go to tutoring. That way they get help and still get to participate.

For these reasons, I hope you keep the rule at a C average.

Sincerely,
A concerned student
