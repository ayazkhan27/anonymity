Dear Principal,

I heard that you are thinking about making a rule that students need a B average to play sports or do other activities. I am against this idea and I think a lot of other students are too. I hope you will read my reasons before you decide.

First of all, many students at our school have a C average. A C is still passing. If you take away sports from all of those kids, the teams would lose half of there players. Our basketball team might not even have enough people to play a game. That is not fair to the students who do have good grades and still want a team to be on.

Second, sports and clubs are the reason some kids come to school at all. My cousin was not a good student, but he tried harder in math because he wanted to stay on the football team. If the rule was a B he probably would of just gave up, because a B felt impossible for him. A goal has to be something you can reach. If kids give up they might start hanging out after school with nothing to do, and that is when people get into trouble.

Also, activities teach us things that classes don't. You learn teamwork, being on time, and how to lose without getting mad. Band and drama club help shy kids make friends. Those things matter in life just as much as a grade on a report card.

I understand that you want us to care about our grades, and I agree school comes first. But I think a better rule would be keeping the C average and giving extra tutoring to players who are struggling. That way students get help instead of getting punished.

Thank you for listening to what students think.

Sincerely,
A concerned student
