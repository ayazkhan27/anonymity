Dear Principal,

I heard that you are thinking about changing the rule so students need a B average to play sports or do other activities. I think this is a bad idea and I hope you will change your mind after reading my letter.

First of all, a lot of students have a C average. A C is still passing. If you make the rule a B, then half the basketball team would probably be gone and we wouldn't even have enough players to have a team. The same thing would happen to band and drama club. It's not fair to punish kids who are passing all there classes.

Second, sports are the reason some kids even try in school. My cousin plays football and he told me that he only does his homework because the coach checks his grades. If he got kicked off the team for having a C, he would probably just stop caring. Then his grades would go down even more, not up. So the rule could actually make things worse.

Another reason is that some kids work really hard and still get C's. Not everybody is good at math or reading. I have a friend who studies every night and she still gets a C in science. She is also the best runner on the track team. Why should she lose the one thing she is good at? That would make her feel terrible about herself.

Also, activities keep kids out of trouble. If students can't go to practice after school, they are going to go home and play video games or hang out with nothing to do. Sports teach you teamwork and how to be on time, and those things matter too.

I understand that you want us to get better grades, and that is a good goal. But maybe instead you could have a study hall before practice for kids who have C's. That way they get help and still get to play.

Thank you for listening to what students think.

Sincerely,
A concerned student
