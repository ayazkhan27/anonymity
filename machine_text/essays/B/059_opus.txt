I think students would benefit from being able to attend classes from home, but only if it is an option and not a requirement. Distance learning gives students flexibility that a regular school day can't, and for some students that makes the difference between keeping up and falling behind.

The first reason is that not every student can make it to school every day. Some students have long-term illnesses or injuries. When my cousin broke her leg sophomore year, she missed almost three weeks and spent the rest of the semester trying to catch up. If she could have logged into her classes by video, she would have heard the same lectures as everyone else and not been so stressed. Other students have jobs or have to watch younger siblings, and being able to learn from home would help them stay in school.

Second, some students just learn better in a quieter place. A normal classroom can be loud and full of distractions, like people talking or phones going off. At home, a student who gets anxious or distracted could focus more on the actual work. They could also rewatch recorded lessons if they didn't understand something the first time.

Some people argue that students at home won't pay attention or will miss out on making friends. That is a fair point, and it is why distance learning should be a choice and not forced on everyone. Students who need the social side of school can still come in.

Overall, giving students the option to learn from home would help more of them succeed. Schools should offer it.
