Dear Principal,

I am writing to share my views on the possible new policy that would require students to have at least a grade B average in order to take part in sports or other activities. I think this policy would be a mistake. Students should be allowed to play sports and join clubs even if their grades are not perfect, because these activities are important parts of school and they can actually help students do better in their classes.

My first reason is that sports and activities keep students motivated. Many students come to school because of their team or their club. If a student knows that they can not play basketball or be in the band unless they get a B average, they might feel like giving up. A student who has a C average might already be struggling, and taking away the thing they enjoy most could make them lose interest in school altogether. When students have something to look forward to, they are usually more willing to try hard in their other subjects.

My second reason is that activities teach skills that are not found in a textbook. On a team, students learn how to work with others, how to handle losing, and how to keep going when things are hard. Students in the drama club learn how to speak in front of people, and students in the robotics club learn how to solve problems together. These skills help students in every part of their lives, including their future jobs. If we take these activities away from students who need them, we are taking away a chance for them to grow.

My third reason is that a grade C student is not necessarily a student who does not care. Some students have a hard time with certain subjects, like math or reading, but they may be very good at other things. Some students also have problems at home or are dealing with stress that makes it hard to focus on homework. If these students are kicked off their teams, they will have even more problems. It would be better for the school to offer extra help, like tutoring or a study hall, so that students can improve their grades and still play.

Some people might say that this policy would make students work harder in class because they would want to keep playing. I understand that point, but I do not think it would work like that for everyone. Some students would just stop trying at all if they were told they could not play. A better idea would be to make sure students have a study time before or after practice, and to have coaches check on grades every few weeks. That way, students who are falling behind get help early, and nobody is removed from the team all at once.

I also think about the students who are already on the team and have been working hard. A rule like this could make the team smaller, and sometimes the school needs every player it can get. Also, many activities are a big part of school spirit. When students can not participate, the school feels less like a community and more like a place where people only go to take tests.

In conclusion, I believe that students should not need a grade B average to play sports or join activities. Instead, the school should focus on helping students who are struggling with their grades. Sports and activities are a reason many students want to come to school, and they teach valuable lessons. I hope you will think about my views and consider other ways to help students succeed.

Thank you for listening to the students' opinions. I hope we can work together to make our school the best it can be.

Sincerely,
A Concerned Student
