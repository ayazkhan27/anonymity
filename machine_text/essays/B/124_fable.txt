Have you ever asked just one person for advice and then found out later it was bad advice? I have, and it was not fun. When people need to make a decision, they sometimes ask more then one person what they think. Seeking multiple opinions can help someone make a better choice because different people know different things, it helps you see problems you didn't think about, and it makes you feel more sure about what you pick.

First, different people know different things. Nobody knows everything, not even adults. For example, last year I wanted to try out for the basketball team but I also wanted to stay in band. I asked my older cousin first, and he said to do basketball because he played in high school and loved it. If I stopped there I would of just quit band. But then I asked my band teacher and she told me that some of the practices don't even overlap and I could do both if I talked to the coach. My cousin didn't know that because he doesn't go to my school. Each person only had part of the answer, and I needed both parts to figure it out.

Second, asking more people helps you see problems you didn't think about. When you only have one opinion, you only hear one side. My mom was trying to decide what kind of car to get, and the guy at the dealership told her this one car was perfect for us. Of course he said that, he wants to sell it. Then she asked my uncle who works on cars, and he said that kind breaks down a lot after a few years and the parts cost a lot. She also asked our neighbor who has the same car and the neighbor said it was to small for trips. If my mom only listened to the salesman, we would be stuck with a car that didn't work for our family. Some people have a reason to tell you a certain thing, so hearing from others helps you notice that.

Lastly, getting multiple opinions makes you feel more confident. If three or four people all say the same thing, it is probably right. When I was picking my science fair project, I asked my dad, my friend Marcus, and my teacher if doing a project about which paper towel holds the most water was a good idea. All three said it was kind of boring and lots of kids do it. That was annoying to hear, but since they all agreed I knew it wasn't just one person being picky. I changed it to testing which liquids make plants grow faster and I got second place. On the other hand, if people disagree, that tells you something to. It means the choice is hard and you need to think more about it yourself.

Some people might say asking too many people is confusing. That can be true, but you don't have to do what everybody says. You still make the final choice. You are just getting more information first.

In conclusion, talking to more than one person is a smart thing to do when you need advice. People know different things, they can point out problems, and they help you feel sure. Next time you have a big decision, don't just ask one person. Ask a few, and then decide for yourself.
