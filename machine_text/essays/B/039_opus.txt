Dear Principal Harris,

I heard that you are thinking about making community service a requirement for every student at our school. I think this is a good idea, and I think you should do it. Community service helps the people who get helped, but it also helps the students who do it.

First of all, community service makes our town a better place. Last spring my friend Marcus and I went with my church to clean up the park by the library. There were chip bags, soda cans, and even an old tire in the creek. It took us four hours but when we were done the park looked so much better and little kids could play there without stepping on broken glass. If every student at our school did something like that, think about how clean our whole town could be.

Second, community service teaches students things they can't learn in a classroom. My cousin tutors second graders at Lincoln Elementary every Thursday. She told me that at first she was nervous and didn't think she could teach anybody anything. Now she says she is more patient and better at explaining stuff, and she even wants to be a teacher when she grows up. She never would of found that out if she didn't try tutoring.

Some of my friends say community service takes too much time. They have sports, homework, and some of them have to watch their little brothers and sisters after school. I understand that, because I play soccer and I know how busy it gets. But the school could make it fair by only requiring a few hours each semester, like ten hours. Students could also pick something that fits their schedule, like helping on a Saturday or during summer break. Ten hours is not that much when you think about how much time we spend on our phones.

Also, a lot of students would never try community service if no one made them. But once they try it they might actually like it. That is what happened to me. I only went to the park cleanup because my mom made me go, and now I want to do it again.

In conclusion, I think you should require community service for all students. It helps our town, it teaches us important lessons, and it doesn't have to take up too much time. Please think about it.

Sincerely,
Jordan Reyes
