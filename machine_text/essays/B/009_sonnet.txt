Dear Principal,

I think Policy 1 is the better choice. Students should be allowed to bring phones to school and use them at lunch and during free time, as long as the phones are off during class.

First of all, phones are important for safety. If something happens after school, like a bus being late or a change in plans, a student can call their parents right away. If phones are banned completely, kids would have no way to reach their families in an emergency. Parents would probably worry a lot more too.

Second, letting students use phones at lunch gives them a way to relax. School is stressful, and a few minutes to text a friend or listen to music can help us feel calmer before the next class. When we are in a better mood, we pay more attention in class.

Some people say phones cause cheating and distractions. That is a fair point, but Policy 1 already deals with it because phones must be turned off during class. If a student breaks the rule, the teacher can take the phone for the day. That is a fair punishment and it teaches responsibility.

Finally, banning phones would be really hard to enforce. Lots of students would sneak them in anyway, and teachers would waste time hunting for them instead of teaching.

For these reasons, I hope you choose Policy 1.

Sincerely,
A concerned student
