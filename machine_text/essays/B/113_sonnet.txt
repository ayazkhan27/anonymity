Drivers should not be able to use cell phones in any capacity while operating a vehicle. Even though almost everyone owns a phone today, driving is a job that needs a person's full attention, and a phone takes that attention away.

The biggest reason is safety. When a driver looks down to read a text, their eyes leave the road for about five seconds. At highway speed, that is long enough to travel the length of a football field with no one watching where the car is going. Many crashes happen because a driver did not see brake lights, a stop sign, or a person crossing the street. These accidents hurt not only the driver but also passengers, other drivers, and pedestrians who did nothing wrong.

Some people argue that hands-free calling is safe because the driver can keep both hands on the wheel. However, studies show that the problem is not just the hands. A phone conversation distracts the mind. A driver who is deep in a conversation may look at the road but not really notice what is on it. Even when your eyes are open, your brain can still be somewhere else. Because of this, a ban on only handheld phones does not fully solve the problem.

Others say that phones are needed for emergencies or for GPS directions. These are fair points, but they have easy solutions. A driver can set up directions before leaving, and if there is a real emergency, the driver can pull over to a safe place and make the call. Waiting a few minutes to answer a message rarely causes any harm, but a crash can change lives forever.

Finally, laws need to be simple to follow and to enforce. If the rule is that phones cannot be used at all while driving, then everyone knows what is expected. Police do not have to guess whether someone was really using a hands-free device. Clear rules also help people build good habits, like putting the phone in the glove box or turning on a do not disturb mode.

In conclusion, drivers should not use cell phones while driving. No text, call, or app is more important than a human life. Putting the phone down is a small sacrifice that can keep everyone on the road safe.
