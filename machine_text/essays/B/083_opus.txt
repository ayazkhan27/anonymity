I agree with my principal's decision that all students should have to do at least one extracurricular activity. Some people might think it is unfair to make kids do something after school, but I think it would help students in a lot of ways. Extracurricular activities help you make friends, learn new skills, and stay out of trouble.

First, doing an activity is a great way to make friends. When I started middle school I didn't really know anybody because most of my friends from elementary school went to a different school. Then I joined the soccer team and I met a lot of people. We practiced together every day and now some of them are my best friends. If I never joined I would probably still be eating lunch by myself. A lot of kids are shy and they won't join anything unless they have to, so this rule would help them.

Second, extracurricular activities teach you skills that you don't learn in class. For example, if you work on the yearbook you learn how to take good pictures and use computer programs to design pages. If you are on student council you learn how to speak in front of people and plan events like the school dance. These are things you can use when you get older and get a job. My cousin was on the debate team in high school and she says it helped her get into college because colleges like to see that you did stuff besides homework.

Finally, activities keep students busy and out of trouble. When kids go home after school with nothing to do they usually just play video games or watch TV for hours. Some kids might even get into bad situations because they are bored. If they have practice or a club meeting they are doing something good with their time instead.

Some people might say that students need time for homework or that they have jobs or have to watch their little brothers and sisters. That is true, but there are lots of activities that only meet once a week, so almost everybody could find something that works for them.

In conclusion, I think the principal made a good choice. Extracurricular activities help students make friends, learn skills, and stay busy. Every student should give it a try.
