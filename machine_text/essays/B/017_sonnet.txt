Dear Principal,

I am writing to you about the cell phone policy. I think Policy 1 is the better choice, which lets students have their phones at lunch and free times as long as they are turned off in class. I have a few reasons why I think this.

First of all, a lot of students need their phones for important things. For example, some kids have to call their parents after school to tell them about a ride or a change in plans. Some students take care of their little brothers and sisters, so their parents need to be able to reach them. If phones were not allowed at all, those students would have to wait in the office to use the phone, and that wastes time. Also, in an emergency, students would be able to contact their families right away.

Another reason is that Policy 1 teaches responsibility. If we are trusted to use our phones at the right times, we learn how to manage them. Middle school is a good time to learn this because in high school and in real life nobody is going to take your phone away all the time. When students follow the rules, they show they can be mature. If a student does use their phone in class, there can be a consequence like having it taken until the end of the day.

Some people might say that phones cause distractions and cheating. I understand that, and that is why the phones must be off during class. This takes care of the problem, because during lunch and free time there is nothing to cheat on or be distracted from. Banning phones completely would be unfair to the students who follow the rules just because a few kids do not.

Finally, lunch is a time for kids to relax. Listening to music or texting a friend can help a student calm down and feel better before the next class. A relaxed student pays more attention.

For these reasons, I hope you choose Policy 1. I think it is fair and it helps students and teachers both.

Sincerely,
A concerned student
