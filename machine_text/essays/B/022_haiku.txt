Dear Principal,

I think students should be required to do community service. Some of my friends think it takes too much time away from things they want to do, but I believe helping our community is worth it, even when it is hard to fit in.

Community service helps people who need it. For example, my friend Maya tutors second graders every Tuesday after school. One of her students could not read at the start of the year, but now he reads short books by himself. Without Maya's time, he might still be struggling. Other students clean up litter at the park near our school. The park is cleaner now, and little kids can play there safely.

Community service also teaches important skills. When students work with younger kids or with adults in our town, they learn how to be patient, how to listen, and how to work as a team. These skills help us in class and later in life.

Some students say they are too busy with sports, music, and homework. I understand that. But school is about more than grades. If every student gives just a few hours a month, the whole town benefits, and we all learn something about caring for others.

For these reasons, I believe the school should require all students to complete community service. It will make our community better and help us grow into responsible citizens.

Sincerely,
A Student
