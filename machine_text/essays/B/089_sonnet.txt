I agree with the principal's decision that every student should take part in at least one extracurricular activity. Some students might think it is just extra work, but I think it would make our school better and help students in a lot of ways. Activities help students make friends, learn new skills, and do better in school.

First of all, extracurricular activities help students make friends. When you go to school, you mostly talk to the people in your classes or the kids you already know. But when you join a club or a team, you meet people who like the same things you do. For example, my cousin was really shy when he started middle school and did not talk to anyone at lunch. Then he joined the yearbook staff. He had to work with other students to take pictures and plan the pages, and by the end of the year he had a whole group of friends. If every student had to join something, a lot more kids would have a chance to feel like they belong.

Second, activities teach students skills that they can't really learn in a classroom. Playing on a sports team teaches you about teamwork and how to keep going even when you lose. Being on the student council teaches you how to speak in front of people and how to work together to make decisions. Working on the yearbook teaches you how to meet deadlines and how to be responsible. These skills are important because we will need them when we get jobs when we are older. A teacher can tell us about teamwork, but it is different when we actually have to do it.

Another reason I agree is that activities can help students do better in their schoolwork. Some people say that students who do activities will fall behind because they have less time for homework. But I think it is actually the opposite. When students are busy, they learn to manage their time better. I know that on days when I have practice after school, I get my homework done right away because I know I do not have a lot of time. Also, a lot of schools have rules that students have to keep their grades up to stay on a team or in a club, so students try harder in class.

Some people might argue that it is not fair to make students join an activity. They say that some students have to go home and take care of their little brothers and sisters or have a job. That is a good point, and I think the principal should make some rules to be fair about it. For example, the school could have activities that meet during lunch or during a free period so that students who cannot stay after school can still take part. There could also be many different choices, like art club, chess club, or the school newspaper, so that students who are not good at sports can still find something they like. If the school offers enough choices, every student can find one that fits them.

In conclusion, I agree that all students should have to participate in at least one extracurricular activity. It helps students make friends, learn important skills, and even do better in their classes. As long as the school gives students lots of choices and makes it work for people who are busy, I think this rule would make our school a better and more fun place for everyone. I hope the other students and teachers will support the principal's decision.
