I agree with my principal that every student should have to do at least one extracurricular activity. Some people might think it is unfair to make students do something after school, but I think it would help students make friends, learn new skills, and stay out of trouble.

First of all, extracurricular activities help students make friends. When I started middle school I didn't know very many people because most of my friends from elementary school went to a different school. Then I joined the soccer team and I met a lot of kids from other grades. Now some of them are my best friends. If I never joined soccer I would probably still be eating lunch by myself. Activities like yearbook or drama club put you with people who like the same things as you, so it is easier to talk to them.

Second, students can learn skills they won't learn in a regular class. For example, if you are on the student council you learn how to speak in front of people and how to plan events like the school dance. If you work on the yearbook you learn how to take good pictures and use design programs on the computer. These are things that can help you get a job someday. My cousin said she got her first job at a summer camp because she was captain of her volleyball team and that showed she was responsible.

Third, being in an activity keeps kids busy and out of trouble. A lot of students just go home after school and play video games or sit on their phones for hours. Some kids even get into trouble because they are bored and have nothing to do. If they had practice or a club meeting, they would be doing something good instead. It also teaches you time management because you have to finish your homework and still go to practice.

Some people might say that some students have jobs or have to babysit their little brothers and sisters after school. That is true, but there are lots of activities that meet during lunch or only once a week, so almost anybody could find something that works for them.

In conclusion, I think making every student join one extracurricular activity is a good decision. It helps students make friends, learn useful skills, and stay out of trouble. Even if some kids don't want to at first, I think they will be glad they did it.
