Dear Principal,

I am writing to you about the new policy you are thinking about, where students need a B average to play sports or do other activities. I think this is a bad idea and I am against it.

First of all, a lot of students at our school have a C average. A C is still passing. If you make the rule a B, then a lot of kids will not be able to play on the basketball team or be in band or drama club. That is not fair because they are not failing, they are just average students. Some of the teams might not even have enough players anymore.

Second, sports and activities are what keep some kids coming to school. My friend has a hard time in math and science, but he loves soccer. Soccer is the reason he does his homework and shows up every day, because the coach checks on his grades. If you take soccer away from him, he might just stop caring about school at all. Then his grades would go down even more.

Third, activities teach things that classes do not teach. You learn teamwork, how to be responsible, and how to work hard even when you are tired. These are important things for life and for getting a job someday.

I understand that you want students to do good in school. Maybe instead of a B average, you could keep it at a C, or have tutoring for kids who are struggling so they can stay on the team and get help at the same time.

Please think about all the students this rule would hurt. Thank you for listening to our opinions.

Sincerely,
A concerned 8th grader
