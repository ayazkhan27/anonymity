Dear Principal,

I heard that you are thinking about making all students do community service. I think this is a bad idea and I don't think it should be required. Community service is a good thing, but it should be a choice and not something you are forced to do.

My first reason is that students are already really busy. I have homework almost every night, and I also play soccer three days a week. By the time I get home and eat dinner it is already like seven o'clock. Some of my friends have to babysit their little brothers and sisters after school because their parents work late. If you add community service on top of all that, when are we supposed to sleep or study for tests? Our grades could go down, and I don't think that is what the school wants.

My second reason is that if you force people to help, they won't really care about it. My friend Marcus tutors second graders at the elementary school and he loves it. He talks about the kids all the time. But he chose to do that. If you made somebody who doesn't like little kids go tutor them, they would probably just sit there and look at the clock. That doesn't help the kids at all. Same thing with picking up litter. A person who is mad about being there is going to do a lazy job.

Also, not everybody has a way to get there. Some students don't have a ride on weekends, and the bus doesn't go everywhere. It wouldn't be fair to punish them for something that is not their fault.

Some people might say that requiring it teaches responsibility. I understand that, but I think we learn responsibility in other ways too, like doing chores and finishing our school work. And helping people means more when it comes from you wanting to do it.

Instead of requiring it, maybe the school could give extra credit or a certificate to students who volunteer. That way the people who want to help get rewarded and more kids might try it, but nobody is forced.

Thank you for reading my letter. I hope you think about my reasons before you decide.

Sincerely,
A student
