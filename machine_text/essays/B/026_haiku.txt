Dear Principal,

I think students should be required to do community service. Helping others is important, and school is the best place to teach it. Some of my friends think community service takes too much time, but I think the benefits are worth it.

When students tutor elementary school kids, they help those kids learn to read and do math. The younger students get extra attention, and the tutors learn patience and how to explain things clearly. When students pick up litter in our park or along the street, our town looks cleaner and safer. Everyone who uses those places benefits.

I know that students have busy lives. They have homework, sports, music lessons, and jobs. However, community service does not have to take up every weekend. We could schedule it once a month or during a few class periods each semester. That way, it fits into our schedules without taking over.

Also, if everyone has to do community service, more students will find out how good it feels to help. Some students might even find a hobby or career they enjoy. Many people who volunteer when they are young keep doing it as adults.

For these reasons, I believe community service should be required for all students. It helps our school, our neighbors, and ourselves. Thank you for considering my opinion.

Sincerely,
A Student
