Dear Principal,

I heard that you are thinking about changing the cell phone policy at our school. I think you should pick Policy 1, where students can bring phones and use them at lunch and free time but they have to be off during class. I think this is the better choice for a few reasons.

First of all, phones are important for safety. A lot of kids at our school walk home or take the bus, and there parents want to know where they are. Last year my bus broke down and we were stuck for almost an hour. I was able to text my mom so she wouldn't freak out. If we had Policy 2, nobody on that bus could of told their parents anything. Also some kids have practice after school and need to call for a ride when it ends early or gets cancelled.

Second, letting us use phones at lunch is not hurting anybody. Lunch is our time to relax. Some kids like to listen to music or play a game or show there friends a funny video. It doesn't get in the way of learning because we are not in class. Actually I think it would help, because if students know they can check their phone at lunch they won't try to sneak it during math.

That brings me to my last reason. If you ban phones completely, kids are still going to bring them. They will just hide them in their backpacks and hoodies. Then teachers have to waste time being phone police instead of teaching. With Policy 1 the rule is fair, so more students will actually follow it. And if somebody has their phone out in class, the teacher can take it away, and that's on them.

Some people might say phones are a distraction. That is true, but Policy 1 already fixes that since phones have to be turned off in class.

In conclusion, I hope you choose Policy 1. It keeps students safe, it gives us a little freedom, and it's a rule that students will respect. Thank you for reading my letter.

Sincerely,
An 8th grade student
