Dear Principal,

I think Policy 1 is the better choice for our school. Students should be allowed to bring their phones and use them at lunch and free times, as long as they are turned off in class.

First, phones are important for safety. If there is an emergency, like a lockdown or someone gets hurt, students need a way to contact their parents. Also a lot of kids have parents who work late, so they need to text them about rides after school or practice.

Second, letting us use phones at lunch is fair. Lunch is our free time, and adults get to use their phones on their breaks. If we can check messages at lunch, we wont be as tempted to sneak our phones out during class.

Some people might say phones are a distraction. But with Policy 1 phones are off during class, so they cant distract anyone from learning. Teachers can still take a phone away if someone breaks the rule.

In conclusion, Policy 1 keeps students safe and teaches us to be responsible. Please choose Policy 1.

Sincerely,
A Student
