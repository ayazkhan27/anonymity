Dear Principal,

I think students should be required to do community service, but only a small amount, like ten hours a year. I know some kids say it takes too much time, but I think the good parts are bigger than the bad parts.

First, community service helps people who really need it. My friend Maria tutors second graders at Lincoln Elementary after school. She told me one boy couldn't read a whole sentence in September, and now he reads short chapter books. That boy is doing better because a middle schooler gave up one hour a week. If every student at our school did something like that, think how many kids could be helped.

Second, it makes our town look better. Last spring my class picked up trash at the park by the creek. We filled like twenty bags with chip bags, bottles, and even an old tire. People walking their dogs said thank you to us. It felt good, and now I don't throw stuff on the ground because I know someone has to pick it up.

Some of my friends say they don't have time because of sports, homework, and chores. That is true, I have soccer three days a week. But ten hours a year is not that much. That is less than one hour a month. Also the school could let students pick what they want to do, so it doesn't feel like a punishment.

In conclusion, I think you should make community service required because it helps kids, it helps our town, and it teaches us to care about other people. Thank you for reading my letter.

Sincerely,
A Grade 8 Student
