Dear Principal,

I think you should pick Policy 1 and let students bring their phones to school as long as they keep them off during class. I know some teachers think phones are just a distraction, but I believe Policy 1 is better for students, parents, and even the school.

My first reason is safety. A lot of kids walk home or take the bus, and sometimes plans change. Last month my mom had to work late and she texted me at lunch to tell me to go to my aunt's house instead. If I didn't have my phone I would of walked home to an empty house and nobody would know where I was. Parents feel better knowing they can reach their kids. Also if there was ever an emergency at school, students could call for help or let their families know they are okay.

My second reason is that lunch and free time belong to students. We work hard in class all day and we need a break. During lunch some kids like to listen to music, check the weather, or text a friend who goes to a different school. That is not hurting anybody. As long as the phones are off in class, they won't distract anyone from learning.

My third reason is that Policy 1 teaches responsibility. When we get to high school and then get jobs, nobody is going to take our phones away. We have to learn when it is okay to use them and when it is not. If the school just bans phones completely, we never get to practice that. Policy 1 gives us a chance to show we can follow the rules.

Some people might say kids will break the rule and use phones in class anyway. That might happen sometimes, but teachers can take the phone away if a student breaks the rule. That is fair, and most students will follow the rules so they don't lose their phone.

In conclusion, Policy 1 keeps students safe, gives us a real break, and teaches us to be responsible. Please choose Policy 1.

Sincerely,
A Grade 8 Student
