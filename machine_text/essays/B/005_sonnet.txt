Dear Principal,

I think Policy 1 is the better choice for our school. Students should be allowed to bring phones and use them at lunch and free times, as long as they are turned off during class.

First of all, phones are important for safety. If something happens at school or on the way home, a student can call a parent right away. Many kids have parents who work late, and a quick text to say "I'm done with practice" helps a lot. If phones were banned completely, students would have to wait in line at the office just to make one call.

Second, a ban would be really hard to enforce. Teachers would spend their time looking for phones in backpacks instead of teaching. Kids would just hide them and sneak them out, which would cause more trouble and more detentions. Policy 1 gives students a fair rule they can actually follow.

Also, using phones at lunch lets students relax. After hours of work, a break to listen to music or check messages helps us focus better in the afternoon. It also teaches us responsibility, because we have to learn when it is okay to use our phones and when it is not.

For these reasons, I hope you choose Policy 1. Thank you for listening to student opinions.

Sincerely,
A concerned student
