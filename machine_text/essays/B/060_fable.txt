Dear Principal,

I am writing to tell you that I do not think students should need a B average to play sports or do other activities. I think a C average is good enough.

First of all, a C is a passing grade. If a student is passing all there classes, they shouldn't get punished for it. Some kids try really hard and still only get C's because school is harder for them. My friend studies every night for math and he still gets C's.

Second, sports actually help kids do better. When I was on the basketball team I had to keep my grades up or coach would bench me, so I did my homework more. If you take sports away, alot of kids won't have a reason to care about school at all. They might just go home and play video games or get in trouble.

Also, our teams would lose half the players and that isn't fair to the ones who are left.

Please keep the rule the way it is.

Sincerely,
A student
