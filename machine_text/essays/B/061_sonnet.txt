Dear Principal,

I am writing to tell you that I disagree with the idea of requiring a grade B average to play sports or join other activities. I think this policy would hurt many students more than it would help them.

First of all, many students have a C average, and a C is a passing grade. These students are not failing. If they work hard and still get Cs, it is not fair to take away the things they like. For some kids, sports or clubs are the only reason they even want to come to school. If we take that away, they might stop trying at all, and their grades could get even worse.

Second, activities teach students things that you cannot learn from a textbook. On a team, you learn how to work with others, how to be on time, and how to keep going when something is hard. Clubs like band or drama help students find what they are good at. These skills matter just as much as grades, and a student with a C average can still be a great leader.

Also, some students need extra time to learn. A student might be great at math but struggle with writing, so their average stays at a C. That does not mean they are lazy. Punishing them with this rule is not right.

Instead of banning students, the school could offer free tutoring after school or study time before practice. That way students with lower grades would get help and still get to take part. I think this would raise grades much better than taking activities away.

For these reasons, I hope you will not change the policy to require a B average. Thank you for listening to the students' views.

Sincerely,
A concerned student
