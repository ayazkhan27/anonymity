Dear Principal,

I am writing to you about the new cell phone policy you are thinking about. I think Policy 1 is the better choice. Students should be allowed to bring their phones to school and use them at lunch and during free time, as long as they are turned off during class. I have three reasons why I believe this.

My first reason is safety. A lot of parents want their kids to have a phone in case something happens. For example, if there is an emergency at school or if a student misses the bus, they need a way to call home. My mom always tells me to text her when I get to school and when I leave. If phones were not allowed at all, she would worry a lot more. Also, some students walk home alone, and having a phone makes them feel safer.

My second reason is that phones help students stay organized. Many of us use our phones to check our grades online, look at homework assignments, and set reminders. During lunch I sometimes check if I turned in all my work. Without my phone I would probably forget things more often. Phones are not just for games, they can be really useful tools too.

My third reason is that Policy 1 teaches responsibility. If the school bans phones completely, students will probably just sneak them in anyway. That would cause more trouble for teachers. But if we are trusted to turn them off in class and only use them at the right times, we learn how to follow rules and make good choices. That is something we will need to know in high school and when we get jobs.

Some people might say that phones are a distraction, and I understand that. But the phones would be off during class, so they would not get in the way of learning. Students who break the rule could get their phone taken away.

In conclusion, I think Policy 1 is the best choice because it keeps students safe, helps them stay organized, and teaches responsibility. Thank you for listening to my opinion.

Sincerely,
A Concerned Student
