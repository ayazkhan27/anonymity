Dear Principal,

I think students should be required to do community service. Some of my friends already do it, and I have seen how much good it can do. Helping other people is part of being a good citizen, and school is a good place to start learning that.

My friend Maya tutors kids at Lincoln Elementary every Tuesday after school. She told me one of her students could not read very well at the start of the year. Maya sat with him for thirty minutes each week, and now he reads chapter books. If Maya had not gone, that boy might have struggled a lot longer. Stuff like that matters.

Another friend, Jordan, goes with his class to clean up the trash along the creek behind our school. They fill up big bags with bottles, cans, and wrappers. Last spring they found an old bike and a whole box of plastic forks. The creek looks so much better now, and the ducks seem happier too. Nobody else was going to pick that up.

I know some students say community service takes too much time. They have sports, music lessons, jobs, and homework. I understand that. Those things are important. But community service does not have to take over your life. If the school required just a few hours each semester, that would still leave plenty of time for everything else. Most people spend more than a few hours a week on video games or scrolling on their phones. A little time spent helping others is a fair trade.

Community service also helps students in ways that are not easy to measure. When you work with someone who is different from you, you learn how to listen and be patient. You also see problems in your town that you might never notice from inside your own house. Some students might find out they like working with little kids and want to be teachers someday. Others might discover that they are good at organizing a group to get something done. These are useful skills for any job.

Some people might say that requiring service is not really volunteering, because you are forced to do it. I see that point. However, many kids would never try something new unless they had to at first. Once they are there, a lot of them end up enjoying it and keep coming back. The requirement is the push that gets them through the door.

For these reasons, I believe the school should require all students to do community service. It teaches kindness, responsibility, and teamwork, and it makes our community a better place. I hope you will consider this idea. Thank you for reading my letter.

Sincerely,
A Concerned Student
