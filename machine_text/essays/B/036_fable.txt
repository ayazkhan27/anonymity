Dear Principal,

I think students should not be required to do community service. I know helping the community is good, but making it a rule is not the right way to do it.

First, a lot of students are already really busy. I have basketball practice three days a week and homework almost every night. My friend Marcus has to watch his little sister after school because his mom works late. If we had to do service hours on top of that, we would have no time left to rest or study.

Second, if you force people to help they wont really care about it. My cousin had to pick up litter for a class and he said most kids just stood around and talked. The kids who tutor at the elementary school do a good job because they want to be there.

Instead, the school could give extra credit or a award for students who volunteer. That way more people would try it and nobody is forced.

Thank you for reading my letter.

Sincerely,
A student
