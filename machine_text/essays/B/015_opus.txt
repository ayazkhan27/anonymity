Dear Principal,

I think Policy 1 is the better choice for our school. Students should be allowed to bring their phones and use them at lunch and during free time, as long as they are turned off in class. I have a few reasons why I believe this.

First, phones are important for safety. If there is an emergency, like a lockdown or someone gets hurt, students need a way to contact their parents. My mom always tells me she feels better knowing I can call her if something happens. If phones are banned completely, parents would be worried and we would have no way to reach them fast.

Second, a lot of students need their phones after school. Some kids take the bus or walk home, and others have practice or work. They need to text their parents about rides or if plans change. If we can't even bring our phones, that makes it really hard for families.

Third, Policy 1 teaches responsibility. If students are trusted to keep their phones off in class, they learn self control. That is a skill we will need in high school and when we get jobs. Banning phones totally just treats us like little kids who can't be trusted.

Some people might say phones are a distraction. But Policy 1 already fixes that because phones have to be off during class. Lunch is our free time, so it should be okay to check messages or play a game with friends.

In conclusion, Policy 1 keeps us safe, helps families, and teaches responsibility. Please choose Policy 1.

Sincerely,
A Concerned Student
