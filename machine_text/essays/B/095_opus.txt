I agree with the principal's decision that all students should have to do at least one extracurricular activity. Some people might think it is unfair to make kids do something after school, but I think it would help students in a lot of ways. Activities help you make friends, learn new skills, and stay out of trouble.

First, extracurricular activities help students make new friends. When I joined the soccer team in sixth grade, I only knew one person on the team. By the end of the season I had like ten new friends, and some of them are still my best friends now. If everyone has to join something, then kids who are shy or new to the school will have a chance to meet people who like the same things they do. Not everybody is good at talking to people in the hallway, but it is easier when you are working on something together.

Second, students can learn skills that they don't learn in regular classes. For example, if you work on the yearbook you learn how to take good pictures and design pages on the computer. If you are on student council you learn how to speak in front of people and plan events like the school dance. These are skills that could help you get a job someday. My cousin did yearbook in middle school and now she wants to be a photographer because of it.

Third, activities keep students busy and out of trouble. A lot of kids just go home and play video games or watch their phones for hours after school. Some kids even get into trouble because they are bored. If they had practice or a club meeting, they would be doing something good with their time instead.

Some people might say that students have too much homework already or that they have jobs or have to babysit. That is a fair point, but there are lots of activities that only meet once a week, like the chess club or art club. So students could still find something that fits their schedule.

In conclusion, I think the principal made a good decision. Making students join an activity will help them make friends, learn new skills, and use their time in a better way. Everyone should support this rule.
