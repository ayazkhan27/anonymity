Dear Principal,

I heard that you are thinking about making every student do community service. I think this is a good idea, and I think you should require it. Community service helps the people who get helped, but it also helps the students who do it.

First of all, community service makes our town a better place. My friend Marcus tutors second graders at Lincoln Elementary every Tuesday after school. He told me one boy could barely read when he started, and now he reads chapter books. That kid might not have caught up without Marcus. Another example is when my church group cleaned up the park by the creek last spring. We filled like twenty trash bags with cans, wrappers and even an old tire. Now little kids can play there without stepping on broken glass. If every student at our school did something like this, think how much cleaner and nicer everything would be.

Second, community service teaches students things you can't learn in a classroom. When you help other people you learn responsibility, because they are counting on you to show up. You also learn how to work with people who are different from you, like older people or little kids. These are skills we will need when we get jobs someday. Plus it looks good on high school and college applications.

Some of my friends say community service takes too much time. They have sports, homework, and some of them just want to hang out or play video games. I understand that, because I am busy too. But the school wouldn't have to make us do a lot of hours. Maybe ten hours a semester would be fair. That is less than one hour a week. Also students could pick what they want to do, so it wouldn't feel like a punishment. Someone who loves animals could help at the shelter, and someone who likes sports could help coach a little league team.

In conclusion, I think you should require students to do community service. It helps our community, it teaches us important lessons, and it does not have to take up too much time. I hope you will make the right choice.

Sincerely,
A Concerned Student
