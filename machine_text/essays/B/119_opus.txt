Phones and Driving Don't Mix

Almost everybody I know has a cell phone, and most people check theirs about a hundred times a day. My mom says she can't even make dinner without looking at hers. Phones are a huge part of our lives, but there is one place they don't belong, and that is in the hands of someone driving a car. I think drivers should not be allowed to use cell phones in any way while they are operating a vehicle.

The first reason is that it's dangerous. When you are driving, you are in control of a machine that weighs thousands of pounds and goes really fast. If you look down at a text for just five seconds while going fifty-five miles per hour, you travel the length of a whole football field without watching the road. A lot can happen in a football field. A kid could run into the street, or the car in front of you could slam on its brakes. Every year people die because someone wanted to read a message that probably could have waited.

Some people say that talking on the phone with hands-free is fine because your eyes are still on the road. But I don't agree. Even if your hands are on the wheel, your brain is somewhere else. Last summer my uncle was on a hands-free call and he drove right past our exit and almost merged into a truck because he wasn't paying attention. He wasn't holding the phone at all, but he was still distracted. Your mind can only focus on so much at once.

Another reason is that it's not fair to other people on the road. When you choose to use your phone, you aren't only risking your own life. You are risking the lives of the people in other cars, people walking, and even your own passengers. They didn't agree to that risk. Driving is a privilege, not a right, and part of that privilege is being responsible for everyone around you.

Some people might argue that they need their phone for GPS or emergencies. I understand that, but you can set up your GPS before you start driving, and if there's an emergency you can pull over somewhere safe and make the call. Pulling over takes maybe a minute. A crash can change your life forever.

In conclusion, cell phones are useful, but they should stay put away while someone is driving. No text, call, or song choice is worth someone getting hurt. If everyone just waited until they got where they were going, the roads would be a lot safer for all of us.
