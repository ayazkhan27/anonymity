Dear Principal,

I think Policy 1 is the better choice for our school. Students should be allowed to bring their phones and use them during lunch and other free times, as long as the phones are off during class.

First, phones can be useful in emergencies. If a student gets hurt or feels sick, they can call a parent right away. Also, if there is a bad storm or some other danger, students can get news and stay safe.

Second, students need a break from class. Lunch is the only time we can relax and talk with friends. Being able to check a message or listen to music can help us feel less stressed and come back to class ready to learn.

Third, banning phones completely is hard to enforce. Students might hide them or sneak them out, and that could lead to more arguments with teachers. If we follow a clear rule that phones stay off during class, it is easier to keep things fair.

For these reasons, I think Policy 1 is the best option. Thank you for considering my opinion.

Sincerely,
A Grade 8 Student
