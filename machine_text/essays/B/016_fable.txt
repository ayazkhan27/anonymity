Dear Principal,

I heard that you are thinking about changing the cell phone policy at our school. I think Policy 1 is the better choice, because students should be able to have there phones at lunch and free times as long as they are off in class.

My first reason is safety. A lot of kids walk home or take the bus, and parents want to know where we are. Last year my bus broke down and we sat there for almost an hour. I texted my mom so she didn't freak out. If phones were banned completly, I would of had no way to tell her. Also plans change all the time, like when practice gets cancelled and you need a ride.

Second, lunch is our time. We work hard all morning and we need a break. Some kids like to listen to music or play a game for a few minutes, and that isn't hurting anybody. It actually helps us calm down so we are ready to learn in the afternoon.

Third, Policy 1 teaches responsibility. When we get to high school and then jobs, nobody is going to take our phones away. We have to learn when it is ok to use them and when it is not. If we never get the chance, how are we suppose to learn that?

Some people say phones are a distraction. That is true, but Policy 1 already fixes this because phones have to be off during class. If someone breaks the rule, the teacher can take it away for the day.

Please choose Policy 1. It is fair to students and it still keeps class time for learning.

Sincerely,
A student
