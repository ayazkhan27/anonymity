Dear Principal,

I heard that you are thinking about making a rule that students need a B average to play sports or do other activities. I think this is a bad idea and I hope you will change your mind.

First of all, a lot of kids at our school have a C average. A C is still passing. If you take away sports from all those kids, the teams would barely have enough players. Our basketball team might lose half of the people on it. That is not fair to them or to the kids who do have B's and still want a team to play on.

Second, sports and clubs are the reason some kids even come to school. My cousin was not good at math, but he never skipped because he wanted to go to football practice. If he got kicked off the team he probably would of stopped caring about school at all. Taking away activities does not make grades go up, it just makes kids feel worse.

Also, activities teach you things that classes don't. In band you learn to practice every day, and in sports you learn teamwork and how to not give up when you are losing. Those things help you in life just as much as a test score.

Some people say the rule would make students work harder. Maybe for some, but not everyone can get a B even when they try there hardest. A better idea is to keep the rule at a C and give tutoring to players who are struggling.

Please think about the students before you decide. Thank you for listening to us.

Sincerely,
A student
