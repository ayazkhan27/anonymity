Dear Principal,

I am writing to tell you which cell phone policy I think is better for our school. I believe Policy 1 is the best choice. Students should be allowed to bring phones to school and use them at lunch and other free times, as long as the phones are turned off during class.

First of all, phones are important for safety. If there is an emergency, like a fire or a lockdown, students can contact their parents right away. Also, a lot of kids have to take the bus or walk home, and sometimes plans change. If a student misses a ride or needs to stay after school for a club, they can text their family so nobody worries. With Policy 2, kids would have to ask to use the office phone, which takes time and bothers the staff.

Another reason is that lunch is supposed to be a break. Students work hard all morning and need time to relax. Using a phone to listen to music, text a friend, or look at something funny helps us feel calm before the afternoon classes. When I get to unwind at lunch, I pay more attention in class afterward.

Some people say phones cause problems because students will cheat or get distracted. I understand that worry, but Policy 1 already fixes it. Phones have to be turned off during class, so there is nothing to distract anyone while the teacher is talking. If a student breaks the rule, the teacher can take the phone until the end of the day. That is a fair punishment, and it teaches responsibility instead of just banning everything.

Finally, learning to use a phone the right way is a skill we will need our whole lives. If the school bans phones completely, students will not learn self control. Policy 1 lets us practice being responsible, which will help us when we are older and have jobs.

For these reasons, I hope you choose Policy 1. It keeps students safe, gives us a needed break, and still protects class time.

Sincerely,
A concerned student
