Dear Principal,

I think we should not change the rule so that students need a B average to play sports or join other activities. Many students have a C average, and this rule would take away something they really need.

First, sports and clubs help students learn important things that grades do not show. In basketball, I learned how to work with a team and how to keep going when I lose. In the band, I learned how to practice every day. These skills help us in school and in life.

Second, some students struggle in class but do better when they have something they enjoy. If they cannot play on the team, they might lose interest in school completely. Activities give students a reason to come to school and stay out of trouble.

Finally, a C student can still work hard. We could make a plan to help students with tutoring or study time instead of taking away their activities. That way, everyone gets a chance to improve.

Thank you for listening to our views.

Sincerely,
A Student
