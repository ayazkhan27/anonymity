Phones and Driving Don't Mix

Almost everyone has a cell phone now, and most people can't go an hour without checking it. But when someone is behind the wheel, I think they should not be allowed to use their phone in any way. Driving takes your full attention, and a phone takes that attention away.

First, texting while driving is really dangerous. When you look down to read a text, your eyes are off the road for a few seconds. At 55 miles per hour, a few seconds means you drive the length of a football field without looking. A kid could run into the street or the car in front could stop, and you would never see it.

Some people say hands-free calling is safe, but I disagree. Even if your hands are on the wheel, your brain is still focused on the conversation. My uncle was talking on speaker once and missed his exit and almost hit a car changing lanes.

Others might argue they need their phone for GPS or emergencies. That's fair, but you can set up your directions before you leave, and if there is an emergency you can pull over.

In conclusion, no text or call is worth a life. Drivers should put their phones away and just drive.
