Dear Principal,

I think students should be required to do community service. Helping other people is important, and school is the best place to start learning how.

Some of my friends tutor elementary school kids every week. One of them helps a little boy learn to read, and now he is really proud of how much he has improved. Other friends pick up litter at the park on Saturday mornings. The park looks so much better because of them. These things show that community service makes a real difference.

I know some students think service takes too much time away from homework, sports, and hanging out with friends. I understand that. But one or two hours a month is not very much, and it teaches responsibility and caring for others. Those skills will help us for the rest of our lives.

If everyone has to do it, then everyone gets to see how good it feels to help. That is why I think it should be required.

Sincerely,
A Concerned Student
