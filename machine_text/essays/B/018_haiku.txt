Dear Principal,

I think Policy 1 is the better choice for our school. Students should be allowed to bring their phones to school and use them during lunch and other free times, as long as the phones are turned off during class. I believe this is the best policy for a few reasons.

First, phones can be useful and even important. If there is an emergency, students need a way to call their parents right away. Some students also ride the bus or walk home alone, and a phone helps them feel safe. If we take phones away completely, kids have no way to reach their families during the day.

Second, Policy 1 still keeps class time focused. Students have to turn their phones off during class, so they will not be distracted while the teacher is teaching. The rule is clear and easy to follow, and it gives students a chance to show they can be responsible.

Third, students need free time to relax and talk with friends. At lunch, many kids just want to check messages, listen to music, or look up something they are curious about. Taking that away completely might make school feel too strict and less fun. Students who feel trusted are often more likely to follow the rules.

Some people worry that phones will be a distraction even at lunch. That is a fair concern. However, the school can always add more rules later if there is a real problem. Starting with a policy that trusts students is a good first step.

For these reasons, I believe Policy 1 is the better choice. It keeps students safe, keeps class time focused, and lets students enjoy their free time. Thank you for considering my opinion.

Sincerely,
A Student
