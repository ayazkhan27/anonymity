Dear Principal,

I am writing to share my opinion about the cell phone policy. I think Policy 1 is better than Policy 2. Students should be allowed to bring their phones to school and use them during lunch and other free times, as long as the phones are turned off during class.

First, phones help students stay in touch with their families. Sometimes a student needs to call a parent after school to talk about a ride home or a change in plans. If phones were banned completely, students would have to ask the front office to make every call, and that takes up time for the office staff. With Policy 1, students can quickly send a text or make a call during lunch and then get back to their day.

Second, lunch and recess are the only times students get to relax after a long morning of learning. Many students like to listen to music, check a game score, or look at pictures of their friends and family during that break. Taking phones away completely would make those short breaks feel longer and more boring. Policy 1 lets students relax while still keeping class time focused.

Third, phones can be useful in emergencies. If there is a bad storm, a fire drill, or a problem on the bus, students need a way to tell their parents what is happening. A phone that is turned off in a backpack during class can still be turned on quickly when it is needed.

I understand that some teachers worry about phones being distracting. That is why Policy 1 has a clear rule: phones must be turned off during class. Teachers can also remind students of this rule and take away a phone if a student breaks it. This keeps learning the main focus while still letting students have their phones at the right times.

For these reasons, I believe Policy 1 is the better choice for our school. Thank you for taking the time to read my letter and think about the students.

Sincerely,
A Grade 8 Student
