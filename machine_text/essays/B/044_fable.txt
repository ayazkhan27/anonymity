Some schools let students take their classes from home on a computer. I think this would be a good option for a lot of students, as long as it stays an option and not something everybody has to do.

The first reason is that not every student can make it to school every day. Last year my cousin broke her leg and missed almost three weeks. When she came back she was so far behind in math that she never really caught up. If she could of watched the class on video from her couch, she would have been fine. The same goes for kids who are sick a lot or who live far away and ride the bus for an hour.

Another reason is that some people just focus better at home. School is loud. In my English class there are thirty one kids and half of them are talking while the teacher is. At home you can pause, go back, and actually hear what is being said. You also get more sleep because you don't have to wake up at six.

Some people say students will just play video games instead of working, and honestly some will. But those students aren't paying attention in a regular classroom either. Teachers can still give tests and check who turned in work.

In conclusion, attending class from home would help students who are sick, far away, or distracted. Schools should offer it and let families decide.
