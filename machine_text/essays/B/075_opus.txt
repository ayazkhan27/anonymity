Dear Principal,

I think requiring a B average to play sports or do other activities is a bad idea. I am against this policy for a few reasons.

First, alot of students have a C average, and a C is still passing. If you take away sports from them, you are taking away the thing that makes them want to come to school. My friend plays basketball and the only reason he does his homework is because coach checks his grades. If he couldnt play he would probably just give up.

Second, sports and clubs teach things you cant learn in class. You learn teamwork, how to be responsible, and how to show up on time. These are skills people need for jobs later in life.

Third, some students are just not good at tests but they try really hard. It isnt fair to punish them for that. They should get a chance to be good at something.

Instead of a B average, maybe the school could keep the C rule and give tutoring to students who are struggling. That way everyone wins.

Thank you for listening to the students opinions.

Sincerely,
A concerned student
