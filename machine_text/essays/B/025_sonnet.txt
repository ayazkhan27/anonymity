Dear Principal,

I am writing to tell you my opinion about making all students do community service. I think that students should not be required to do it, but the school should encourage it a lot.

First of all, I think community service is a good thing. My friend Jenna tutors little kids at the elementary school every Thursday. She says it makes her feel proud when a kid finally learns how to read a hard word. Another friend of mine picks up litter at the park with her family. The park looks way better now and more people go there. So I understand why some of my friends think helping the community is so important.

However, making everyone do it could cause problems. A lot of students already have very busy schedules. Some kids have sports practice after school, and some have to take care of their younger brothers and sisters because their parents work late. Other students have a job to help their family with money. If the school forces them to do community service too, they might not have enough time for homework. Then their grades could go down, and that would be the opposite of what school is supposed to do.

Also, I think people do a better job when they want to do something. If a student is forced to pick up litter, they might just stand around and not really try. They might even start to hate volunteering. But if they choose it themselves, like Jenna did, they will care more and work harder.

Instead of a requirement, my idea is that the school could have a volunteer club and give small rewards, like a certificate or a mention in the announcements, to students who help out. The school could also plan one community day a year during school hours so nobody loses their free time. That way students can try it and see how it feels.

In conclusion, I think community service should be a choice and not a rule. Thank you for reading my letter and thinking about this.

Sincerely,
A student
