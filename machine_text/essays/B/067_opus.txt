Dear Principal,

I heard that you are thinking about changing the rules so students need at least a B average to play sports or join other activities. I think this is a bad idea, and I hope you will not do it. I understand that you want students to do better in school, but I think this policy would hurt more kids than it would help.

First of all, a lot of students at our school have a C average. A C is not failing. It means you are doing average work. If you make the rule a B, a huge number of kids would get kicked off their teams and clubs. The basketball team might not even have enough players. The same goes for the band, the drama club, and the yearbook. Some of these activities might have to shut down completely, and that would not be fair to anyone.

Second, sports and activities are sometimes the main reason kids want to come to school. My cousin is not the best at math, but he loves football. He works hard at practice every day after school. If you took football away from him, he would probably stop caring about school at all. Activities give kids something to look forward to. They also teach things you can't always learn in class, like teamwork, being responsible, and showing up on time.

Third, being on a team can actually help your grades. When you have practice, you have to manage your time better. A lot of coaches also check on their players' grades and push them to study. If kids get taken off the team, they might just go home and play video games or get into trouble instead of doing homework. That would make things worse, not better.

Some people might say that a B rule would make students try harder. Maybe that is true for some kids. But for others, a B might feel impossible, so they would just give up. I think a better idea is to keep the rule at a C average, or passing all classes. Then, if a student is struggling, the school could give them tutoring or a study hall before practice. That way they get help and still get to do what they love.

In conclusion, I think requiring a B average would take away something important from a lot of students. It could make kids less interested in school and might even hurt their grades. Please think about the students who need these activities the most. Thank you for listening to our opinions.

Sincerely,
An 8th Grade Student
