Dear Principal,

I heard that you are thinking about changing the cell phone policy at our school. I think you should pick Policy 1, which lets students bring phones and use them at lunch and free time as long as they are off during class. I believe this is the better choice for a few reasons.

First, phones are important for safety. A lot of kids walk home or ride the bus, and their parents want to know where they are. Last year my bus broke down and we sat there for almost forty minutes. I was able to text my mom so she didn't freak out. If phones were banned, she would of had no idea what happened to me. Also practice gets canceled sometimes and we need to call for a ride.

Second, Policy 1 is fair because it still protects class time. The main problem with phones is that they distract people from learning. But if they are turned off in class, that problem is already solved. Lunch is our time to relax, so I don't see how it hurts anyone if we listen to music or play a game for a few minutes.

Third, banning phones completely would not really work. Students would just sneak them in there backpacks and hide them. Then teachers would have to spend time searching and taking phones away instead of teaching. With Policy 1 students are more likely to follow the rules because the rules are reasonable. It also teaches us responsibility, because we have to learn when it is ok to use a phone and when it is not. We will need that in high school and at a job.

Some people might say students will use phones in class anyway. If that happens, the teacher can take it away for the day.

In conclusion, please choose Policy 1. It keeps us safe and it is fair to everyone.

Sincerely,
A student
