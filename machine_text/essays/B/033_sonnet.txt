Dear Principal,

I am writing to tell you my opinion about making all students do community service. I think that students should not be required to do it, but the school should encourage it a lot more.

First of all, I know that community service is a good thing. My friend Maya tutors little kids at the elementary school every Thursday, and she says it is the best part of her week. She helps them read, and some of them have gotten much better. Another friend of mine picks up litter at the park with her family. The park looks nicer now, and people use it more. So I am not saying that community service is bad. It really helps people.

However, I do not think it should be forced on everybody. A lot of students already have very busy lives. Some of my friends have sports practice after school, and then they have a ton of homework. Other kids have to watch their little brothers and sisters because their parents work late. If we make them do community service too, they might get stressed out and their grades could drop. Also, when somebody is forced to do something, they usually do not try very hard. A student who is made to clean up litter might just stand there and talk to friends instead of really working. That does not help the community at all.

Instead of making it a rule, I think the school could do other things. For example, we could have a volunteer club or a service day once a month where students can sign up. The school could also give a certificate or a shout out in the morning announcements to students who volunteer. That way, the kids who want to help will have an easy way to do it, and other kids might join when they see how much fun it is. I bet a lot more students would volunteer if it was their own choice.

In conclusion, I think community service is important, but it should not be required. Please think about making it something students can choose to do. I think this would help the community and the students too.

Sincerely,
A student at your school
