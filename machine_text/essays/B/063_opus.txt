Dear Principal,

I think requiring a B average to play sports or do other activities is a bad idea. I know you want students to do better in school, but this rule would hurt more kids than it helps.

First, a lot of students have a C average. A C is still passing. If those kids get kicked off the team, they lose something that keeps them coming to school. My cousin almost quit caring about school until he joined basketball. Now he shows up every day because he wants to play. If you take that away, some students might just give up.

Second, sports and clubs teach things you can't learn in a classroom. You learn teamwork, how to be responsible, and how to handle losing. Those skills matter for getting a job someday, just as much as grades do.

Third, some students try really hard and still get C's. Not everybody is good at tests. It isn't fair to punish someone who is doing their best.

Instead of a B average, maybe the school could keep a C average rule and give tutoring to players who are struggling. Coaches could even have study hall before practice. That way students get help and still get to play.

Please think about all the students who need these activities. Thank you for listening to our opinions.

Sincerely,
An 8th grade student
