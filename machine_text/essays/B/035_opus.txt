Dear Principal,

I think students should be required to do community service, but not too many hours. I know some kids will complain, but I think it would help our school and our town a lot.

First, community service helps people who really need it. My friend Maya tutors second graders at Lincoln Elementary after school. She told me one boy could barely read when she started, and now he reads chapter books. That is something you can't learn from a textbook. If every student did something like that, think about how many kids we could help.

Second, it makes our town look better. Last spring my church group picked up trash at the park by the creek. We filled like twenty bags in two hours. People walking their dogs actually stopped and said thank you. It made me feel proud of where I live.

I understand why some of my friends don't like the idea. They have sports, homework, and some of them babysit their little brothers and sisters. That is a lot. But community service doesn't have to take forever. Maybe the school could require ten hours a year, and let students pick what they want to do. That's less than one hour a month, so it's fair.

Also, it would look good when we apply for high school programs and jobs later. Plus it teaches responsibility.

In conclusion, I think you should make community service required, as long as it is a reasonable amount of time. It helps others and it helps us too.

Sincerely,
Jordan
