Dear Principal,

I heard that you are thinking about making all students do community service. I think this is a bad idea and students should not be required to do it. I'm not saying community service is bad, I just think it should be a choice.

First of all, alot of students don't have time. I have basketball practice three days a week and then homework after that, and some nights I don't get done until ten. My friend Marcus has to watch his little sister every day after school because his mom works late. If you add required hours on top of that, when are we supposed to do it? Our grades would probably go down.

Second, if you force people to help it's not really helping anymore. My cousin's school made everybody pick up litter at a park and she said most of the kids just stood around and complained. They didn't care because they had to be there. But my friend who tutors second graders loves it because she picked it herself, and the kids can tell she wants to be there.

Some people say that requiring it will teach us responsibility. Maybe for some kids, but I think it would just make most of us hate it.

Instead of making it required, you could give extra credit or a award for students who volunteer. That way more people would want to try it.

Sincerely,
A student
