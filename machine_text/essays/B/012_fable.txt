Dear Principal,

I think you should pick Policy 1, where we can bring phones and use them at lunch and free time. I know some teachers think phones are a distraction, but if they are turned off in class then there isn't really a problem.

My first reason is safety. A lot of kids walk home or take the bus, and our parents want to know where we are. Last year my bus was almost an hour late and I had no way to tell my mom. She was really worried. If I had my phone I could of just texted her.

Another reason is that lunch is our time. We work hard all morning and it is nice to listen to music or play a game for a few minutes. It helps us relax before the next class.

Also, if you ban phones completely, kids will just sneak them in anyways. Then teachers have to waste time catching people.

Please choose Policy 1. It is fair to everyone.

Sincerely,
A student
