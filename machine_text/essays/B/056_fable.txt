Last winter I missed almost two weeks of school because I had the flu and then a sinus infection right after it. When I came back I had a pile of chemistry worksheets and no idea what any of them meant, because I wasn't there when the teacher explained it. If my school had let me watch class from home, I think I would have been fine. That is the main reason I believe students would benefit from having the option to attend classes from home.

The first reason is that students get sick, and right now being sick means falling behind. Teachers say "just get the notes from a friend," but notes are not the same thing as hearing the lesson. In math especially, you need to see the teacher work the problem out step by step. A student who is home with a fever but can still sit up and watch a video call could keep up with the class instead of spending the next month trying to catch up. It also means sick kids would not drag themselves to school and get everyone else sick, which happens all the time during the winter.

Another reason is that not every student has an easy way to get to school. My cousin lives out in the country and her bus ride is over an hour each way. When the roads are icy, her school just cancels, and then they have to make up the days in June. With distance learning, a snow day would not have to be a wasted day. Students who have jobs or who take care of younger siblings could also use it when their schedule gets hard.

Some people argue that students at home will not pay attention. They say kids will just turn the camera off and go back to sleep. I admit this is true for some students. However, those same students are probably on their phones in the back of the classroom anyway. Being in the building does not make someone learn. Also, I am not saying everybody should stay home every day. It should be an option, and schools could take it away from students whose grades start dropping.

I do think there are things you lose at home, like labs, group work, and just seeing your friends at lunch. That is why regular school should still be the normal thing. But having the choice would help a lot of students in real situations, like mine last winter. Schools should offer distance learning because it gives students a way to keep learning when they can't be in the building.
