Dear Principal,

I heard that you are thinking about making all students do community service. I think this is a bad idea and that it should stay a choice. I know helping people is good, but forcing it on everybody is not the right way to do it.

First of all, a lot of students just don't have the time. I have basketball practice three days a week and games on Saturday, and I still have homework every night. My friend Marcus has to watch his little sister after school until his mom gets home from work at seven. If he had to go pick up trash or tutor somewhere, who is going to watch her? Some kids have way more going on at home than teachers know about. Adding more hours on top of that would only make us more stressed out and our grades would probably go down.

Second, community service doesn't mean as much if you are forced to do it. My friend Kayla tutors second graders in reading and she loves it. She talks about them all the time. The kids like her because they can tell she wants to be there. Now imagine a kid who hates it and is only there for the requirement. He is going to sit there and look at the clock, and the little kids will not learn anything. The same thing goes for cleaning up litter. People who don't care will do a sloppy job and then leave.

Also, I think being required would make students like service less. When something is a rule, it feels like a punishment. It is kind of like when you are about to clean your room and then your mom tells you to, and now you don't want to anymore.

Instead of requiring it, the school could make it easier to volunteer. You could have a sign up sheet in the office, or give extra credit, or have one day a year where classes go out together during school hours. That way more people would try it and nobody would lose time they don't have.

In conclusion, please do not make community service required. Let students choose to help because they want to.

Sincerely,
A student in eighth grade
