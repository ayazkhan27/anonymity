Dear Principal,

I think Policy 1 is the better choice for our school. Students should be allowed to bring phones to school and use them during lunch and other free times, as long as the phones are turned off during class.

First, phones can be really helpful. If a student forgets something, like the time of a club meeting or a homework assignment, they can check it quickly. Phones also help if there is an emergency, because students can call their parents right away. If we ban phones completely, kids will have no way to reach home if something goes wrong.

Second, students need a break from class. Lunch is the only time we can relax and talk with friends. Many kids like to listen to music or play a quick game during that time. It is not hurting anyone, and it helps us feel less stressed when we go back to our lessons.

Third, a full ban is hard to enforce. Students who really want their phones will find ways to hide them, and then teachers will spend more time searching backpacks instead of teaching. If we set a clear rule that phones stay off during class, most students will follow it because they know they can use them later.

I understand that phones can be distracting. That is why I agree that they should be off during class. But a total ban takes away useful things without solving the real problem. Policy 1 keeps the classrooms focused and still lets students stay connected. Please consider choosing this policy.

Sincerely,
A Grade 8 Student
