Last winter I missed almost two weeks of school because I had the flu and then a sinus infection right after it. When I came back I had a pile of makeup work in every class, and in pre-calc I was so lost that it took me until the end of the quarter to catch up. If my school had let me watch my classes from home, I don't think that would have happened. I believe students would benefit from being able to attend classes from home, as long as it is an option and not the only way to go to school.

The first reason is the one I already mentioned. Students get sick, and when they do they have two bad choices. They can stay home and fall behind, or they can come to school and cough on everybody. A lot of people pick the second one because they are scared of missing a test or a lab. If a student could log on from their bed and at least listen to the lesson, they would not have to choose. Even if they only understood half of it, that is better than getting a worksheet a week later with no explanation.

Another reason is that not every student has an easy time getting to school. My cousin lives about forty minutes outside of town and his bus picks him up at 6:10 in the morning. He is basically asleep through first period. There are also students who have jobs, or who watch their little brothers and sisters, or who have anxiety that makes a crowded building really hard for them. Distance learning would give those students a way to keep going instead of giving up. Some of them might actually do better work at home where it is quiet.

Some people say students at home will just play on their phones and not pay attention. That is true for some students, I won't lie. But those same students are on their phones in the back of the classroom too. Being in the room does not make somebody learn. Also, teachers can still take attendance, call on people, and give the same tests. If a student's grades drop, the school can make them come back in person.

I do think regular school is better for most people most of the time, because you see your friends and you can ask a question right away. But having the choice would help the students who need it. School should be about learning the material, not about what chair you are sitting in while you do it.
