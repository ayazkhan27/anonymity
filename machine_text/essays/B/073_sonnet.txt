Dear Principal,

I am writing to tell you my opinion about the possible new policy that would stop students from doing sports or other activities unless they have at least a B average. I think this policy is a bad idea and I hope you will not pass it.

First of all, a lot of students in our school have a C average. If the policy passes, all of those students would be kicked off teams and out of clubs. That is a huge number of kids. Teams might not even have enough players to play games, and clubs like drama or band might have to shut down. That would hurt the whole school, not just the students with C's.

Second, activities are often the reason students come to school and try at all. For example, my friend Marcus is not great at math, but he works hard because he loves playing basketball. He told me that he keeps his grades up just so he can stay on the team. If he got cut for having a C, he might stop caring about school completely. Sports and clubs give students something to look forward to, and that can keep them from giving up.

Also, a C is not a failing grade. A C means average, and that is okay. Some students have a hard time with certain subjects, or they have learning problems, or they have a lot of stress at home. These students should not be punished for that. A B average is really hard for some people even when they study every night. Being in an activity might actually be what helps them feel good about themselves and do better.

Another reason is that activities teach students things that you can't learn from a textbook. Sports teach teamwork, and being in a club teaches responsibility and leadership. Students also get exercise and make friends. If we take that away from kids with C's, they will have more free time to get into trouble or just sit at home playing video games, which is not helping their grades either.

I do understand why you want this policy. You want students to care about their grades and that is a good goal. But there are better ways to do it. Maybe the school could offer free tutoring after school or a study hall for students who are struggling. Maybe students could keep playing if they have at least a C average, and get a warning first if their grades drop. This would push students to do better without taking away what they love.

In conclusion, I think requiring a B average is too strict and unfair to students with C averages. Please think about the students who would be hurt by this. Thank you for listening to our views.

Sincerely,
A concerned student
