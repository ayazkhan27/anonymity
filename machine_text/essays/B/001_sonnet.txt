Dear Principal,

I am writing to tell you that I think Policy 1 is the better choice. Students should be allowed to bring their phones to school and use them at lunch and other free times, as long as the phones are turned off during class. I have a few reasons for this.

First of all, phones are important for safety. If something happens at school or on the way home, a student can call their parents right away. A lot of kids have parents who work late, and they need to text them about rides or after school plans. If phones were not allowed at all, students would have to go to the office just to make a simple call, and that wastes time. Also, in an emergency, having a phone could really help.

Second, Policy 1 gives students a break. School is a long day, and by lunch most of us are tired of working. Being able to check messages or listen to music for a little while helps us relax. When we feel less stressed, I think we pay more attention in class afterwards. Using a phone at lunch is not hurting anybody, and it is better than being bored.

Some people might say that phones cause problems like cheating or distractions. I understand that, but Policy 1 already fixes this by making students turn them off during class. If a student breaks the rule, the teacher can take the phone for the day. That way students will learn to be responsible instead of just being told no.

Banning phones completely would make many students upset, and many would probably sneak them in anyway. It is smarter to make a fair rule that everyone can follow.

For these reasons, I hope you choose Policy 1. Thank you for listening to student opinions.

Sincerely,
A student
