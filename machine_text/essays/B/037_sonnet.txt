Dear Principal,

I am writing to tell you my opinion about requiring all students to do community service. I think you should not make it a requirement, but you should encourage students to do it.

First of all, community service is a great thing. My friend Maya tutors little kids at the elementary school every Thursday, and she says she loves it. The kids get better at reading and she feels proud of herself. Another friend of mine helps clean up litter at the park near our school. The park looks a lot nicer now, and more families go there. So I understand why some of my friends think it is so important.

However, making everybody do it is a different thing. Some students have jobs after school, or they have to take care of their little brothers and sisters. Others play sports or have a lot of homework. If they are forced to do community service too, they might get really stressed out and their grades could drop. I know a boy who already goes to bed late because of basketball practice and homework. Adding more hours would be too much for him.

Also, when people are forced to do something, they usually do not try very hard. A student who is made to pick up trash might just stand around and wait for the time to be over. Volunteers who actually want to be there do a better job.

Instead of a requirement, the school could have a volunteer club and give awards to students who help out. That way people who want to serve can, and nobody feels trapped.

Thank you for reading my letter.

Sincerely,
A concerned student
