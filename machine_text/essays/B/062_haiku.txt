Dear Principal,

I think we should not change the policy so that students need at least a B average to play sports or join other activities. I know grades are important, but I do not think this rule would help students do better in school.

First, many students with a C average work very hard, and sports or clubs are often the reason they come to school every day. If they cannot play, they might stop caring about school altogether. Second, activities teach things that a classroom cannot, like teamwork, responsibility, and how to handle losing. Third, students who are struggling in class often need more support, not less. If we take away the thing they enjoy most, it will be harder for them to get better.

Instead of taking students out of activities, the school could offer free tutoring or study halls for students who have low grades. That way, students can get help and still be part of the team or club.

Thank you for listening to the students' views.

Sincerely,
A Concerned Student
