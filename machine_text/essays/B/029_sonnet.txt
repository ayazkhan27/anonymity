Dear Principal,

I am writing to tell you my opinion about requiring all students to do community service. I think students should not be forced to do it, but the school should encourage it.

First of all, some of my friends already do community service and they love it. One friend tutors second graders in reading after school, and she says it makes her feel proud when a kid finally sounds out a hard word. Another friend helps clean up litter at the park every month. Nobody made them do it, so they actually care about it.

On the other hand, many students are really busy. Some have sports practice, jobs, or have to babysit their little brothers and sisters. My friend Marcus works at his family's store every day after school. If the school made him do service hours too, he would have almost no time left for his homework. That would not be fair, and his grades might go down.

Also, when people are forced to do something, they usually do the bare minimum. Students might just stand around during a cleanup to get their hours signed off. That does not help the community very much.

Instead of a requirement, I think the school should set up clubs and give awards to students who volunteer. That way kids can choose what they want to do. Thank you for reading my letter.

Sincerely,
A concerned student
