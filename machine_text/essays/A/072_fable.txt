Dear Principal,

I am writing about the proposal to require a B average for sports and other activities. I understand why the idea is appealing, since school is supposed to be about learning first. Still, I think the policy would hurt more students than it would help, and I am asking you to keep the current requirement.

Start with the numbers. A large share of our students carry a C average. A C is a passing grade, and it means a student is meeting the standard the school itself set. Under the new rule those students would lose band, the soccer team, drama, the yearbook and the robotics club all at once. Rosters would shrink, and some teams and clubs might not have enough members to continue at all.

I also doubt the rule would raise grades. For many students, an activity is the reason they come to school on time and stay out of trouble. My cousin was a C student who kept his grades from slipping lower because his wrestling coach checked his report card every two weeks. If he had been cut from the team, he would not have spent those hours studying. He would have spent them at home, with nobody checking anything.

Activities teach things too. A student who is weak in algebra may be the best person in the building at running a stage crew or leading a defense. Those students deserve one place where they succeed, and the confidence often carries back into class.

There is a fairer way to reach your goal. Keep the C requirement, but add a study hall or tutoring session for any participant whose grades drop during the season. Coaches and club advisers could help enforce it. That would push students to improve without taking away the thing that keeps them connected to school.

Thank you for asking what we think before deciding.

Sincerely,
A concerned student
