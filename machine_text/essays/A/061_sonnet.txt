Dear Principal,

I am writing as a student to share my view on the proposed policy that would bar students from sports and other activities unless they keep at least a B average. I respectfully disagree with it.

First, many students who earn a C average are not failing. They are passing every class, and a C is a respectable grade. Penalizing them by taking away the one part of school they enjoy treats an average result as if it were a serious problem.

Second, activities often help students do better in class. A student on the track team learns to manage time, since practice leaves fewer free hours and homework has to be finished on a schedule. Someone in the drama club or band may feel connected to school for the first time, and that connection gives them a reason to show up and try. If we remove these activities from the students who struggle most, we may push their grades lower, not higher.

Third, the rule would be unfair. Some students work after school, care for younger siblings, or have learning difficulties that make a B much harder to reach. For them, a sport might be the place where they succeed. Cutting them off tells them they do not belong.

I agree that grades matter, and I understand why you want to encourage them. A better idea would be a lower requirement, such as a C average, combined with free tutoring and study hall for anyone who falls below it. That would support students instead of shutting them out.

Thank you for taking the time to hear our opinions.

Sincerely,
A concerned student
