Dear Principal,

I know you are deciding whether every student should have to do community service, and I think you should make it a requirement. I have friends on both sides of this, so I have heard the arguments, but I believe the good it does outweighs the time it takes.

First, community service teaches things a classroom cannot. My friend Maya tutors second graders at Lincoln Elementary twice a week. She told me that explaining fractions to a seven-year-old made her finally understand them herself. She also learned patience and how to talk to people who are different from her. Those are skills every student needs, and many would never practice them unless the school asked them to.

Second, service makes our town better, and students are part of this town. Last spring a group from our class picked up trash along the creek behind the park. In one Saturday morning they filled more than forty bags. Now families walk there again. When students see a difference they made with their own hands, they start to care about where they live.

I understand why some of my friends worry about time. They have homework, sports, and jobs. But the requirement does not have to be huge. Even twenty hours a year is less than one hour every two weeks. The school could also let students pick projects that fit their interests, like coaching younger kids or helping at the animal shelter, so it feels less like a chore.

Requiring service would help students grow and help our community at the same time. Please make it part of what it means to graduate from our school.

Sincerely,
A Student
