Dear Principal Morris,

I heard you are deciding whether every student should have to do community service. I think you should require it, but keep the number of hours small, maybe ten a semester.

My friend Dana tutors second graders at Lincoln Elementary on Tuesdays. She started because her church group asked her to, and she complained about it for a month. Now she talks about a boy named Marcus who could not read a full page in September and finished a chapter book in March. She would never have met him if someone had not pushed her to sign up. A lot of us are like that. We don't volunteer because nobody asks, and then we assume we wouldn't like it.

I know the argument on the other side. Some of my friends work after school or watch their little brothers and sisters, and they say they have no time left. That is a fair point, which is why the requirement should be small and flexible. Ten hours is one Saturday morning a month. Students could also count things they already do, like coaching a youth soccer team or helping at a food pantry.

The park behind our school is covered in litter, and nobody cleans it. If each student gave a few hours, it would look different by spring. Please make service a requirement, and let us choose how we do it.

Sincerely,
A student
