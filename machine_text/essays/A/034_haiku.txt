Dear Principal,

I believe students should be required to perform community service. Some of my friends tutor elementary school children, and others help clean up litter in our neighborhood parks. These activities show that helping others matters, and they give students a chance to see how their community works beyond the classroom.

I understand that some students feel community service takes time away from homework, sports, jobs, or other activities. However, a few hours each month is a small commitment, and students can often schedule it around their other responsibilities. Many of the skills gained, such as patience, teamwork, and responsibility, will help them in school and in future careers.

Requiring service also makes it fair for everyone. When all students take part, no one is left out, and the school builds a culture of care. I urge you to adopt this requirement.

Sincerely,
A Concerned Student
