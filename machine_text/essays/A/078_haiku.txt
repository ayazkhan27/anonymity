Dear Principal,

I respectfully urge you not to require a B average for students to take part in sports and other activities. A grade requirement may sound like a way to encourage studying, but it would most likely punish the students who need the most support.

Many students with a C average are struggling in one or two classes, not failing across the board. For them, sports, band, or debate are often the reason they come to school, stay engaged, and build friendships with teachers and classmates. Taking those activities away removes the very structure that could help their grades improve.

Activities also teach skills that classrooms do not always measure: teamwork, time management, and handling pressure. Students who learn these lessons often return to their studies with more focus and discipline.

Instead of cutting students off, the school could offer tutoring, study halls, or required academic check-ins for anyone whose grades slip. That approach supports struggling students rather than shutting them out.

Thank you for considering the views of students.

Sincerely,
A Concerned Student
