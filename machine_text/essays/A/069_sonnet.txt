Dear Principal,

I am writing to respectfully oppose requiring a grade B average for students to join sports and other activities.

First, many students with a C average are doing honest work. A C is a passing grade, and punishing those students by taking away activities sends the message that average effort does not count.

Second, activities often help grades instead of hurting them. Practice gives students a schedule, and teams make them want to stay eligible. A student who loves soccer may study harder just to keep playing. Without it, that student might lose the one reason he or she comes to school happily.

Third, some students struggle in class but shine in art, music, or athletics. These activities build confidence, teamwork, and friendships that classrooms alone do not provide.

Instead of a B requirement, I suggest a lower standard, such as a C average, along with free tutoring for students who fall behind. That keeps academics important while still giving every student a chance to belong.

Thank you for considering our views.

Sincerely,
A Concerned Student
