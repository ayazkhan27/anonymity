Dear Principal,

I am writing to ask you not to require a B average for students who want to play sports or join other activities. I understand the goal is to push students to do better in class, but I think this policy would hurt the very students it is meant to help.

First, many students here have a C average. A C is a passing grade. If these students are cut from teams and clubs, a large part of the school would lose something that keeps them connected. For some of my friends, practice after school is the main reason they show up in the morning at all.

Second, activities teach things a classroom cannot. On the soccer team I learned to manage my time, take criticism from a coach, and work with people I did not always like. Those lessons matter just as much as a test score.

Finally, taking activities away does not raise grades by itself. A better plan would be to require tutoring or study hall for students below a C, so they get help instead of punishment.

Please keep activities open to every student who is passing.

Sincerely,
A concerned student
