Dear Principal,

I am writing to ask you to choose Policy 1, which lets students bring phones to school and use them at lunch and during free periods, provided they stay off in class.

My main reason is safety. Many of us walk home, take the bus, or stay late for practice, and our parents need a way to reach us when plans change. A phone left at home cannot help a student whose ride never shows up.

Second, Policy 1 teaches responsibility. Learning when to put a phone away is a skill we will need at every job we ever hold. A total ban only postpones that lesson.

Finally, Policy 2 would be hard to enforce. Teachers would spend their time searching backpacks instead of teaching, and students would simply hide their phones.

Policy 1 protects class time while trusting students during their own time. I hope you will give us the chance to earn that trust.

Sincerely,
A Student
