Dear Principal,

I am writing about the idea of requiring a B average to play sports or join activities. I understand why it sounds good. You want us to take our classes seriously. But I think this rule would hurt more students than it would help, and I am asking you not to pass it.

First, a C is a passing grade. A student with a C average is doing what the school asks, even if it is not perfect. Many of those students work hard for that C. Some have jobs after school, some watch younger brothers and sisters, and some just struggle with math or reading. Taking away the one thing they look forward to will not make algebra easier for them.

Second, activities are the reason a lot of students come to school at all. My cousin was on the wrestling team, and he told me he only kept his grades up because his coach checked his report card every few weeks. If he had been cut from the team for having a C, nobody would have been checking, and he would have had nothing to work for. Coaches and club advisors push students in a way that a rule cannot.

Third, think about what those students would do with the extra time. Most would not spend it studying. They would go home, sit on their phones, or hang around with nothing to do. Practice gives them a schedule and a place to be.

A fairer plan would be to keep the current C requirement and add help for students who are close to failing, like a study hall before practice or tutoring from teammates. That way grades improve and nobody is shut out.

Thank you for asking what we think.

Sincerely,
A concerned student
