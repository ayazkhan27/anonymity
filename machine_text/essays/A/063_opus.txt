Dear Principal,

I am writing to ask you not to require a B average for students to take part in sports and other activities. I understand that you want students to take their grades seriously, but I think this policy would hurt the very students it is meant to help.

First, many students at our school have a C average. A C is a passing grade. If these students are cut from teams and clubs, a large part of the school will lose something that keeps them coming to class every day. For a lot of kids, practice after school is the reason they show up and stay out of trouble.

Second, activities teach lessons that a classroom cannot. On a team you learn to work with others, manage your time, and keep going after a loss. In the band or the debate club you learn discipline and confidence. Taking these away from a C student will not make them a better student. It may make them give up.

Finally, there is a better way to reach your goal. Instead of a B cutoff, the school could keep a C requirement and offer study hall or tutoring for players whose grades drop. Coaches could check grades every few weeks and help students who are struggling.

Please keep activities open to students who are passing. Support them instead of shutting them out.

Sincerely,
A Concerned Student
