Dear Principal,

I am writing to ask you to adopt Policy 1, which would let students bring phones to school and use them during lunch and other free times, as long as they stay off during class.

First, phones keep students safe. If there is an emergency, a lockdown, or a sudden change in pickup plans, students need a way to reach their families. Banning phones entirely would leave many parents worried and unable to contact their children when it matters most.

Second, Policy 1 already protects learning. The biggest concern about phones is distraction in class, and this policy solves that by requiring phones to be turned off during lessons. Teachers would still have quiet, focused classrooms, and students who break the rule could face clear consequences.

Third, allowing phones during free time teaches responsibility. In a few years, we will be in college or at jobs where nobody takes our phones away. Learning to put a phone away when it is time to work, and to use it sensibly during breaks, is a skill we will need for life.

Finally, a total ban would be difficult to enforce and would create constant conflict between students and staff.

For these reasons, I believe Policy 1 is the better choice for our school.

Sincerely,
A Concerned Student
