Dear Principal,

I am writing to share my opinion on whether students should be required to perform community service. I believe schools should not require it, but they should strongly encourage it.

Service matters. My friends who tutor elementary school children see real progress when a child finally reads a whole paragraph, and the ones who clean up litter take pride in a park that looks better. These experiences teach responsibility and empathy in ways a classroom cannot.

However, a requirement would create problems. Some students work after school to help their families, others care for younger siblings, and many play sports or study for demanding classes. For them, mandatory hours would be one more burden, and they might do the work resentfully. A student who picks up litter only to fill out a form is not learning to care about the community.

A better approach is to make service easy and appealing. The school could host volunteer days, partner with local organizations, and recognize participating students with awards. Students who choose to serve do so with real commitment, and that commitment is what helps a community.

For these reasons, I ask you to encourage community service rather than require it.

Sincerely,
A concerned student
