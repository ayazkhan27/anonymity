Dear Principal Morgan,

I am writing about your decision on whether to require community service for every student. I believe you should make it a requirement, because the benefits to students and to our town are worth the hours it takes.

First, community service teaches things a classroom cannot. Last spring my friend Danielle started tutoring second graders at Lincoln Elementary twice a week. She told me that explaining subtraction to a seven-year-old made her understand math better herself, and that she learned patience she never had before. My friend Marcus joined a group that cleans litter along Riverside Park. He says he never thought about where trash ends up until he pulled forty plastic bottles out of the creek in one afternoon. Experiences like these change how students see responsibility, and many students would never try them unless they were required to.

Second, our community actually needs the help. The elementary school has more struggling readers than its teachers can work with one-on-one. The food bank on Main Street is short of volunteers on weekends. If every student at our school gave even a few hours each semester, that would add up to thousands of hours of help. That is a real difference for the people who depend on these services.

I understand why some of my friends are against the idea. They have homework, sports, jobs, and family duties, and they worry that service will take time they do not have. That concern is fair, but it can be solved with a reasonable plan. The requirement could be small, such as ten hours per semester, and students could choose projects that fit their schedules. Some of the service could even happen during school, like reading to younger students during a free period. A flexible requirement would ask a little of everyone without overwhelming anyone.

Finally, a requirement makes service part of who we are as a school. When everyone takes part, it stops being something only a few students do and becomes something we are proud of together.

For these reasons, I hope you will require community service for all students. A few hours from each of us can teach us lessons we will keep and help the neighbors who need it most.

Sincerely,
Jordan Ellis
