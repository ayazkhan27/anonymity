Dear Principal,

I am writing about your decision on whether to require community service. I believe every student should be required to do it, as long as the school keeps the hours reasonable and lets students choose how they help.

First, community service teaches things a classroom cannot. My friend Maya tutors second graders at Lincoln Elementary twice a week. She told me that explaining fractions to a seven-year-old made her understand them better herself. She also learned patience and how to talk to people who are frustrated. Those are skills every job will ask for later.

Second, service connects students to the place they live. Last spring a group of us cleaned up litter along the creek behind the park. We filled twenty bags in one afternoon. Now when I walk past that creek, I notice when trash shows up, and I pick it up. I would never have cared about that spot if I had not worked on it.

I understand that some students feel they are already too busy with homework, sports, and jobs. That concern is fair. However, a requirement of around twenty hours a year is less than half an hour a week. The school could also count activities students already do, like coaching a youth team or helping at a church food drive, so it does not feel like an extra burden.

Requiring service would help our community and make us better people. Please consider making it part of what it means to graduate from our school.

Sincerely,
A Concerned Student
