Dear Principal,

I am writing to respectfully disagree with the proposal to require a grade B average for participation in sports and other activities. I understand the goal is to encourage better grades, but I believe this rule would hurt many students more than it would help.

First, many students with a C average are not failing. They are passing every class, and some of them work hard for those grades. Sports and clubs are often the very reason they come to school and stay motivated. If we take away their teams, we may take away their best reason to try. A student who feels left out of school life is more likely to give up, not study harder.

Second, activities teach skills that a report card does not measure. Teamwork, discipline, time management, and leadership all come from practicing a sport or running a club. These lessons will matter just as much after graduation as any single grade.

Third, the rule would be unfair to students who struggle in one subject but excel elsewhere. A talented athlete with one difficult math class should not lose a season because of it.

A better solution is to offer tutoring, study halls, or a minimum requirement such as a C average. That way, students are supported rather than punished.

Thank you for considering our views.

Sincerely,
A Concerned Student
