Distance learning is often described as a poor copy of the real classroom, but I think students would benefit from having the option to attend classes from home. The key word is option. For many students, a school that offers both choices serves them far better than one that offers only a building.

The first reason is health. A student with a chronic illness, a broken leg, or a long recovery from surgery often falls behind simply because getting to school is too hard. With video conferencing, that student can still hear the lesson, ask questions, and turn in work on time. Instead of missing three weeks and struggling to catch up, he stays with his class. The same is true during a bad snowstorm or a flu outbreak, when sending everyone to school helps no one.

The second reason is that learning at home can fit the student's situation. Some teenagers care for younger siblings, and others live hours from the nearest school. A girl who spends ninety minutes on a bus each way loses three hours a day that she could use to study or sleep. Others are talented athletes or actors whose training schedules clash with a standard school day. Distance learning lets them keep up with their education without giving up something they are good at.

Critics say that students will lose social skills or get distracted by their phones and televisions. These worries are fair, which is why distance learning should not replace regular school for everyone. Many students need the structure of a classroom and the friendships they build there. But that is an argument for choice, not against it. A school can ask students to attend in person most of the time and allow home attendance when there is a good reason, with teachers checking attendance and grades to make sure the student is succeeding.

In the end, education is about learning, not about where the chair happens to be. When a student who would otherwise miss class can log in and take part, everyone gains. Schools should offer distance learning as an option, and students who need it should be able to use it.
