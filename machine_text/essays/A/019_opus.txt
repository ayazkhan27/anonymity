Dear Principal,

I am writing to urge you to adopt Policy 1, which would let students bring phones to school and use them during lunch and other free times, as long as they stay off during class.

First, phones matter for safety. Parents want to reach their children when plans change, when someone gets sick, or during an emergency. A complete ban would cut off that connection during the hours families may need it most.

Second, Policy 1 teaches responsibility. Students will soon live in a world where they must decide for themselves when a phone is appropriate. Letting us practice that judgment at school, with clear rules and consequences, prepares us better than simply taking phones away.

Third, a total ban would be hard to enforce. Many students would bring phones anyway and hide them, which creates more conflict between teachers and students. A fair rule that students can follow is more likely to be respected.

Finally, free time is a good moment for checking messages, coordinating rides, or looking up homework details.

Policy 1 keeps classrooms focused while respecting students' needs. I hope you will choose it.

Sincerely,
A Concerned Student
