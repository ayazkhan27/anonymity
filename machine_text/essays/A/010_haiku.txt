Dear Principal,

I believe Policy 1 is the better choice: students should be allowed to bring phones to school and use them during lunch and other free times, as long as phones stay off during class.

First, phones give students a safety net. Parents want to reach their children after a bus delay, a family emergency, or a dismissal change, and a phone makes that possible without a trip to the front office.

Second, a total ban is hard to enforce. Students who want their phones will hide them, and staff will spend class time hunting for devices. A clear rule that allows phones at free times but bans them during class is easier to understand and follow.

Third, lunch and breaks are when students rest and socialize. Checking messages, reading news, or calling family during these periods is a reasonable use of personal time, and it keeps students from feeling treated like young children.

Finally, Policy 1 teaches responsibility. Students learn when a device is appropriate and when it is not, a skill they will need in college and at work. Banning phones entirely removes that chance to practice.

For these reasons, I ask that you adopt Policy 1, with strict enforcement during class time.

Respectfully,
A Student
