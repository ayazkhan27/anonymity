Dear Principal,

I believe Policy 1 is the better choice: students should be allowed to bring phones and use them at lunch and during free time, as long as they are turned off in class.

First, phones are a safety tool. If a bus breaks down, practice runs late, or a family emergency happens, a student can reach a parent right away. Under a total ban, that call has to go through the front office, which wastes time when it matters most.

Second, a complete ban is hard to enforce and creates constant conflict. Many students will hide phones in their backpacks anyway, and teachers will spend class time searching for them instead of teaching. Policy 1 gives students a clear rule they can actually follow: phones off during class, free to use otherwise. People tend to respect a rule that feels fair.

Third, lunch and free time are when students need a break. Checking a message from home, listening to music, or looking up a homework question helps students relax and come back to class ready to focus.

Finally, learning to manage a phone is a real-life skill. Students will carry phones for the rest of their lives, and school is a good place to practice self-control.

For these reasons, I urge you to adopt Policy 1.

Sincerely,
A concerned student
