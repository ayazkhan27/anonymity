Dear Principal,

I am writing to urge you to adopt Policy 1, which lets students bring phones to school and use them at lunch and during other free times, as long as they are turned off in class. I believe this policy is fairer, safer, and more realistic than a complete ban.

First, a total ban would be very hard to enforce. Almost every student owns a phone, and many would simply hide theirs in a backpack or pocket. Teachers would then spend valuable class time hunting for hidden devices and handing out punishments instead of teaching. Policy 1 avoids this problem because the rule is simple and reasonable. Students who know they can check their phones at lunch have little reason to sneak a look during a lesson.

Second, phones are a matter of safety. Many of us have parents who work late, siblings we need to pick up, or medical conditions we need to manage. If there is an emergency, a student should be able to reach family quickly. Under Policy 2, a student would have to find a school office phone, which could waste precious minutes. With Policy 1, a phone is still close by when it is truly needed.

Third, this policy teaches responsibility. Students will soon be in a world of jobs and college where phones are everywhere and no one takes them away. Learning to put a phone away when it is time to focus is a skill we need to practice, not avoid. A ban only delays that lesson, while Policy 1 lets us earn trust and show maturity every day.

Some people worry that phones distract students and lead to cyberbullying or cheating. These are real concerns, and that is why Policy 1 keeps phones off during class. The school can also set clear penalties for misuse, such as losing phone privileges for a week. That targets the students who break the rules without punishing everyone.

For these reasons, I hope you will choose Policy 1. It protects learning time while respecting students' needs and teaching us to be responsible.

Sincerely,
A Concerned Student
