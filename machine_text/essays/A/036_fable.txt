Dear Principal,

I am writing to ask you not to make community service a requirement for every student. I think service is valuable, and that is exactly why I would hate to see it turned into homework.

My friend Dana tutors second graders in reading every Thursday. She goes because she likes those kids, and they can tell. Now picture a student who is only there to get a form signed. He watches the clock, the child notices, and nobody gains much from the hour.

There is also the matter of time. Several of my classmates work after school to help their families pay bills. Others look after younger brothers and sisters until a parent gets home at seven. A required number of hours would land hardest on the students who already have the least room in their week.

Instead, please make it easier to volunteer. Post openings, give credit to those who take part, and invite groups to visit. Many of us will sign up by choice.

Sincerely,
A student
