Dear Principal,

I believe Policy 1 is the better choice. Letting students have phones during lunch and free time, as long as they are off in class, gives us the benefits of phones without the biggest problems.

First, phones are a safety tool. If there is an emergency, a late bus, or a change in after-school plans, students can reach their families right away. A total ban would remove that option, and many parents would worry about it.

Second, Policy 1 teaches responsibility. Students will have to decide for themselves when a phone is appropriate. Learning to put a phone away when it is time to focus is a skill we will need in college and at work. If phones are banned completely, we never get the chance to practice it.

Third, a full ban would be very hard to enforce. Teachers would spend time searching for hidden phones and arguing with students, and that takes time away from teaching. Under Policy 1, the rule is simple and fair: phones are off during class. Students who break it can lose their phone privileges.

Lunch is also the one time we can relax, check on family, or talk with friends. That break helps us concentrate better in the afternoon.

For these reasons, I hope you will choose Policy 1.

Sincerely,
A Concerned Student
