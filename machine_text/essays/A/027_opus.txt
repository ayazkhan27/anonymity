Dear Principal,

I think our school should require every student to do some community service, as long as the number of hours stays reasonable.

Some of my friends tutor second graders at Lincoln Elementary after school. At first they went only because a teacher asked them to. Now they talk about "their kids" all the time, and one of them wants to become a teacher. She would never have found that out if nobody had pushed her to try.

Other friends spent a Saturday picking up litter along the creek behind the park. They filled twenty bags, and the next week people were walking their dogs there again. A few hours of work changed the place for the whole neighborhood.

I know some students worry that service will take time away from homework, sports, or jobs. That is a fair concern. But a requirement of ten or fifteen hours a year, with many choices of when and where to serve, would not overload anyone.

Required service teaches responsibility and helps our town. Please make it part of our school.

Sincerely,
A concerned student
