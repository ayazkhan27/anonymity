Dear Principal,

I am writing about the proposal to require a B average for sports and other activities. I understand the reason behind it, since school is supposed to come first. Still, I think the rule would hurt more students than it would help, and I am asking you to keep the requirement at a C.

First, a C is a passing grade. A student with a C average is doing what the school asks, even if the work is not outstanding. Many of us study hard and still land there, especially in classes like algebra or chemistry. Taking away the team or the band from those students punishes effort that simply has not paid off yet.

Second, activities are often the reason students keep trying in class. My friend on the soccer team does his homework on the bus because he knows the coach checks grades every Friday. If he lost soccer, he would not suddenly spend those hours studying. He would go home and play video games, and his grades would probably drop.

Third, activities teach things that classes do not. Showing up on time, working with people you did not choose, and losing without quitting are all lessons students need. Those who struggle in class may need them most.

Finally, with so many students at a C, teams and clubs would shrink. Some might not have enough members to continue at all.

A better plan would be to keep the C rule and add tutoring for students who are close to failing. That would raise grades without taking away what keeps us coming to school.

Sincerely,
A concerned student
