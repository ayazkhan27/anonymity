Dear Principal,

I am writing about the proposal to require a B average for sports and other activities. I understand the reason behind it, since school is supposed to come first, but I think the rule would hurt more students than it would help, and I am asking you not to adopt it.

Most of the students at this school have a C average. A C is a passing grade. It means a student is doing the work and meeting the standard the school itself set. If we tell those students they cannot play soccer or join the band, we are punishing them for doing what we said was acceptable. Half the teams would not have enough players to finish a season.

For a lot of students, the activity is the reason they try in class at all. My cousin kept his grades up through eighth grade because his wrestling coach checked his report card every quarter. When he quit the team, nobody was checking, and his grades got worse, not better. Taking away the thing a student cares about does not give him more study time. It gives him more time on his phone.

Activities also teach things that classes do not. A student who shows up to practice at six in the morning is learning to keep a commitment. A student in the drama club learns to speak in front of two hundred people. Those lessons matter after graduation as much as algebra does.

If you want grades to improve, I would suggest keeping the C requirement and adding a study hall for any athlete or club member who falls below a B. That way students get help without losing the one part of school they look forward to.

Thank you for asking for our opinions.

Sincerely,
A student
