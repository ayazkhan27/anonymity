I think students should be allowed to take classes from home, but only if it stays a choice and doesn't replace going to school. If students get to pick, the ones who do better at home can stay home and the ones who need a classroom can still go.

My first reason is access. Some kids live really far from school, some have an illness that lasts a long time, and some have to watch their little brothers or sisters while their parents are at work. For them one missed day can turn into a missed week, and then they just fall behind. If they could join the class on a video call, they would hear the same lesson and could ask questions like everybody else. Like if a girl has surgery, she could keep up in algebra instead of coming back a month later to stuff she never learned.

My second reason is that people learn differently. Some students get nervous in a crowded room or get distracted by the kids around them, and they focus better somewhere quiet. Also if lessons are recorded you can pause and rewatch a part you didn't get, which you can't do when the teacher is talking at the front of the room.

Distance learning does have problems though. Not every family has good internet or a quiet place to work, and some students need a teacher right there or they won't do anything. It can also get lonely at home, because a lot of school is about working with other people. That's why it should be a choice and not something everyone has to do, and schools would need to lend out laptops and check on the kids learning from home a lot.

So it won't work for everyone, but for some students it could be the difference between keeping up and falling behind.
