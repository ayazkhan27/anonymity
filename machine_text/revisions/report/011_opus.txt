Dear Principal,

I am writing about the cell phone policy you are thinking about changing. I think Policy 1 is the better choice. Students should be allowed to bring phones to school and use them at lunch and during free time, as long as the phones are off during class.

My first reason is safety. A lot of parents want to be able to reach their kids if there is an emergency. Students also need a way to call home if they get sick, miss the bus, or their plans change after school. If something really bad happened at school, a phone would let a student call their family or 911 right away. Banning phones would take that away from everybody just to stop a few distractions.

My second reason is that phones help us keep our lives organized. Lots of us have sports practice, after school jobs, or little brothers and sisters to pick up. Sending a quick text at lunch to a coach or a parent can fix a schedule problem before it turns into a bigger one. Our phones also have calendars, reminders, and school apps that help us keep track of homework.

Third, Policy 1 teaches responsibility. After we graduate, nobody is going to take our phones away at work or in college. We will have to decide for ourselves when it is okay to use them. If we practice that self control now, with clear rules for class time, we will be more ready than if there is a total ban that just takes the choice away.

Some people are worried that students will break the rule and use their phones in class anyway. I get that, but the answer is to enforce the rule, not punish every student. Teachers could take the phone from anyone caught using it in class, and kids who keep doing it could lose the privilege. I think most students would follow the rule if they knew they could check their phones at lunch.

Policy 2 would also be hard to enforce, and kids would probably just hide their phones. Policy 1 is fair and it makes sense. I hope you pick it.

Sincerely,
A Concerned Student
