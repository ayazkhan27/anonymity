Dear Principal,

I am writing as a student about the new idea to keep students out of sports and clubs unless they have a B average. I don't agree with it, and here is why.

First, a lot of kids with a C average are not failing. They pass all their classes, and a C is still an okay grade. Taking away the one thing they like about school seems like a big punishment for an average grade.

Second, activities can actually help people do better in class. If you are on the track team, you have to learn to manage your time, because practice takes up part of the afternoon and you still have to get your homework done. Someone in band or drama might feel like they belong at school for the first time, and that gives them a reason to come and try. If we take activities away from the students who struggle the most, their grades might go down instead of up.

Third, the rule isn't fair. Some students have a job after school, take care of their little brothers and sisters, or have a learning problem that makes getting a B way harder. For them, a sport might be the one place where they feel good about themselves. Kicking them out tells them they don't belong.

I do know grades are important, and I get why you want students to work harder on them. Maybe a better idea would be to make the rule a C average and also offer free tutoring or a study hall for anybody who falls below it. That would help students instead of shutting them out.

Thank you for reading my letter and listening to what students think.

Sincerely,
A concerned student
