I think drivers should not use their cell phones at all while the car is moving. The one exception I would allow is a phone that is mounted on the dash and set up for directions before the trip starts. Driving takes your full attention, and a phone is designed to grab that attention for itself.

A lot of people think they can handle a quick look at a screen. I used to think that too, but the numbers show otherwise. If you look away from the road for five seconds at highway speed, you travel the length of a football field without seeing what is in front of you. Changing a song or reading one text can take that long. Every year thousands of people get hurt in crashes caused by distraction, and many of them are passengers, pedestrians, and other drivers who never chose to share that risk.

Some people say hands-free calling fixes this. I disagree. Studies on attention say the problem is mostly in your mind, not your hands. You can be looking right at a stoplight during a serious conversation and not notice it turned red. The person on the other end can't see the traffic, so your brain is split between them and the road. Voice texting and hands-free calls feel safer, but your reaction time is about the same as it is with a phone in your hand.

Other people say a full ban is too strict because phones hold maps, music, and emergency tools. That is a fair point, and it is why I would keep a small exception. A driver can type in the destination before leaving, mount the phone, and listen to the directions. If there is a real emergency, the driver can pull over in a safe spot and call for help. None of that needs your eyes or mind to leave the road while the car is rolling.

I also think we need laws because willpower isn't enough. Plenty of drivers will tell you that texting behind the wheel is dangerous and then do it anyway. Clear rules with real fines can change habits, the same way seat belt laws did. Today almost nobody thinks buckling up is a big hassle, and it saves many lives.

A call or a message can nearly always wait ten minutes. A life can't be given back. So drivers should keep their phones out of their hands and out of their heads until the car is parked, and then enjoy catching up with everyone.
