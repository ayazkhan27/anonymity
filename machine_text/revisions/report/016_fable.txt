Dear Principal Harmon,

I heard you are thinking about changing the cell phone policy. I think you should pick Policy 1, where we can use phones at lunch and free time but they have to be off in class.

My first reason is safety. A lot of us ride the bus, walk home, or stay late for practice. Last month my rehearsal got out forty minutes early. Without my phone I would have been stuck outside in the dark until my mom got off work. A ban doesn't stop stuff like that from happening. It just sends everybody to the front office to call, and the office is already busy.

Second, a ban would be really hard to enforce. Kids are still going to bring phones. They will just hide them in backpacks or use them in the bathroom, and teachers will waste class time looking for phones instead of teaching. Policy 1 is a rule most kids would actually follow because it is fair. If I know I can check my phone at lunch, I don't need to sneak it during math.

Third, Policy 1 teaches us something. In a few years we will have jobs and nobody is going to take our phones away. We will have to decide on our own when to put them down. School is a good place to practice that.

I know people worry about phones being distracting, and I worry about that too. That is why phones should be all the way off in class, not just on silent, and the teacher should take it if someone breaks the rule.

Please choose Policy 1. It protects class time without treating every student like a problem.

Sincerely,
An eighth-grade student
