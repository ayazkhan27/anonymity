Dear Principal,

I am writing about the idea of making students have a B average to play sports or join activities. I think this would be a mistake.

A lot of us have a C average, and we are not lazy. Some students study for hours and still get a C in math or science. Taking away the team would not raise that grade. It would just take away the one part of school they look forward to.

Sports also keep students on track. My cousin told me the only reason he showed up to first period was because his coach checked attendance. Without practice, kids like him would have less reason to come at all, and then their grades could drop even lower.

Activities teach things too. You learn to be on time, work with people you don't like, and lose without quitting.

A fairer rule would be to keep the C rule and give tutoring to players who are slipping. Please keep the doors open for us.

Sincerely,
A student
