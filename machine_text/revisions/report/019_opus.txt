Dear Principal,

I think you should pick Policy 1. It lets students bring phones to school and use them at lunch and other free times, as long as they are off during class.

First, phones are about safety. Parents need to reach their kids when plans change, like if practice gets canceled or someone gets sick. If there was a full ban, a parent couldn't text their kid in an emergency, and that isn't fair to families.

Second, Policy 1 teaches us to be responsible. Pretty soon we will have to decide for ourselves when it is okay to use a phone and when it's not. Practicing that at school, with rules and consequences, is better than just taking phones away.

Third, a total ban would be hard to enforce. A lot of kids would still bring phones and just hide them in their backpacks. Then teachers would spend time catching people instead of teaching. A rule students can actually follow is one they will respect more.

Last, free time is when we check messages, figure out rides home, or look up what the homework was.

Policy 1 won't fix everything, but it keeps class focused and still lets students use phones when it makes sense. Please choose it.

Sincerely,
A Student
