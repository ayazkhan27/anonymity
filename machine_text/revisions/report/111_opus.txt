I think drivers should not be allowed to use their phones while driving, except for hands-free GPS and calling 911.

Driving takes all of your attention. My driver's ed teacher told us that at sixty miles per hour a car goes the length of a football field in about four seconds. That is about how long it takes to look down and read a text. In those few seconds a kid could run into the street or the cars ahead could stop. Distracted driving causes thousands of crashes every year, and phones are one of the biggest reasons.

Some people say hands-free calling fixes this. It does help, but your brain is still on the call. If you are arguing with your mom on speaker, you are thinking about her and not the road. Texting, scrolling, and watching videos are a lot worse because they take your eyes, your hands, and your mind off driving at the same time.

To be fair, phones can be really useful in a car. GPS helps people find their way, and calling 911 after an accident can get someone help fast. Those uses should stay legal as long as the phone is mounted and you use your voice.

Everything else can wait until you park.
