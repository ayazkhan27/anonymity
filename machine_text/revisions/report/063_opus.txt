Dear Principal,

I am writing because I don't think you should make students have a B average to play sports or be in activities. I get that you want us to care about our grades, but I think this rule would hurt the kids it's supposed to help.

First, a lot of students at our school have a C average. A C is still passing. If all those kids get kicked off teams and clubs, a big part of the school loses the thing that makes them want to come to school. For a lot of my friends, practice after school is the reason they show up every day and stay out of trouble.

Second, activities teach you stuff you can't learn in class. On a team you learn how to work with other people, manage your time, and keep going after you lose a game. In band or debate club you learn discipline and how to be confident. Taking that away from a C student won't make them a better student. It might just make them give up.

Last, there is a better way to get what you want. Instead of a B rule, the school could keep the C rule and have study hall or tutoring for players whose grades go down. Coaches could check grades every few weeks and help kids who are struggling.

Please let students who are passing keep doing activities. Help them instead of shutting them out.

Sincerely,
A Concerned Student
