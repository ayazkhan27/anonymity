Dear Principal,

I am writing to ask you to pick Policy 1, which lets students bring phones to school and use them at lunch and in other free times, as long as they are turned off during class. I think this way keeps learning safe and it is fair to students too.

First, a full ban would be hard to enforce. Almost every kid has a phone, and lots of them would just hide them in their backpacks or pockets. Teachers wouldn't be teaching, they would be searching for phones and handing out detentions. With Policy 1 the rule is simple. Phones are off in class and we can use them outside of it. Students don't follow rules they think are dumb, but they do follow ones that make sense.

Second, phones help with safety. A lot of us use them to text our parents after practice, set up a ride, or report an emergency. If someone feels sick or the bus is late, one call can fix it. A total ban cuts that off for the whole day, and I don't think parents who worry about their kids would be happy about that.

Third, we need time to relax at lunch. Texting a friend or listening to music helps me come back to class calm. When students get a little trust, they usually act more responsible. Policy 1 also teaches us to handle technology, which we will have to do after we graduate.

Some people say phones distract students. That is true, and it is the reason Policy 1 says they must be off in class. It won't make every student behave, and some will probably try to sneak a text. If a student breaks the rule, the school can take the phone for the day, so the rule has a real consequence.

I still think Policy 1 is the better choice. It keeps classrooms focused and lets students reach their families. It isn't perfect, but a full ban would cause more problems than it solves. Thank you for reading my letter.

Sincerely,
A Concerned Student
