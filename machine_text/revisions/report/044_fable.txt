Last winter my cousin broke her leg and missed three weeks of school. When she came back she was behind in every class. If her school had put lessons online, she could have followed along from the couch. I think students should be able to attend class from home, as long as it is a choice and not a replacement for school.

The main reason is that stuff happens that keeps kids out of the building. Students get sick, families move, the bus doesn't show up, or snow closes the roads. With a video class, a kid with a small fever could still hear the teacher and not give it to thirty other people.

Learning from home also fits some students better. A few people in my math class never raise their hand because the room is loud, but they would type a question in a chat box. A recorded lesson can be paused and played again. A teacher in person can't do that when twenty other kids are waiting.

I know it isn't perfect. At home your phone is right there, and not every family has fast internet or a quiet room. Students also need friends, and you can't really make friends through a screen. That is why I wouldn't want online classes every day.

Still, I think schools should give us the choice. A school that offers both lets students keep learning on the days they can't be there in person.
