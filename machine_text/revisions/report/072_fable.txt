Dear Principal,

I am writing about the idea to make students have a B average to play sports or do other activities. I get why you would want that, because school is supposed to be about learning first. But I don't think it is a good idea, and I think you should keep the rule the way it is now.

First of all, a lot of kids at our school have a C average. A C is not a failing grade. It means you passed. If the rule changed, those kids couldn't do band, soccer, drama, yearbook or robotics anymore. Some teams wouldn't even have enough players. Our drama club is already small, so it might not be able to put on a play at all.

Also I don't think the rule would make grades go up. For a lot of kids, a sport or a club is the reason they show up on time and don't get in trouble. My cousin was a C student and he was on the wrestling team. His coach looked at his report card every two weeks, so he never let his grades get worse. If they kicked him off the team he would not have used that time to study. He would have just been at home with nobody checking on him.

Activities teach you stuff too. Somebody who isn't good at algebra could be really good at running the stage crew or playing defense. It isn't fair to take away the one thing they are good at. When you feel good about something, you try harder in class too.

I think there is a better way. Keep the C rule, but if somebody's grades drop during the season, make them go to a study hall or tutoring. The coaches and club teachers could make sure they go. That way kids would have to work on their grades, but they wouldn't lose the thing that makes them want to come to school.

Thank you for asking us what we think before you decide.

Sincerely,
A concerned student
