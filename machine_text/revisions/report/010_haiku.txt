Dear Principal,

I think Policy 1 is the better choice. Students should be allowed to bring phones to school and use them at lunch and other free times, as long as phones stay off during class.

First, phones keep students safe. If a bus is late or there is a family emergency, parents can reach their kids right away without someone having to walk to the front office.

Second, a total ban is hard to enforce. Students who really want their phones will hide them, and teachers will spend class time looking for devices. A rule that allows phones at free times but bans them in class is easier to understand and follow.

Third, lunch and breaks are when students relax and talk with friends. Checking messages or calling family during these times is a fair use of personal time, and it shows students that the school trusts them.

Finally, Policy 1 teaches responsibility. Students learn when a phone is okay to use and when it is not. That is a skill they will need in high school, college, and at work. A full ban takes away the chance to practice it.

For these reasons, I ask that you adopt Policy 1, with strict enforcement during class time.

Respectfully,
A Student
