Dear Principal,

I think Policy 1 is the better choice. Students should be able to bring phones and use them at lunch and during free time, as long as they are off in class.

My first reason is safety. If the bus breaks down or practice runs late, a student can't just wait around for the front office to call home. With a total ban, the call has to go through the office, and that is slow. A text to a parent takes about ten seconds.

My second reason is that a full ban won't work very well. Lots of kids will keep their phones in their backpacks anyway, and teachers will spend class time looking for them instead of teaching. Policy 1 is a rule students can actually follow. Phones are off in class and not a problem the rest of the day. I don't think many people would argue with that.

Third, lunch and free time are the only breaks we get. Checking a message from home or looking up a homework question isn't hurting anybody. It also gives you a minute to relax before the next class.

Last, students are going to have phones for the rest of their lives. School isn't a bad place to learn how to leave one alone when you need to.

So I think the school should pick Policy 1, though it will not fix every problem.

Sincerely,
A concerned student
