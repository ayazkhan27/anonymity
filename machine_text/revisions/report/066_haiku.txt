Dear Principal,

I am writing to share my opinion about the new rule that would make students need at least a B average to play sports or be in other activities. I get why the school wants this. We all want students to do well in class. But I think this rule would do more harm than good. Please read my reasons before you decide.

First, a lot of students right now have a C average. This rule would kick them off the teams and clubs that keep them motivated. For many of us, sports and clubs are the reason we come to school at all. Practice gives us a place to belong, goals that are not just tests, and coaches who notice when we are doing good things. If we lose those, we will probably care less about school, not more. A kid who is bad at math does not need to be pulled off the team that gives him a reason to get up in the morning.

Second, activities teach things grades do not measure. On the field, in the band room, or on the debate team, students learn to manage time, work with people who are different from them, and bounce back after losing. Those lessons carry into class. A lot of kids who play sports plan their homework around practice, and that routine usually helps their grades over time. Taking away the activity takes away the structure that could help a struggling student get better.

Third, this rule would hit the students who need help most. Kids with C averages are often not lazy. Some have jobs to help their families, some have problems at home, and some just learn differently. Kicking them out of activities punishes them for their situation and does not help them at all. If the goal is better grades, the school should offer tutoring, study halls, or extra time with teachers. Then those students can raise their grades and still stay with the activities they like.

Finally, a grade rule could make activities less fair and less safe for everyone. Coaches and advisors would spend their time on eligibility paperwork instead of teaching and mentoring. Students who lose eligibility in the middle of a season could get pulled from a team right when they need it most. It would be better to have a system where students who fall behind get early warnings, a plan to improve, and help along the way.

So I respectfully ask you not to adopt the B average requirement. I think students should be encouraged to stay in activities, and the school should spend its money on helping every student raise their grades. I would be glad to join any discussion or student survey that could help us find a better solution.

Thank you for asking for our opinions and for thinking about this important question.

Respectfully,

A Concerned Student
