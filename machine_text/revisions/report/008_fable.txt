Dear Principal,

I heard you are thinking about changing the cell phone policy. I think you should pick Policy 1, where we can bring phones and use them at lunch and free time but they have to be off in class.

My first reason is safety. A lot of kids walk home or ride the city bus or stay late for practice. Last winter my basketball practice ended forty minutes early because the heater in the gym broke. I called my mom and she came and got me. If I didn't have my phone I would of been standing outside in the cold till 5:30. People say just use the office phone, but the office is already locked by the time most practices get out.

My second reason is that a total ban would not work. Kids are going to bring phones anyway and hide them in their backpacks and lockers and hoodie pockets. Then teachers have to waste time hunting for phones and writing people up instead of teaching. Also if almost everybody breaks a rule every single day, it starts to seem like rules don't matter. Policy 1 is something most of us would actually follow.

Third, Policy 1 still protects class, which is the whole point of having a phone rule. Phones are off during lessons so nobody is texting under the desk or bothering the person next to them. If somebody breaks it, the teacher takes the phone until the end of the day. Nobody likes that but it's fair and we would get it.

Last, lunch and free periods are the only break we get. Checking a text or listening to music for twenty minutes is not going to wreck anyone's grades. I will admit some kids will stare at their phone the whole lunch and not talk to anybody, and that is a problem. But a ban doesn't fix that either, it just makes them sneak it.

So I am asking you to go with Policy 1. It is not perfect, and some people will still break it, but it has less problems than banning phones.

Sincerely,
A student
