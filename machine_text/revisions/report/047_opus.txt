Learning From Home Should Be a Choice

When most people think of school they think of desks in rows, a whiteboard, and a hallway packed with kids between classes. For a lot of students that works fine. For some students though, it is a problem they have to deal with every single day. I think students should have the option to take classes from home through online or video classes, as long as it is a choice and not something everyone is forced to do.

The biggest reason is that some students lives don't fit a normal school schedule. Some teenagers have part-time jobs to help their family pay rent. Others have to watch their little brothers and sisters or take care of a sick grandparent. A student athlete who travels for tournaments can miss three or four days in a row. For these students, learning from home is not just a nice extra, it is the difference between keeping up and falling behind. If they can watch a recorded lesson or join a live video class, they are learning on a day they would have just been marked absent.

Online classes also help students who have health problems or disabilities. If a student is recovering from surgery or has a weak immune system, they might not be able to come to school for weeks. Instead of losing a whole semester, they could log on and still be part of the class. Students with bad anxiety might also do better at home where it is calmer, so they can focus on the actual work instead of how stressed out they feel in a loud classroom.

Another good thing is that students could take classes their school doesn't offer. A small high school out in the country might not have enough kids to hire a teacher for AP Physics or Japanese. With video classes, a student there could join a class at a different school or even a college. That gives them chances they wouldn't have just because of where they live.

Some people say students at home will get distracted, and that they will miss out on the social part of school like friends, clubs, and learning how to work with people in person. I agree these are real problems. Not every student can make themselves do work alone, and not every house has a quiet place or good wifi. But these are reasons to plan online learning carefully, not reasons to get rid of it. Teachers can check in with students at home, schools can lend out laptops and hotspots, and students can still come in for clubs and sports when they are able to.

In conclusion, every student is different, and one kind of school is never going to work for everybody. Letting students choose to learn from home respects what they are going through and keeps more of them in school. Having both options doesn't make the classroom worse. It just makes sure nobody gets left out of it.
