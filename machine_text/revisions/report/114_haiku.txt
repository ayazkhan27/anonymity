Cell phones are part of almost everyone's daily life, so many people check them without thinking, even while driving. I believe drivers should not be allowed to use cell phones at all while they are behind the wheel. Driving takes full attention, and a phone takes some of that attention away in ways people often do not notice.

Even hands-free calls make the driver listen, talk, and think about a conversation at the same time. Studies on distracted driving have shown that this mental load slows reaction times and makes drivers less aware of traffic, pedestrians, and road conditions. Texting and scrolling are even worse, because they take the driver's eyes off the road and one hand off the wheel at once. Keeping both eyes and both hands on the road is the safest habit a driver can have.

Some people say a full ban is unrealistic and that careful drivers can handle a quick look at a map or a message. However, distraction is hard to predict, and a single second of lost attention at highway speed can make the difference between a safe stop and a crash. Partial laws also help very little, since police cannot easily tell whether someone is dialing, listening, or reading.

A clear and complete ban gives drivers one simple rule that is easy to follow and easy to enforce. Phones can wait until the car is parked, and most messages can wait too. A short wait is a small price to pay for safer roads, and it protects drivers, passengers, and walkers alike.
