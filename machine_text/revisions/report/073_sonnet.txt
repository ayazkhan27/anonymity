Dear Principal,

I am writing about the idea of making students have a B average to play sports or join clubs. I know you want kids to get better grades, but I think this rule would hurt a lot of students and wouldn't really work. Please don't pass it.

First, the students with a C average are often the ones who need activities the most. Someone who is bad at math might be great at soccer or really into drama club. Those things give kids a reason to come to school. If we take them away, we might take away the only thing keeping some kids going. If a student feels like they are failing at everything in school, they will probably give up instead of trying harder.

Second, this rule would affect way more students than you think. You said a lot of students have a C average. That means a big part of our school could not be on teams, in clubs, or in band. Teams might not have enough players, and some clubs could get so small they just disappear. The school would lose its spirit, and students would lose friends and memories they will remember long after they forget a chemistry quiz.

Third, activities teach things that grades don't show. Being on a team teaches you to work with others and to stick with something. With practices and games, you have to manage your time. A lot of us do our homework right after practice because we know we don't have much time. Kids who have nothing to do after school usually fall behind anyway, or they just spend the time on their phones or getting in trouble. Activities keep students busy in a good way with adults watching.

Fourth, a B average is hard for some students for reasons they can't control. Some kids have jobs, take care of younger brothers and sisters, or have learning problems that make school harder. Kicking them out of activities doesn't seem fair. Their grades might show their situation more than how hard they try.

I do agree that grades are important, and I think there are better ways to protect them. The school could keep a minimum like a C average instead of a B. If students fall under it, they could be put on probation and get free tutoring or a supervised study hall. That helps students instead of shutting them out. Coaches and advisors could also check grades every few weeks so problems get caught early.

In conclusion, a B average rule would take away motivation, make our teams and clubs smaller, and be unfair to students who are struggling. Support and a reasonable minimum would do more to raise grades. Thank you for taking the time to think about what students have to say.

Sincerely,
A Concerned Student
