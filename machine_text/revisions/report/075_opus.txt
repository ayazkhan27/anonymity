Dear Principal,

I am writing to ask you not to make students have a B average to play sports or be in other activities. I get that you want kids to try harder in class, but I think this rule would hurt the same students it is supposed to help.

First, a lot of students at our school have a C average. A C is still passing. If those kids get kicked off teams and clubs, a big part of the school would lose something that keeps them coming. For some of my friends, practice after school is the main reason they even show up in the morning.

Second, activities teach you stuff a classroom can't. On the soccer team I learned how to manage my time, take criticism from my coach, and work with people I didn't always like. That matters just as much as a test score.

Last, taking activities away won't make grades go up by itself. A better idea would be to make students below a C go to tutoring or study hall, so they get help instead of getting punished.

Please keep activities open to every student who is passing.

Sincerely,
A concerned student
