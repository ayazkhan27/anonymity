I think drivers should not be allowed to hold their phone while the car is moving, but I don't think the law should ban phones in the car completely. Pretty much everyone I know has a phone and pretty much everyone I know has looked at it while driving. I've done it too. It feels normal because everybody does it, and I think that's why people don't see it as dangerous.

The main reason to ban holding a phone is that you need your eyes and your hands to drive and the phone takes both. Reading one text only takes a few seconds, but on the highway a car goes about the length of a football field in that time. So you basically drove a whole football field without looking. Your car doesn't have to do anything wrong for that to be bad. A kid could step off the curb or the truck in front of you could hit its brakes. Safety agencies say thousands of people die every year because of distracted driving, and a lot of them weren't the person on the phone. To me that is the biggest reason. If somebody doesn't wear a seat belt they are mostly risking their own life. If somebody texts going forty miles an hour they are risking people who never got a choice about it.

Some people say they are good enough at driving to do both. I doubt that. People are bad at telling how much attention they are really paying, and the drivers who feel the most sure of themselves are a lot of the time the ones drifting into the other lane. Also the only way you find out you were wrong is by crashing, which is too late.

But banning every use of a phone would be going too far. Nobody uses paper maps anymore, and a voice telling me to turn left in half a mile is safer than trying to unfold a map on the steering wheel. You also need a phone to call for help if your car breaks down or to report an accident. Hands-free calls aren't totally safe because a conversation still takes your mind off the road. They do let you keep your eyes up and your hands on the wheel though, and it's hard to say how that is different from talking to someone in the passenger seat. If the law banned all of that, most people would just ignore it, and once people get used to ignoring one law it's easier for them to ignore the next one.

So the rule should be about touching the phone. Set up the directions before you leave the driveway, let the phone talk, and keep it somewhere you can't reach until you're parked. A text can wait ten minutes.
