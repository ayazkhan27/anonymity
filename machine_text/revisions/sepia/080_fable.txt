Our principal wants to make every student join at least one extracurricular activity. I get why, because clubs and sports are a lot of kids' favorite part of school. But I don't think it should be required. I think it would hurt the kids it's supposed to help and make the clubs worse too.

First, a lot of students just don't have time. My friend Marcus gets out of school at 2:45 and he has to be at his job at the grocery store by 3:30. He does that four days a week, and the money helps his mom pay rent. Other kids have to watch their little brothers and sisters until a parent gets home. Some ride a bus that leaves right after the last bell. For them a required activity is just one more thing to fail at, and the busiest families would get punished the most.

Also, when you force people to join something they make it worse for everybody. I was in band for two years. By the second rehearsal you could tell who wanted to be there and who got signed up by their parents. The ones who didn't want to be there talked during practice and didn't show up to concerts. Imagine a soccer team where a third of the players only joined because of a rule. The kids who actually care would have to drag everybody else along.

Plenty of students also do good things outside of school that the school never sees. A girl in my class takes karate at a studio downtown. Another girl volunteers at her church's food pantry every Saturday. If only school activities count, that's like telling them what they do doesn't matter.

I think the school should make it easier to join instead of making it a rule. They could have an activity fair in September, run a late bus twice a week, and let some clubs meet at lunch. Kids who join because they found something they like will stay and work hard. Kids who got ordered to join will just count the days until they can quit.
