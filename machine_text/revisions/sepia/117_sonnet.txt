Almost everybody has a cell phone these days, and most people check theirs all the time. Because of that, I think people forget how dangerous it is to use one in a car. A car weighs about two tons and it's going fast, so whoever is driving needs to be paying attention. That's why this matters. I believe drivers should not use their cell phones at all while they are driving.

The main reason is how our brains work. A lot of people think they can multitask, but studies on divided attention say that's not really true. The brain jumps back and forth between things, and every time it jumps you lose a little bit of awareness. If you look at a text for five seconds while going highway speed, you travel the length of a football field without looking at the road. That is not safe, and being an experienced driver doesn't change it.

Some people say hands-free calling is okay because both hands stay on the wheel. But the problem isn't just your hands. Talking on the phone uses your mind too. Studies of drivers on hands-free calls found they reacted slower to hazards and missed things right in front of them, like tunnel vision. You can be looking at the road and still not see it. Also, one rule for everything is easier to understand and enforce than a half rule. If all phone use is banned, a police officer or passenger doesn't have to decide which kind counts.

Other people say you need your phone for directions or emergencies. Those are fair points, but there are easy answers. You can type in the route before you start the car, or put the phone in a mount and let it speak the directions without touching it. If there's an emergency, you can pull over somewhere safe and call. Waiting a few minutes to stop costs almost nothing next to what a crash costs.

Driving also isn't private. A distracted driver puts passengers, pedestrians, bikers, and other drivers in danger, and they never agreed to take that risk. Their safety is not the driver's to gamble with. We already have rules against things that threaten other people on the road, like drunk driving and running red lights. Phone use belongs with those.

I know it's hard to break a habit you've had for a long time, but it can be done. Drivers can turn off notifications, use do-not-disturb mode, or put the phone somewhere they can't reach it. Every message can wait until the car is parked. A reply isn't worth a life. So the rule should be simple: phones stay down while the car is moving.
