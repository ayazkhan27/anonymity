Dear Principal,

I am a student and I am writing about the new rule that says we can't play sports or do other activities unless we have at least a B average. I don't agree with this rule, and here is why.

First of all, a C average is not failing. Kids with a C are passing all their classes. That is okay. Taking away the one thing they like about school because they didn't get a B seems like too much.

Also, activities can actually help people do better in school. Say someone is on the track team. They have practice, so they don't have as much free time, and they have to get their homework done on time. Or take someone in band or drama. Maybe that is the first time they feel like they belong at school. That is a reason to come and try. If you take activities away from the kids who are struggling the most, their grades might go down instead of up.

The rule is also not fair. Some students have a job after school. Some take care of their little brothers and sisters. Some have a learning problem, so getting a B is a lot harder for them. Maybe a sport is the one place they do well. If we cut them off, it tells them they don't belong here.

I do know grades are important, and I get why you want us to care about them. But I think there is a better way. What if the rule was a C average instead, and kids who fall below it got free tutoring and a study hall? That would help us instead of kicking us out.

Thank you for listening to what students think.

Sincerely,
A concerned student
