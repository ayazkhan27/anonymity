Dear Principal,

I think Policy 1 is better. Students should be able to bring their phones and use them at lunch and during free time, but they have to be off in class.

One reason is safety. If the bus breaks down or practice runs late, a kid can call their parents right away. Same if something happens at home. With a full ban the call has to go through the front office, and that takes time when you don't have any.

Also, a total ban is hard to enforce. A lot of students will just hide their phones in their backpacks anyway. Then teachers spend class time looking for them instead of teaching. Policy 1 is a rule people can actually follow, phones off in class and okay the rest of the day. People usually follow a rule more when it seems fair.

Lunch and free time are when we need a break. Reading a text from home, putting on music, or looking up a homework question helps us relax, so we are ready to focus when class starts again.

Last, knowing how to handle a phone is something we will need our whole lives. School is a fine place to practice that before we're on our own.

So I hope you pick Policy 1.

Sincerely,
A concerned student
