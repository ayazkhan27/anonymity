Dear Principal,

I think you should not make students have a B average to play sports or be in clubs. I get that you want kids to try harder in school, but I think this rule would hurt the kids who need help the most.

First of all, alot of students at our school have a C average, and a C is still passing. If all of them get kicked off teams and out of clubs, a big part of the school loses the thing that keeps them coming. Some of my friends honestly only come to school in the morning because they have practice after.

Also, you learn stuff in sports that you dont learn in class. When I was on the soccer team I had to figure out how to get my homework done around practice, listen when my coach yelled at me, and get along with people I didn't even like. That is just as important as a test grade.

Last, taking away sports won't make grades go up by itself. It would be better to make kids under a C go to tutoring or study hall so they actually get help and not just get punished.

Please let everyone who is passing stay in activities.

Sincerely,
A concerned student
