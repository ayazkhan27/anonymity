Dear Principal,

I heard you might make a rule that students need a B average to play sports or be in clubs. I don't think you should do this. I get that you want us to care about our grades but I think this rule would end up hurting the kids it's supposed to help.

My first reason is that a lot of kids at our school have C's. A C is still passing. If all those kids get kicked off their teams and clubs, that's a big part of the school losing the thing that gets them to come to school. Some of my friends only show up every day because they have practice after. It also keeps them out of trouble after school.

Another reason is you learn stuff in activities that you don't really learn in class. On a team you have to work with other people and figure out your time and keep trying even when you lose. In band or debate you learn discipline and to be more confident. If you take that away from a kid with a C they're not going to suddenly be a better student. They might just give up.

Also I think there is a better way to do this. You could keep the rule at a C and then have study hall or tutoring for players whose grades go down. Coaches could check grades every couple weeks and help the kids who are struggling.

So please let kids who are passing stay in activities. Help them instead of kicking them out.

Sincerely,
A Concerned Student
