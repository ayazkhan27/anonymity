Phones and Driving

Almost everybody I know has a phone, and almost everybody I know has looked at it while driving. I have too. One time I looked down at a text while I was stopped at a red light, and when I looked back up the light had been green long enough that the driver behind me honked. Nothing happened, but it showed me how fast my attention can leave the road. So I think drivers shouldn't be allowed to hold or handle a phone while the car is moving. I wouldn't ban every single use though.

The main reason is that driving takes your eyes, your hands and your attention, and a phone takes all of them at once. Reading a text takes about five seconds. On the highway a car goes the length of a football field in that time. If you're typing you aren't steering well or checking your mirrors, and you aren't ready to brake. A lot of the time the person who pays for it isn't even the driver. It's a kid crossing the street or somebody on a bike, or a family in the next lane. The text could have waited ten minutes, but you can't undo an injury.

Some people say they're good enough to do both. I doubt it. Nobody feels distracted while they're distracted, and that's why the habit is so dangerous. Being confident doesn't mean you're actually able to do it, and the law shouldn't depend on what each driver thinks of himself.

I still don't think a total ban makes sense. People use their phones as maps now. A phone on a mount that says the directions out loud is safer than a paper map on the passenger seat, and it's safer than a lost driver making sudden turns. A driver should also be able to call for help in an emergency. Hands-free calls are harder to judge because a conversation still pulls your mind away from traffic. But a short call is closer to talking with a passenger than it is to texting.

So this is the rule I would support. Set the route before you leave, mount the phone, and don't touch it again until the car is parked. Navigation and emergency calls stay legal. Texting, scrolling and holding the phone don't. That asks very little of drivers and it protects everyone who shares the road with them.
