I think drivers should not be allowed to use their phones while driving. The only exception should be hands-free GPS and calling 911.

When you drive you need to pay attention the whole time. At sixty miles per hour a car goes about the length of a football field in four seconds, and that's about how long it takes to look down and read a text. A kid could run into the street in that time, or the car in front of you could slam on its brakes. Distracted driving kills thousands of people every year, and phones are one of the biggest reasons.

Some people say hands-free calling fixes this. It does help some, but your mind is still on the call. If you're arguing with someone on the phone you aren't really thinking about the road. Texting, scrolling, and watching videos are even worse because your eyes, your hands, and your brain are all off of driving.

I know phones can be useful in a car. GPS keeps you from getting lost, and if there's an accident calling 911 could save someone's life. Those should still be legal as long as the phone is mounted and you use your voice.

Anything else can wait until you get where you're going.
