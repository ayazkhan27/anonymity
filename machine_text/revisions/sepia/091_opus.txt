I agree with my principal that every student should have to do at least one extracurricular activity. In class we learn facts, but in activities you learn how to get along with people, how to use your time, and what you actually like.

The first reason is teamwork. If you play soccer you figure out pretty fast that you can't win by yourself. You have to pass and trust the other players. Yearbook is kind of the same way, because the writers and photographers and editors all have to finish on time or there is no yearbook. Adults need to work with people at their jobs too, so this matters.

Another reason is time management. I joined debate club and we had practice three days a week after school. I was scared my grades would go down. They didn't. I started planning out my homework and I stopped wasting my whole night. Being busy made me focus more.

Also activities help kids find out what they're into. A shy kid could join drama and find out they're good at acting. Someone on student council might decide she wants to be a lawyer.

Some people will say students are busy enough already. But it's only one activity, and you get a lot out of it. That is why I agree with the principal.
