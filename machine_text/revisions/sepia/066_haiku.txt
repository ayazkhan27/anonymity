Dear Principal,

I am writing to share my opinion about the new policy that would make students need at least a B average before they can play sports or do other activities. I get why the school wants this. We all want kids to do well in class. But I think this policy would cause more problems than it fixes. Please read my reasons before you decide.

First, a lot of students right now have a C average. If this policy happens, they would get kicked off their teams and out of their clubs. For a lot of us, sports and clubs are the only reason we even come to school. Practice gives us a place where we belong. It gives us goals that are not just tests, and it gives us adults who notice when we do good stuff. If we lose all of that, we will probably care less about school, not more. A kid who is bad at math does not need to get pulled off the team that makes him want to get up in the morning.

Second, activities teach stuff that grades don't measure. On the field, in band, or on the debate team, you learn how to manage your time, how to work with people who are different from you, and how to deal with losing. Those things help in class too. A lot of kids who play sports have to plan their homework around practice, and that usually helps their grades over time. If you take away the activity, you take away the thing that could help a struggling kid get better.

Third, this policy would hit the kids who need help the most. Kids with a C average are not always lazy. Some of them have jobs to help their families. Some have problems at home. Some just learn differently than the way teachers teach. Kicking them out of activities punishes them for things they can't control and does not help them at all. If the school really wants better grades, it should give these students tutoring, study halls, or extra time with teachers. That way they can bring up their grades and still stay with the stuff they like.

Finally, a grade rule would make activities less fair and less safe for everybody. Coaches and advisors would have to spend their time on eligibility paperwork instead of teaching and helping kids. A student could lose eligibility in the middle of the season and get pulled off the team right when they need it most. It would be better to have a system where kids who start falling behind get early warnings, a plan to improve, and help along the way.

So I respectfully ask you not to adopt the B average requirement. I think students should be encouraged to stay in activities, and the school should spend its effort on helping every student raise their grades. I would be happy to join any discussion or student survey that could help find a better answer.

Thank you for asking students what we think and for thinking about this important question.

Respectfully,

A Concerned Student
