Dear Principal,

I think you should pick Policy 1. Students would be allowed to bring their phones to school and use them at lunch and during free time, but they would have to keep them off in class.

The first reason is safety. Sometimes plans change or somebody gets sick, or there could be an emergency, and parents need to be able to reach their kids. If phones are banned completely then families can't get in touch during the school day, which is when they might need to the most.

Another reason is it teaches responsibility. When we get older nobody is going to tell us when we can use our phones, we have to figure that out ourselves. If we practice at school with clear rules and consequences we will be more ready than if you just take the phones away.

Also a total ban would be really hard to enforce. Lots of kids would just bring their phones anyway and hide them, and then teachers and students would be fighting about it more. A fair rule is something people will actually follow.

Last, free time is a good time to check messages, set up rides, or look up homework.

Policy 1 keeps class focused and still respects what students need. Please choose it.

Sincerely,
A Concerned Student
