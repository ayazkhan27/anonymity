Dear Principal,

I am writing to argue against the proposed policy that would require at least a B average for students to take part in sports and other activities. I get that the school wants kids to try harder in class. But this rule would hurt the students it is supposed to help.

First, a lot of kids who struggle in class finally feel like they belong on a team or in a club. That gives them a reason to come to school and stay involved. It also gives them friends and adults who support them. If we take that away, kids with C averages could check out emotionally, and their grades could fall even more.

Second, sports and activities teach stuff that grades don't measure. Teamwork, discipline, leadership, and time management are learned through practice and competition. Students who get barred from these lose the chance to grow in ways that will really matter after graduation.

Finally, the policy punishes kids for struggling instead of helping them get better. Tutoring, study hall, or mentoring would work much better for students who are falling behind. They could raise their grades and still keep the activities that motivate them.

For these reasons, I'm asking the school to keep sports and activities open to all students.

Sincerely,
A Concerned Student
