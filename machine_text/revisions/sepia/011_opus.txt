Dear Principal,

I heard you are thinking about changing the cell phone policy and I think you should pick Policy 1. Students should be able to bring their phones to school and use them at lunch and during free time, as long as they are off in class.

My first reason is safety. A lot of parents want to be able to get ahold of their kids if there is an emergency. Also kids need to call home sometimes, like if they feel sick or they missed the bus or their plans changed after school. If something really bad happened at school a student with a phone could call their family or 911 right away. If you ban phones totally, everybody loses that just because a few kids get distracted.

My second reason is phones help us keep our lives together. Lots of students have jobs or sports practice or little brothers and sisters to pick up. If I send my coach or my mom a quick text at lunch I can fix a schedule problem before it gets worse. My phone also has my calendar and reminders and school apps on it, and that helps me stay organized.

Third, Policy 1 teaches us to be responsible. When we graduate nobody is going to take our phones away at our job or at college. We will have to decide on our own when it's ok to use them. If we get to practice that now with clear rules for class time, it will help us more than a total ban where we don't even get a choice.

Some people think students will break the rule and use their phones in class anyway. I get that, but the way to fix it is to enforce the rule, not punish every student. Teachers could take the phone from anyone who uses it in class, and if someone keeps doing it they could lose the privilege. I think most kids would follow the rule if they knew they could check their phone at lunch.

Policy 2 would also be really hard to enforce and kids would probably just hide their phones. Policy 1 is fair and it actually makes sense. I hope you choose it.

Sincerely,
A Concerned Student
