I think students should be allowed to go to class from home if they want to. But it should be an option, not a replacement for regular school. If kids get to choose, the ones who do better at home can stay home and the ones who need a classroom can still go.

My first reason is that some students can't always get to school. Some live really far away, some have an illness that lasts a long time, and some have to watch their little brothers or sisters while their parents are at work. For these students missing one day can turn into missing a whole week, and after that it's hard to ever catch up. If they could log into a live class on video they would hear the same lesson and get to ask questions just like everyone else. Like if a girl had surgery, she could keep doing algebra instead of coming back a month later to stuff she never learned.

My second reason is that everybody learns differently. Some students get anxious in a crowded room or get distracted by the people around them, and they can focus better somewhere quiet. Also if the lessons are recorded you can pause and rewatch a part you didn't get. You can't do that when the teacher is talking in front of the class.

I know distance learning has problems too. Not every family has good internet or a quiet place to work, and some kids need a teacher right there or they won't do anything. Being at home all day can also get lonely, and a lot of what you learn at school comes from working with other people. That's why it should be a choice and not required. Schools should also lend out laptops and check on the kids learning from home a lot.

If schools do it right, distance learning gives more students a fair chance to keep learning.
