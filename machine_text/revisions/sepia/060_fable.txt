Dear Principal,

I am writing about the idea that students would need a B average to play sports or do activities. I don't think you should do it.

A lot of kids here have a C average and they aren't lazy. Some kids study for hours and still get a C in math or science. Kicking them off the team won't make that C a B. It just takes away the one thing at school they look forward to.

Sports also keep kids coming to school. My cousin told me he only showed up to first period because his coach checked attendance. If kids like him can't go to practice, they have less reason to come, so their grades could get even worse.

You also learn stuff in activities. You have to be on time and work with people you don't like. You learn to lose without quitting.

It would be more fair to keep the C rule and give tutoring to players who are slipping. Please let us keep playing.

Sincerely,
A student
