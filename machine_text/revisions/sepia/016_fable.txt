Dear Principal Harmon,

I heard you might change the cell phone policy. I think you should pick Policy 1, where we can use phones at lunch and free time but they stay off in class.

My first reason is getting home. A lot of us ride the bus or walk, or stay late for practice. Last month my rehearsal got out forty minutes early. Without my phone I would have been standing outside in the dark until my mom's shift ended. A ban doesn't stop stuff like that from happening. It just makes every kid go to the front office, and the office is already busy.

Also, I don't think a total ban would even work. Kids will bring phones anyway. They'll hide them in backpacks and bathrooms, and teachers will waste class time searching when they should be teaching. Policy 1 is fair, so most kids would actually follow it. If I can check my phone at lunch, I don't need to sneak it out in math.

Policy 1 also teaches us something. In a few years we'll have jobs and nobody is going to take our phones away. We'll have to decide on our own when to put them down. School is a good place to practice that because there are clear rules and real consequences.

I know you are worried about distraction, and I am too. That's why phones should be all the way off in class, not just on silent, and taken away from anyone who breaks the rule.

Please choose Policy 1. It protects class time and doesn't treat every student like a problem.

Sincerely,
An 8th grade student
