Dear Principal,

I heard you are thinking about making students have a B average to play sports or do other activities. I get why. School is supposed to be about learning first. But I think this rule would hurt a lot more kids than it helps, so I am asking you to keep the rule we have now.

A lot of students at our school have a C average. A C is still passing. The school is the one that decided a C is good enough to pass, so those kids are doing what they were asked to do. If the rule changes they would lose band, soccer, drama, yearbook and robotics club all at the same time. Then the teams get smaller, and some clubs might not even have enough people to keep going.

I also don't think the rule would make grades go up. For a lot of kids an activity is the reason they show up on time and stay out of trouble. My cousin was a C student and he was on the wrestling team. His coach checked his report card every two weeks, and that is why his grades didn't get any worse. If he got cut from the team he would not have used that time to study. He would have just been at home with nobody checking on him at all.

Activities teach you things too. Somebody who is bad at algebra might be the best in the whole school at running stage crew or leading the defense. Those kids should have one place where they are good at something. A lot of times that confidence helps them in class after.

I think there is a fairer way to do this. Keep the C rule, but if somebody's grades drop during the season they have to go to a study hall or tutoring. Coaches and club advisers could help make sure they go. That way students have to work harder but they don't lose the thing that keeps them connected to school.

Thank you for asking us what we think before you decide.

Sincerely,
A concerned student
