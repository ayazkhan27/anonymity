Dear Principal,

I think Policy 1 is the better choice. Students should be allowed to bring phones to school and use them at lunch and other free times, but phones must stay off during class.

First, phones keep students safe. If a bus is late, something happens at home, or our dismissal plan changes, parents need to reach us. A phone lets them do that without anyone having to go to the front office.

Second, a total ban is hard to enforce. Kids who want their phones will just hide them, and teachers will end up spending class time looking for devices. A clear rule that allows phones at free times but bans them during class is easier to understand and follow.

Third, lunch and breaks are when we rest and hang out with friends. Checking messages, reading the news, or calling family during those times is a normal use of our own time. It also keeps us from feeling like little kids.

Finally, Policy 1 teaches responsibility. We learn when a phone is okay and when it is not. We will need that skill in college and at work, and a full ban takes away the chance to practice it.

So I ask that you adopt Policy 1, with strict enforcement during class time.

Respectfully,
A Student
