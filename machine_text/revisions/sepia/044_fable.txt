Last winter my cousin broke her leg and missed three weeks of school. When she came back she was lost in every class. If her school had online lessons she could have kept up from the couch. So I think students should be able to attend class from home, but only as an option. It shouldn't replace regular school.

The main reason is that stuff happens that keeps kids out of school. Students get sick, families move, the bus breaks down, or it snows and the roads are closed. Right now every one of those is a day you just lose. If there was a video class, a kid with a small fever could still hear the lesson, and he wouldn't be giving his germs to thirty other people either.

It also helps students who learn better by themselves. Some people in my classes are too shy to ask a question when the room is loud, but they would type it in a chat box. And if the lesson is recorded you can pause it and play it again. A teacher can't do that for you when twenty other kids are waiting.

I know there are problems with it. At home it's really easy to get distracted. Not every family has fast internet or a quiet room. Students need friends too, and you can't really make friends through a screen. That's why I wouldn't want online class every single day.

But having the choice doesn't hurt anybody. If a school has both, students can keep learning on the days they can't show up.
