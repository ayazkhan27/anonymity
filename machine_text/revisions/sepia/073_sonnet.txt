Dear Principal,

I'm writing about the idea of making students keep a B average to play sports or be in other activities. I get that you want kids to have better grades, but I think this rule would hurt a lot of students and it wouldn't really fix the problem. Please don't pass it.

The first reason is that a lot of students with a C average are the ones who need activities the most. Say someone is bad at math. They might still feel good about themselves on the soccer field or in drama club. That's the reason some kids come to school at all. If you take that away, you might take away the only thing keeping them going. When a student feels like they fail at everything in school, they're more likely to quit than to study harder.

Second, you said that many students have a C average. That means a big part of our school couldn't be on teams, in clubs, or in band. Teams wouldn't have enough players and some clubs would probably shrink until they're gone. We'd lose school spirit too. Students would lose friends and memories they'll still have long after they forget some chemistry quiz.

Third, activities teach things grades don't show. On a team you learn to work with other people and stick with something. You also have to plan your time. A lot of us do our homework right after practice because we know we don't have many hours. Kids who have nothing to do after school fall behind anyway, or they just sit on their phones or get in trouble. Activities keep them busy in a good way, and there are adults watching.

Fourth, a B is hard for some students and it isn't always their fault. Some have jobs. Some take care of their little brothers and sisters. Some have learning differences that make school harder than it is for everybody else. Kicking them out of activities because of that doesn't seem fair. Their grades might say more about their situation than about how hard they try.

I do agree grades are important, and there are better ways to protect them. The school could keep a minimum, like a C average, instead of a B. If a student drops below it, they could go on probation and get free tutoring or a supervised study hall. That helps them instead of locking them out. Coaches and advisors could also check on grades every so often so problems get caught early.

So in the end, a B average rule would take away motivation, make our teams and clubs smaller, and be unfair to students who are struggling. Support and a reasonable minimum would do more to bring grades up. Thank you for listening to what students think.

Sincerely,
A Concerned Student
