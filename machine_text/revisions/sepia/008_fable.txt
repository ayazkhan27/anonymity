Dear Principal,

I think you should pick Policy 1, where we can bring our phones to school and use them at lunch and free time as long as they are off during class.

The biggest reason is safety. A lot of kids walk home or take the city bus, and a lot of us stay late for practice. Last winter the heater in the gym broke and my basketball practice ended forty minutes early. I called my mom and she picked me up. Without my phone I would have been standing outside in the cold until five thirty. The office phone doesn't fix this, because the office closes before most practices let out.

Also, I don't think a total ban would even work. Kids are going to bring their phones anyway and hide them in their backpacks or lockers or pockets. Then teachers have to spend their time looking for phones and writing referrals when they should be teaching. And if most students break a rule every single day, it teaches us that rules don't matter. Policy 1 is a rule we can actually follow because it asks for something reasonable.

Policy 1 still protects class time, which is the main thing a phone policy is for. Phones would be off during lessons, so nobody is texting under a desk or distracting the person next to them. If somebody does it anyway, the teacher can take the phone until the end of the day. That's fair and students would understand it.

Lunch and free periods are different because that is our time to relax. Checking a message or listening to music for twenty minutes doesn't hurt anybody's grades. It also gives us practice at deciding when it is okay to use a phone, and we are going to need that in high school and at work.

I hope you choose Policy 1. It keeps us safe, it keeps class focused, and it treats us like people who can be responsible.

Sincerely,
A student
