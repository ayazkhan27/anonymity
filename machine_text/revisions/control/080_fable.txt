Our principal wants every student to join at least one extracurricular activity. I get why he thinks that. Clubs and sports can be the best part of school. But I don't think it should be required, because it would hurt some students and make the activities worse too.

First, a lot of kids just don't have time. My friend Marcus gets out of school at 2:45 and has to be at his job at the grocery store by 3:30. He does that four days a week, and the money helps his mom pay rent. Other kids have to watch their little brothers and sisters until a parent gets home. Some ride a bus that leaves right after the last bell, and there is no other bus. For these kids a required activity isn't a fun chance to try something. It's just one more thing they could get in trouble for, and that isn't fair because it's not their fault.

Second, making people join makes the activities worse. I was in band for two years, and you could tell right away who wanted to be there and who got signed up by their parents. The ones who didn't want to be there talked the whole practice and didn't even show up to concerts. Now think about a soccer team or the yearbook where a bunch of the people only joined because of a rule. The kids who actually care would have to do all the work and drag everybody else along.

Third, lots of students already do good things outside of school that the school doesn't know about. One girl in my class does karate downtown. Another one helps at her church's food pantry every Saturday. If only school activities count, it's like saying what they do doesn't matter.

I think a better idea is to make joining easier instead of forcing it. The school could have an activity fair in September, run a late bus twice a week, and let some clubs meet at lunch. Kids who join because they found something they like will stay and work hard. Kids who join because they have to will just wait until they can quit.
