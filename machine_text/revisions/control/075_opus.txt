Dear Principal,

I am writing to ask you to please not make students have a B average to play sports or be in clubs. I know you want kids to try harder in school, but I think this rule would actually hurt the kids it is supposed to help.

First of all, a lot of students at our school have a C average. A C is still passing. If all those kids get kicked off teams and clubs, a big part of the school would lose the thing that makes them feel like they belong here. Some of my friends honestly only come to school because they have practice after.

Second, sports and clubs teach you stuff you can't learn in class. When I was on the soccer team I learned how to manage my time, how to listen when my coach yelled at me, and how to work with people I didn't really like. That stuff matters just as much as a test grade.

Last, taking away sports won't make grades go up by itself. A better idea is to make kids below a C go to tutoring or study hall so they get help, not punishment.

Please let every student who is passing keep doing activities.

Sincerely,
A concerned student
