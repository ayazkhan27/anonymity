Phones and Driving

Pretty much everybody I know has a phone, and pretty much everybody I know has looked at it while driving. I only got my license last year and I've already done it too. It was at a red light, and I told myself it didn't count because the car wasn't moving. But that excuse is kind of the whole problem. I think the rule should be simple. Drivers should not be allowed to hold or mess with a phone at all while they are driving. The only things that should be allowed are the ones where your hands stay on the wheel and your eyes stay on the road.

The biggest reason is attention. Driving feels easy because most of the time nothing happens. My mom has driven the same way to work for years and nothing surprising ever happens to her. But the real point of paying attention is for the one second when something does happen, like a little kid running out from between parked cars or the truck in front of you slamming on its brakes. Reading a text takes your eyes off the road for a few seconds. In drivers ed they told us that on the highway you go about the length of a football field in that time. So for a whole football field nobody is really driving the car. It's just moving, and everybody around you is hoping nothing changes.

A lot of people say they are good at multitasking. I used to think I was too. The thing is, when you are distracted you can't tell what you missed. If I look down at my phone and nothing bad happens, I think I handled it fine. I never see the guy on the bike I would have hit if it was a different day. So every time you get away with it you get more confident, even though you didn't really prove anything. That is why there should be a law. The person holding the phone is the worst person to decide if it's safe.

Another reason is who gets hurt. If texting and driving only put the texter in danger, then maybe you could say it's their own choice. But it doesn't work like that. The person in the crosswalk and the family in the next lane never agreed to any of it. We already think this way about drunk driving. Nobody says a driver gets to decide for himself if four beers are too many. A phone in your hand is sort of the same thing, except people do it over and over again in one trip.

I don't think every single use of a phone should be banned though. Nobody uses paper maps anymore, and a GPS that says the directions out loud is probably safer than a lost driver trying to read every street sign. Calling 911 after a crash or to report a dangerous driver is a good use too. If the phone is on a mount on the dashboard, set up before you leave the driveway, and you only use your voice, that seems fair to me. Hands free calls still take some of your attention, so I wouldn't say they are totally safe. But it is a rule that can actually be enforced. A cop can see a phone in somebody's hand. He can't see if somebody's mind is wandering.

So my position is that you set up your phone before you start driving and then leave it alone until you get there. A text can wait twenty minutes. If it really can't wait, you can pull over, and that takes maybe two minutes. Compared to what could happen in the few seconds you are looking down, that is not a lot to ask, and I think the law should make people do it.
