Dear Principal,

I am writing to tell you what I think about the new rule that says students can't play sports or do other activities unless they have a B average. I don't agree with this rule, and I have a few reasons why.

First of all, a lot of students with a C average are not failing. They are passing all their classes, and a C is still an okay grade. It doesn't seem fair to take away the one thing they like about school just because their grades are average.

Also, activities can actually help students do better in class. When I'm on a team, I have less free time after practice, so I have to get my homework done on time. That makes me plan my day better. Kids in band or drama might feel like they belong at school for the first time, and that makes them want to come and try harder. If we take activities away from the kids who are struggling the most, their grades might go down instead of up.

Another reason is that the rule is not fair to everyone. Some students have jobs after school, or they have to watch their little brothers and sisters. Other students have learning problems that make getting a B really hard. For some of them, sports might be the only place where they feel successful. If they get kicked off, it tells them they don't belong here.

I do know that grades are important, and I get why you want students to work harder. Maybe a better idea would be to make the rule a C average and also give free tutoring and study hall to anyone who falls below it. That way the school would be helping students and not shutting them out.

Thank you for listening to what students think.

Sincerely,
A concerned student
