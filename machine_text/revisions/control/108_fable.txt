Phones and Driving

Pretty much everyone I know has a phone, and pretty much everyone I know has looked at it while driving. I have too. One time I looked down at a text at a red light and when I looked back up the light was already green and the guy behind me was honking. Nothing bad happened, but it kind of scared me how fast I stopped paying attention. Because of that I think drivers should not be allowed to hold or use a phone in their hands while the car is moving. I don't think every single use should be banned though.

The reason is pretty simple. To drive you need your eyes, your hands, and your brain, and a phone takes all of them. We learned in driver's ed that reading a text takes about five seconds, and on the highway that means you go the length of a football field without really looking. If you are typing you are not steering good, you aren't checking your mirrors, and you aren't ready to hit the brakes. And the person who gets hurt is a lot of times not even the driver. It could be a kid crossing the street or somebody on a bike or a family in the other lane. A text can wait ten minutes. You can't take back hurting somebody.

Some people say they are good enough to do both. I don't really believe that. Nobody thinks they are distracted when they are distracted, and that is the whole problem. Just because you feel confident doesn't mean you are actually good at it, and the law shouldn't be based on what every driver thinks about themself.

But I also don't think banning phones completely makes sense. People use their phones for maps now. A phone on a mount that says the directions out loud is a lot safer than being lost and making a sudden turn. Also a driver should be able to call 911 if there is an emergency. Hands free calls are harder to decide on because talking still takes your mind off the road, but a short call is more like talking to somebody in the passenger seat than it is like texting.

So here is the rule I would want. Put in your directions before you leave, put the phone on the mount, and don't touch it until you are parked. Maps and emergency calls would be legal. Texting, scrolling, and holding the phone would not. That isn't asking a lot from drivers and it keeps everybody else on the road safer.
