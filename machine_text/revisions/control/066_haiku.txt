Dear Principal,

I am writing to share my opinion about the new policy that would make students need at least a B average to play sports or be in other activities. I get why the school wants this. We all want to do well in class. But I think this policy would do more harm than good, and I hope you will read my reasons before you decide.

First, a lot of students right now have a C average, and this policy would take them out of the activities that keep them motivated. For lots of us, sports and clubs are the reason we even come to school. Practice gives us a place to belong and goals that are not just tests. It also gives us adults who notice when we are doing good things. If we get cut off from all that, we will probably lose interest in school completely, not get more interested. A kid who is struggling in math does not need to get pulled off the team that gives him a reason to get up in the morning.

Second, activities teach stuff that grades do not measure. On the field, in the band room, or on the debate team, you learn to manage your time, work with people who are different from you, and get over losing. Those lessons come into the classroom too. A lot of kids who play sports learn to plan their homework around practice, and that kind of discipline usually helps their grades over time. If you take away the activity, you take away the structure that could help a struggling student get better.

Third, the policy would hit hardest on the students who need help the most. A lot of kids with a C average are not lazy. Some have jobs to help their families, some are dealing with problems at home, and some just learn in a different way. Kicking them out of activities punishes them for things they cannot control and does not really help. If the goal is better grades, the school should offer tutoring, study halls, or extra time with teachers to these students. That way they can bring up their averages and still stay with the activities they like.

Finally, a grade rule could make activities less fair and less safe for everybody. Coaches and advisors would have to spend their time on eligibility paperwork instead of teaching and mentoring. Students who lose eligibility in the middle of a season could get pulled off a team right when they need it most. It would be better to have a system where students who start falling behind get early warnings, a plan to improve, and help along the way.

For these reasons, I respectfully ask you not to adopt the B average requirement. I think students should be encouraged to stay in activities, and the school should spend its money on helping every student raise their grades. I would be happy to join any discussion or student survey that could help us find a better solution.

Thank you for asking for our opinions and for thinking about this important question.

Respectfully,

A Concerned Student
