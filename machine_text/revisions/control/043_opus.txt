I think students would benefit from being able to go to classes from home, but only if distance learning is an option and doesnt replace regular school. If students get to choose, the kids who do better at home can stay home and the kids who need the classroom can still go.

My first reason is that it helps students who cant always get to school. Some people live really far away, some have a long illness, and some have to watch their little brothers and sisters while their parents are at work. For them missing one day can turn into missing a whole week and then they just fall behind. If they could join the class on video they would hear the same lesson and could ask questions like everybody else. Like if a girl had surgery she could keep up in algebra instead of coming back a month later and being totally lost.

My second reason is that everyone learns differently. Some students get anxious in a crowded classroom or get distracted by the people around them, and they focus way better somewhere quiet. Also if lessons are recorded you can pause and rewatch a part you didnt get, which you cant do when the teacher is just talking in front of the class.

I know distance learning has problems too. Not every family has good internet or a quiet place to work, and some kids need a teacher right there to stay on task. It can also get lonely at home, because a lot of what you learn at school comes from working with other people. Thats why it should be a choice and not something everyone has to do. Schools should also lend out laptops and check on the students who learn from home a lot.

If schools do it the right way, distance learning can give more students a fair chance to keep learning.
