Last winter my cousin broke her leg and missed three weeks of school. When she finally came back she was lost in pretty much every class, and she spent the rest of the semester trying to catch up. If her school had online lessons she could of kept up from the couch. So I think students would benefit from being able to attend classes from home, but only if it's an option and not a replacement for regular school.

The main reason is that stuff happens. Kids get sick, families move, the bus breaks down, or it snows and the roads close. Right now every one of those things means you just lose a day. With a video class, somebody with a small fever could still hear the lesson and not get thirty other people sick too.

Another reason is some students learn better by themselves. I know people in my classes who are too shy to raise their hand when the room is loud, but they would type a question in a chat box. Also if the lesson is recorded you can pause it and watch it again. A teacher can't really do that when twenty other kids are waiting on her.

There are problems with it though. At home it's really easy to get distracted, and not every family has good internet or a quiet room. Students also need friends and you can't make friends through a screen. That's why I wouldn't want online classes every single day.

But having the choice doesn't hurt anybody. A school that has both gives students a way to keep learning when they can't show up.
