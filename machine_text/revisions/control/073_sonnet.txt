Dear Principal,

I am writing about the idea of making students have a B average to play sports or be in clubs. I know you want kids to get better grades, but I think this rule would hurt a lot of students and it wouldn't really work. Please don't pass it.

First, lots of students with a C average are the ones who need activities the most. Someone who is bad at math might still feel good about themselves on the soccer field or in drama club. Activities give kids a reason to come to school every day. If you take them away, you might take away the only thing that keeps some kids trying. A kid who feels like a failure at everything is more likely to give up than to study more.

Second, think about how many students this would affect. You said that many students have a C average. That means a big part of our school could not be on teams, in clubs, or in band. Some teams might not have enough players and clubs might get so small they just disappear. The school would lose its spirit, and students would lose friends and memories that they will remember way after they forget a chemistry quiz.

Third, activities teach things that grades don't show. Being on a team teaches you to work together and be disciplined. With practice and games you have to manage your time. A lot of us do our homework right after practice because we know we don't have much time. Kids who have nothing to do after school fall behind anyway, or they just sit on their phones or get in trouble. Activities keep kids busy in a good way with adults watching.

Fourth, a B average is hard for some students for reasons they can't control. Some have jobs, take care of little brothers and sisters, or have learning problems that make school harder. It isn't fair to punish them by kicking them out. Their grades might show their situation more than how hard they try.

I do agree grades are important, and there are better ways to help them. The school could keep a lower rule like a C average instead of a B. If students go below it, they could be put on probation and get free tutoring or a study hall with a teacher. This helps students instead of shutting them out. Coaches and advisors could also check grades every few weeks so problems get caught early.

In conclusion, a B average rule would take away motivation, make our teams and clubs smaller, and be unfair to students who are struggling. Having support and a fair minimum would do more to raise grades. Thank you for listening to what students think.

Sincerely,
A Concerned Student
