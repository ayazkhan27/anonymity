Dear Principal,

I think Policy 1 is the better choice. Students should be able to bring their phones and use them at lunch and free time, as long as they are turned off in class.

My first reason is safety. If the bus breaks down or practice ends late, a student needs to be able to call their parents right away. With a total ban, the call has to go through the front office, and that takes a lot of time when it is an emergency.

Also, a total ban is hard to enforce. A lot of kids will hide their phones in their backpacks anyway. Then teachers will waste class time trying to find them instead of teaching us. Policy 1 is a rule that students can follow, phones off in class and okay to use the rest of the time.

Another reason is that lunch and free time are when we need a break. Reading a text from home, listening to music, or looking up a homework question can help students relax.

Last, knowing how to handle a phone is something we will need for life. We will have phones for the rest of our lives, so school is a good place to practice self control.

For these reasons, I hope you will pick Policy 1.

Sincerely,
A concerned student
