Dear Principal,

I am writing to ask you to pick Policy 1, which lets students bring phones to school and use them at lunch and other free times, as long as they are turned off in class. I think this is the best way to keep learning going and still be fair to students.

First, a total ban would be really hard to enforce. Almost every kid has a phone, and a lot of them would just hide them in their backpacks or pockets. Then teachers would spend their time looking for phones and giving out punishments instead of teaching us. With Policy 1 the rule is easy to understand. Phones are off in class and okay to use outside of it. When students think a rule is fair, they actually follow it.

Second, phones help keep us safe. Lots of students use them to call their parents after practice, to get a ride, or to tell someone about an emergency. If somebody feels sick or the bus is late, a quick call can fix the problem right away. A full ban would cut that off for the whole day.

Third, students need time to relax during free time. Lunch is a break from all the hard work, and being able to text a friend, listen to music, or check messages helps us go back to class calm and ready to focus. When students get some trust, they usually act more responsible. Policy 1 also teaches us to handle technology, a skill we need later.

Some people say phones are a distraction. That is a good point, and that is exactly why Policy 1 says they have to be off during class. This protects lessons but still lets us use our phones during breaks. If a student breaks the rule, the school can take the phone for the rest of the day, so the policy has real consequences.

For these reasons, I think Policy 1 is the better choice. It keeps classrooms focused, keeps students safe, and shows that our school trusts us to act mature. Thank you for reading my letter.

Sincerely,
A Concerned Student
