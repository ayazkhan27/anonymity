I think drivers should not be allowed to use their phones while driving. The only exception should be hands-free GPS and calling 911.

When you are driving you need to pay attention the whole time. If you are going sixty miles per hour, you go about the length of a football field in four seconds. That is about how long it takes to look down and read one text. In those few seconds a little kid could run into the street or the car in front of you could slam on its brakes. Thousands of people die every year because of distracted driving, and phones are a big reason why.

Some people say hands-free calling fixes the problem. It does help, but your brain is still distracted. If you are in the middle of an argument on the phone, you are thinking about that and not the road. Texting, scrolling, and watching videos are even worse because they take your eyes, your hands, and your mind off driving all at the same time.

I will admit phones can be useful in a car. GPS keeps you from getting lost, and calling 911 after a crash can save someone's life. Those should still be legal if the phone is mounted and you use voice commands.

Everything else can wait. No text is worth dying for.
