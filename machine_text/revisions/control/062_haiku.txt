Dear Principal,

I am writing to argue against the proposed policy that would require at least a grade B average for students to take part in sports and other activities. I know the school wants students to try harder in class, but this rule would hurt the kids it is supposed to help.

First, a lot of students who struggle in class find a place where they belong on a team or in a club. Those activities give them a reason to come to school, to stay involved, and to make friends with adults and other kids who support them. If we take that away, students with C averages might give up on school, and their grades could get even worse.

Second, sports and activities teach things that grades do not measure. Teamwork, discipline, leadership, and time management are learned through practice and competition. Students who cannot join these activities miss the chance to grow in ways that will matter after graduation.

Finally, the policy would punish students for struggling instead of helping them get better. A better idea would be tutoring, study hall, or mentoring for students who are falling behind. That way they can raise their grades and still keep the activities that motivate them.

For these reasons, I respectfully ask that the school keep sports and activities open to all students.

Sincerely,
A Concerned Student
