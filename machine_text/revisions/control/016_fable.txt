Dear Principal Harmon,

I heard you are thinking about changing the cell phone policy at our school. I think you should pick Policy 1, where we can use our phones at lunch and free time but they have to be off during class.

The first reason is safety. A lot of kids ride the bus or walk home or stay after for sports. Last month my band rehearsal got out forty minutes early and my mom was still at work. If I didn't have my phone I would of been standing outside in the dark waiting for her. If phones are banned, everybody would have to go to the front office to call home, and the office is already really busy.

Another reason is that a ban would not even work. Kids are still going to bring their phones, they will just hide them in their backpacks or go to the bathroom to use them. Then teachers have to waste class time looking for phones instead of teaching. Policy 1 is fair, so most kids would actually follow it. If you can text at lunch you don't need to sneak it in math.

Also, Policy 1 teaches us responsibility. When we are older and have jobs nobody is going to take our phones away. We have to learn when to put them down on our own, and school is a good place to practice.

I know some people think phones are distracting, and I agree. That is why they should be all the way off in class, and if someone breaks the rule the teacher can take it.

Please choose Policy 1.

Sincerely,
A student
