Dear Principal,

I am writing to you about the rule where you need a B average to play sports or do activities. I think this is a bad idea and I will tell you why.

First, alot of kids have a C average and there not lazy. Some kids study for hours and still get a C in math or science. If you take away the team it won't make the grade go up. It just takes away the one thing they like about school.

Second, sports keep kids on track. My cousin said the only reason he went to first period was because his coach checked attendance. Without practice kids like him wouldn't even come, and then their grades would get even worse.

Also activities teach you stuff. You learn to be on time and work with people you don't like and to lose without quitting.

I think a better rule is to keep the C rule and give tutoring to players who are slipping. Please don't shut us out.

Sincerely,
A student
