Dear Principal,

I am writing to you about the idea of making students have a B average to play sports or do other activities. I get why some people like it, because school is about learning first. But I think this rule would hurt way more kids than it helps, so I am asking you to keep the rule we have now.

First of all, a lot of kids at our school have a C average. A C is still passing. If the rule changes, all those kids would lose band, soccer, drama, yearbook and robotics all at the same time. The teams would get smaller and some clubs might not have enough people.

I also don't think the rule would make grades go up. For a lot of kids, their activity is why they show up on time and stay out of trouble. My cousin was a C student, and the only reason his grades didn't get worse was because his wrestling coach looked at his report card every two weeks. If they kicked him off the team, he would not of spent that time studying. He would of just been at home with nobody checking on him.

Another reason is that activities teach you stuff too. Somebody who is bad at algebra could be the best person in the whole school at running stage crew or leading the defense. Those kids should have one place where they are good at something, and a lot of times that helps them in class.

I think there is a better way to get what you want. Keep the C rule, but if someone's grades drop during the season, make them go to a study hall or tutoring. The coaches and club teachers could help make sure they go. That way kids have to do better but still keep their activity.

Thank you for asking us what we think before you decide.

Sincerely,
A concerned student
