Dear Principal,

I think you should pick Policy 1. It lets students bring their phones to school and use them at lunch and during free time, as long as they are turned off in class.

The first reason is safety. Sometimes parents need to get a hold of their kids, like if plans change or someone gets sick or there is an emergency. If phones are banned, our parents can't reach us when it might matter the most.

The second reason is that it teaches us to be responsible. When we get older nobody is going to take our phones away, we have to decide on our own when it's okay to use them. School is a good place to practice that because there are rules and consequences.

My third reason is that a full ban wouldn't really work. Lots of kids would just bring their phones anyway and hide them, and then teachers and students would fight about it more. If the rule is fair, people will actually follow it.

Also, free time is a good time to check messages, figure out rides home, or look up homework.

Policy 1 keeps class focused and still is fair to students. Please choose it.

Sincerely,
A Concerned Student
