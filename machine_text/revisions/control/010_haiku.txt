Dear Principal,

I think Policy 1 is the better choice. Students should be allowed to bring phones to school and use them during lunch and other free times, as long as phones stay off during class.

First, phones keep students safe. If a bus is late or there is a family emergency, parents can reach their kids quickly without someone having to go to the front office.

Second, a total ban would be hard to enforce. Kids who want their phones will hide them, and teachers will waste class time looking for devices. A simple rule, phones allowed at free times but off during class, is easier for everyone to follow.

Third, lunch and breaks are when students relax and hang out with friends. Checking messages, reading the news, or calling home during these times is fine, and it makes students feel trusted instead of treated like little kids.

Finally, Policy 1 teaches responsibility. Students learn when a phone is okay to use and when it is not. We will need that skill in high school, in college, and at work. A full ban takes away the chance to practice it.

For these reasons, I ask that you adopt Policy 1, with strict enforcement during class time.

Respectfully,
A Student
