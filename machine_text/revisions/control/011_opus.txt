Dear Principal,

I think Policy 1 is the better choice for our school. Students should be able to bring their phones and use them at lunch and during free time, as long as they are turned off in class.

My first reason is safety. A lot of parents want to be able to reach there kids if there is an emergency. Also students need a way to call home if they get sick, miss the bus, or there plans change after school. If something really bad happened at school a phone could let a kid call their family or 911 right away. Taking phones away from everybody just to stop a few distractions isn't fair.

My second reason is that phones help us keep track of our lives. Lots of kids have sports practice, jobs, or little brothers and sisters they have to pick up. If you can send a quick text at lunch to your coach or your mom, you can fix a problem before it turns into a bigger problem. Phones also have calendars and reminders and school apps that help us stay organized.

Third, Policy 1 teaches us to be responsible. When we grow up and go to college or get a job, nobody is going to take our phones away. We will have to decide for ourselves when its ok to use them. If we get to practice that now with clear rules about class, it will help us more than just banning phones.

Some people might say kids will break the rule and use their phones in class anyway. That is a fair point, but the answer is to enforce the rule, not punish every student. Teachers could take the phone from anyone who uses it in class, and if someone keeps doing it they could lose the privilege. I think most kids would follow the rule if they knew they could check there phone at lunch.

Policy 2 would be really hard to enforce and kids would probably just hide their phones anyway. Policy 1 is fair and it makes sense. I hope you pick it.

Sincerely,
A Student
