Dear Principal,

I heard that you are thinking about changing the cell phone policy at our school. I think you should pick Policy 1. Kids should be able to bring there phones and use them at lunch and free time as long as they are off during class.

The first reason is safety. Alot of kids walk home or take the city bus or stay after for sports. Last winter the heater in the gym broke so my basketball practice got out like forty minutes early. I had to call my mom to come get me. If I didn't have my phone I would of been standing outside freezing until 5:30. People say just use the office phone but the office is closed way before practice is over so that doesn't even work.

Another reason is that banning phones is not going to work anyway. Kids are still going to bring them, they will just hide them in there backpack or locker or pocket. Then teachers have to waste time looking for phones and writing people up when they could be teaching. Also if everybody breaks a rule every single day then it makes it seem like rules don't matter. Policy 1 is a rule kids would actually follow because it is fair.

Policy 1 also still keeps class time safe, and that is the whole point of having a phone policy. Phones would be off in class so nobody is texting under there desk or bothering the person next to them. If somebody does it anyway the teacher can take the phone until the end of the day. I think that is fair and most kids would get it.

My last reason is that lunch and free time is our time to relax. Listening to music or checking a text for twenty minutes is not going to make anybodys grades go down. It also helps us learn when its ok to be on your phone and when its not, and we are going to need to know that in high school and when we get jobs.

So I hope you choose Policy 1. It keeps us safe, it keeps class focused, and it shows that you trust us to be responsible.

Sincerely,
A student
