Dear Principal,

I am writing to ask you to please not make students have a B average to play sports or do other activities. I get that you want kids to care about their grades, but I think this rule would actually hurt the kids it's supposed to help.

First of all, a lot of students at our school have a C average. A C is still passing. If all these kids get kicked off teams and clubs, a big part of the school is going to lose the thing that makes them want to come to school every day. For alot of kids, practice after school is the reason they show up and don't get in trouble.

Second, activities teach you stuff that you can't really learn in a classroom. When you're on a team you learn how to work with other people, manage your time, and keep trying even after you lose. In band or debate club you learn discipline and you get more confident. Taking that away from a C student won't make them a better student. It might just make them give up.

Last, there is a better way to get what you want. Instead of a B cutoff, the school could keep it at a C and have study hall or tutoring for players whose grades go down. Coaches could check grades every couple weeks and help kids who are struggling.

Please keep activities open for students who are passing. Help them instead of shutting them out.

Sincerely,
A Concerned Student
