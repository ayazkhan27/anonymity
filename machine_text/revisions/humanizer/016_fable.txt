Dear Principal Harmon,

I heard you are thinking about changing the cell phone policy. I think you should pick Policy 1, where we can use phones at lunch and free time but they have to be off in class.

The biggest reason for me is getting home. A lot of kids ride the bus or walk, and some of us stay late for practice. Last month my rehearsal got out forty minutes early. If I didn't have my phone I would of been standing outside in the dark until my mom's shift was over. If you ban phones all day that stuff still happens, and then everybody has to go to the front office to call home. The office is already really busy.

Also I don't think a ban would even work. Kids are going to bring their phones anyway and hide them in their backpacks or use them in the bathroom. Then teachers have to go looking for phones when they are supposed to be teaching us. Policy 1 is fair, so I think most kids would follow it. If I can check my phone at lunch I don't really need to sneak it out in math.

Policy 1 also helps us learn something. In a few years we are going to have jobs and nobody is going to take our phones away. We will have to know when to put them down by ourselves, and school is a good place to practice that because there are rules and you get in trouble if you break them.

I know phones can be distracting and I worry about that too. So in class they should be all the way off, and if somebody breaks the rule the teacher should take it.

Please choose Policy 1.

Sincerely,
A student
