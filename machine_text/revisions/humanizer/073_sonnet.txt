Dear Principal,

I am writing about the idea of making students keep a B average to play sports or be in clubs. I know you want kids to get better grades, but I think this rule would hurt a lot of students and wouldn't really work. Please don't pass it.

First, a lot of the kids with C averages are the ones who need activities the most. Say a kid is bad at math but really good at soccer or drama. That might be the only reason he wants to come to school. If you take it away, he might stop trying completely. When someone feels like they are failing at everything, they usually give up instead of studying harder.

Second, you said many students have a C average. That means a big part of our school would get kicked off teams and out of clubs and band. Some teams might not even have enough players, and some clubs could get so small they just end. The school would lose a lot of spirit. Students would also lose friends and memories that they will remember way longer than a chemistry quiz.

Third, activities teach things grades can't show. On a team you learn to work with other people and to keep trying even when it is hard. You also learn to use your time well. A lot of us do our homework right after practice because we know we don't have much time. Kids who have nothing to do after school still fall behind a lot of the time, or they just sit on their phones or get in trouble. Activities keep kids busy and an adult is watching.

Fourth, a B is hard for some students for reasons they can't help. Some have jobs, take care of little brothers and sisters, or have learning problems that make school harder. It isn't fair to punish them by kicking them out. Their grades might show their situation more than how hard they try.

I do think grades matter, and there are better ways to protect them. The school could keep a minimum, like a C average, instead of a B. If a student goes below that, they could get a probation time and free tutoring or a study hall with a teacher. That helps students instead of shutting them out. Coaches and advisors could also check grades every few weeks so problems get caught early.

In conclusion, a B average rule would take away motivation, make our teams and clubs smaller, and treat struggling students unfairly. Helping students and having a fair minimum would raise grades more. Thank you for listening to what students think.

Sincerely,
A Concerned Student
