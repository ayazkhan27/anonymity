Dear Principal,

I am writing because I don't think students should need a B average to play sports or be in clubs. I get that you want kids to try harder in class, but I think this rule would hurt the same kids it's supposed to help.

First, a lot of students at our school have a C average. A C is still passing. If all of them get kicked off teams and out of clubs, a big part of the school loses the thing that keeps them involved. Some of my friends honestly only come to school in the morning because they have practice after.

Second, you learn stuff in activities that you can't learn in class. When I was on the soccer team I had to figure out how to manage my time, how to take it when the coach yelled at me, and how to work with people I didn't always like. I think that counts as much as a test grade does.

Also, taking away activities won't make grades go up on its own. It would be better to make kids below a C go to tutoring or study hall so they get help and not just punished.

Please let every student who is passing keep doing activities.

Sincerely,
A concerned student
