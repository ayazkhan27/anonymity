I think drivers should not be allowed to use their phones while driving. The only exceptions should be hands-free GPS and calling 911.

When you drive you need to pay attention the whole time. At sixty miles per hour a car goes about the length of a football field in four seconds, and that's about how long it takes to look down and read a text. In those four seconds a kid could run into the street or the car in front of you could slam on its brakes. Crash data shows distracted driving kills thousands of people every year, and phones are one of the biggest reasons.

Some people say hands-free calling fixes this. It helps some, but your mind is still on the call. If you're in an argument on the phone, you're thinking about the argument and not the road. Texting, scrolling, or watching videos is even worse because your eyes, your hands, and your brain are all off of driving.

I do get that phones are useful in a car. GPS keeps you from getting lost, and calling 911 after a crash can save someone's life. Those should stay legal as long as the phone is mounted and you use your voice.

Anything else can wait until you park.
