Dear Principal,

I think you should pick Policy 1. Students could bring their phones to school and use them at lunch and during free time, but they would have to keep them off in class.

My first reason is safety. Parents need to be able to reach their kids if plans change or someone gets sick or there is an emergency. If phones are banned, my mom can't text me when she is running late to pick me up, and that's when you need a phone most.

My second reason is that it teaches us to be responsible. When we get older nobody is going to take our phones away, we have to decide for ourselves when it's ok to use them. School is a good place to practice that since there are rules and you get in trouble if you break them.

Also a total ban wouldn't really work. Lots of kids would just bring their phones anyway and hide them, and then teachers and students would fight about it more. If the rule is fair people will actually follow it.

Lunch is a good time to check messages, figure out rides, or look up homework.

Policy 1 keeps class focused and still lets students have what they need. Please choose it.

Sincerely,
A Concerned Student
