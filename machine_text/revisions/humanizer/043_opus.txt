I think students should be allowed to go to class from home, but only if it stays an option and doesn't replace the regular classroom. If kids get to pick, the ones who do better at home can stay home and the ones who need school can still go.

My first reason is that some students can't always get to school. Some live really far away, some have an illness that lasts a long time, and some have to watch their little brothers or sisters while their parents are at work. For them missing one day can turn into missing a whole week, and then they just fall behind and never catch up. If they could join the class live on a video call they would hear the same lesson and get to ask questions like everybody else. Say a girl has surgery. She could keep doing algebra instead of coming back a month later to stuff she has never seen before.

My second reason is that people learn differently. Some kids get anxious in a crowded room or get distracted by the people around them, and they focus a lot better somewhere quiet. Also if the lessons are recorded you can pause and rewatch the part you didn't get, which you can't do when the teacher is talking at the front of the room.

I know distance learning has problems too. Not every family has good internet or a quiet place to work, and some students need a teacher right there or they won't stay on task. It can also get lonely at home, because a lot of what you learn at school comes from working with other people. That's why it should be a choice and not something everyone has to do. Schools should also lend out laptops and check on the students learning from home a lot.

If schools do it carefully, more students get a fair chance to keep learning.
