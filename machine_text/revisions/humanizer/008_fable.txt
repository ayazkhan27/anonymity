Dear Principal,

I heard you are thinking about changing the cell phone policy and I think you should pick Policy 1. That is the one where we can bring our phones to school and use them at lunch and free time, but they have to be off in class.

The biggest reason for me is safety. Lots of kids walk home or take the city bus, and some stay late for practice. Last winter the heater in the gym broke so my basketball practice ended forty minutes early. I called my mom and she came and got me. If I didn't have my phone I would of been standing outside in the cold until five thirty. People say just use the office phone, but the office closes before most practices even finish so that doesn't work.

Another reason is that a total ban would be really hard to enforce. Kids are going to bring phones anyway and hide them in their backpacks or lockers. Then teachers have to waste time looking for phones and writing referrals when they could be teaching. Also if almost everybody breaks a rule every day, it kind of teaches us that rules don't matter. Policy 1 is reasonable so we can actually follow it.

Policy 1 also still protects class time, and I think that is the main thing a phone policy is for. Phones would be off during lessons so nobody is texting under their desk or distracting the person next to them. If somebody breaks the rule the teacher can take the phone until the end of the day. I think that is fair and students would understand it.

My last reason is that lunch and free periods are our time to relax. Checking a message or listening to music for twenty minutes isn't going to hurt anyone's grades. It also lets us practice deciding when it is okay to use a phone. We are going to need to know that in high school and when we have jobs.

I hope you choose Policy 1. It keeps us safe and keeps class focused, and it treats us like we can be responsible.

Sincerely,
A student
