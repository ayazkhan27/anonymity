Dear Principal,

I think Policy 1 is the better choice. Students should be allowed to have their phones and use them at lunch and during free time, as long as the phones are turned off in class.

My first reason is safety. If the bus breaks down or practice runs late, a student can call a parent right away. If phones are banned completely, the student has to go through the front office, and that takes a lot longer when it is an emergency.

My second reason is that a total ban is really hard to enforce. A lot of kids will just hide their phones in their backpacks anyway. Then teachers waste class time looking for them instead of teaching. Policy 1 has a rule that students can actually follow, phones off in class and okay the rest of the time.

Also, lunch and free time are when students need a break. Checking a text from home, listening to music, or looking up homework can help us relax and focus in class.

Last, learning to use a phone is a real life skill. We will have phones our whole lives, so school is a good place to practice self control.

For these reasons, I think you should pick Policy 1.

Sincerely,
A concerned student
