Dear Principal,

I think Policy 1 is the better choice. Students should be allowed to bring phones to school and use them at lunch and other free times, as long as the phones stay off during class.

First, phones keep students safe. If a bus is late, there is a family emergency, or dismissal changes, parents can reach their kids without anyone having to go to the front office.

Second, a total ban is hard to enforce. Students who really want their phones will hide them, and teachers will end up spending class time looking for devices. A simple rule, phones allowed at free times but not during class, is easier for everyone to follow.

Third, lunch and breaks are when students relax and talk with friends. Checking messages, reading the news, or calling family during those times is a fair use of personal time. It also keeps students from feeling treated like young children.

Finally, Policy 1 helps students learn responsibility. We learn when a phone is okay to use and when it is not. We will need that skill in college and at work, and a full ban would take away the chance to practice it.

So I ask that you adopt Policy 1, with strict rules about phones during class.

Respectfully,
A Student
