Dear Principal,

I am writing to ask you to please not make students have a B average to play sports or be in activities. I get that you want us to care about our grades, but I think this rule would hurt the kids it is supposed to help.

First of all, a lot of students at our school have a C average, and a C is still passing. If all those kids get kicked off teams and clubs, a big part of the school loses the thing that makes them want to come to school. For a lot of kids, practice after school is the reason they show up and don't get in trouble.

Second, you learn stuff in activities that you can't really learn in class. When you're on a team you have to work with other people, manage your time, and keep trying even after you lose a game. Band and debate club teach you discipline and make you more confident. If you take that away from a C student they probably won't get better grades. They might just give up.

Last, I think there is a better way to do this. The school could keep the C rule and have study hall or tutoring for players whose grades go down. Coaches could check grades every few weeks and help the kids who are struggling.

So please let students who are passing stay in activities. Help them instead of kicking them out.

Sincerely,
A Concerned Student
