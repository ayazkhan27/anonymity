Dear Principal,

I think you should pick Policy 1, which lets students bring phones to school and use them at lunch and other free times, as long as they are off during class. I think this is fair to students and it still keeps learning safe.

First, a total ban would be hard to enforce. Almost every kid has a phone and a lot of them would hide them in their backpacks or pockets. Then teachers would spend their time looking for phones and giving out punishments instead of teaching. With Policy 1 the rule is easy to understand. Phones are off in class and we can use them outside of class. Students follow rules more when they think the rules make sense.

Second, phones help keep us safe. A lot of us use them to call our parents after practice, to get a ride, or to tell someone about an emergency. If a student feels sick or the bus is late, one quick call can fix it. If phones are banned all day, that connection is gone, and parents would worry.

Third, students need time to relax at lunch. Lunch is our break from hard work. Being able to text a friend, listen to music, or check messages helps us come back to class calm and ready to focus. When students are trusted a little, they usually act more responsible. Policy 1 would teach us how to handle technology, and we will need that skill long after we graduate.

Some people say phones are a distraction. I agree that this is a real problem, and that is why Policy 1 says phones have to be off during class. That way lessons are protected but students can still use their phones on breaks. If someone breaks the rule, the school can take their phone for the day so there are real consequences.

For these reasons I think Policy 1 is the better choice. Classrooms stay focused, students stay safe, and the school shows it trusts us to be mature. Thank you for reading my opinion.

Sincerely,
A Concerned Student
