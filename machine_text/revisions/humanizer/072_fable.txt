Dear Principal,

I heard about the idea to make students have a B average to play sports or do other activities. I get why people like it, because school is supposed to be for learning first. But I think it would hurt more kids than it helps, so I am asking you to keep the rule we have now.

A lot of students at our school have a C average. A C is still passing. It means you did what the school said you had to do. If the rule changes, those kids would lose band, soccer, drama, yearbook and robotics club all at the same time. The teams would get smaller and some clubs might not have enough people to keep going.

I also don't think the rule would make grades go up. For a lot of kids the activity is why they show up on time and stay out of trouble. My cousin was a C student and he wrestled. His coach checked his report card every two weeks, and that kept his grades from getting worse. If they cut him from the team he would not have used that time to study. He would have just been at home with nobody checking on him.

You learn stuff in activities too. Somebody who is bad at algebra could be the best person in the whole school at running stage crew or leading the defense. Those kids should get one place where they are good at something, and a lot of times that makes them do better in class also.

There is a more fair way to do this. Keep the C rule, but if somebody's grades drop during the season they have to go to study hall or tutoring. The coaches and club advisers could help make sure they go. Then students would have to work harder but they would still have the thing that makes them want to be at school.

Thank you for asking us what we think before you decide.

Sincerely,
A concerned student
