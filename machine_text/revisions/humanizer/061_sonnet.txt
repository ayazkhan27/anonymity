Dear Principal,

I am a student and I want to give my opinion on the new rule that says students can't be on sports teams or other activities unless they have a B average. I disagree with this rule.

First of all, a lot of students with a C average are not failing. They pass all their classes, and a C is a okay grade. Taking away the one thing they like about school just because they got an average grade seems too harsh.

Also, activities can actually help students do better in class. If you are on the track team, you learn to use your time well because practice takes up your afternoon and you still have to get your homework done. Someone in drama club or band might feel like they belong at school for the first time, and that makes them want to come and try harder. If we take activities away from the kids who struggle the most, their grades might get worse instead of better.

The rule is also not fair. Some students have a job after school, take care of their little brothers or sisters, or have a learning problem that makes getting a B a lot harder. For them, a sport might be the one place where they do well. Kicking them off the team tells them they don't belong.

I know grades are important and I get why you want students to work on them. I think a better idea would be to make the requirement lower, like a C average, and have free tutoring and a study hall for anyone below it. That would help students instead of shutting them out.

Thank you for listening to what students think.

Sincerely,
A concerned student
