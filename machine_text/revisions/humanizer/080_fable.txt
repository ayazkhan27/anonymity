Our principal wants every student to join at least one extracurricular activity. I get why, because clubs and teams can be the best part of school. But I don't think it should be required. A rule like that would hurt the kids it is supposed to help, and it would make the clubs worse too.

The first reason is time. A lot of students just don't have any. My friend Marcus gets out of school at 2:45 and has to be at his job at a grocery store by 3:30, four days a week. The money helps his mom pay rent. Other kids have to watch their little brothers and sisters until a parent gets home. Some ride a bus that leaves right after the last bell and doesn't come back. If an activity is required, kids like Marcus are going to get in trouble for something they can't control, and their families already have the least free time.

My second reason is that forcing people to join makes the activity worse for everybody. I was in the school band for two years. By the second rehearsal you could tell who wanted to be there and who got signed up by their parents. The ones who didn't want to be there talked during practice and skipped concerts. So what happens to the yearbook staff or the soccer team if a third of the people only joined because of a rule? The kids who care would have to drag everybody else along.

Also, plenty of students already do good things that the school never sees. One girl in my class takes karate at a studio downtown. Another one volunteers at her church's food pantry every Saturday. If only school activities count, that is basically telling them what they do doesn't matter.

I think a better plan is to make joining easier. The school could have an activity fair in September and run a late bus twice a week. Some clubs could meet during lunch. Kids who join because they found something they like will stay, work hard, and probably bring their friends. Kids who join because they were told to will just count the days until they can quit.
