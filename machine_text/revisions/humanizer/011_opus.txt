Dear Principal,

I am writing about the cell phone policy you are thinking about changing. I think Policy 1 is better. Students should get to bring phones to school and use them at lunch and during free time, as long as they are off in class.

My first reason is safety. A lot of parents want to be able to reach their kids if there is an emergency. Students also need a way to call home if they get sick, miss the bus, or their plans change after school. If something really bad happened at school, a student with a phone could call their family or 911 right away. Taking phones away from everybody just to stop a few distractions doesn't seem worth it to me.

My second reason is that phones help us keep our lives organized. Lots of kids have jobs or sports practice, or they have to pick up a younger brother or sister. If you text your coach or your mom at lunch you can fix a schedule problem before it gets worse. I also use my phone for my calendar and reminders and the school apps.

Third, Policy 1 teaches us to be responsible. After we graduate nobody is going to take our phones away at work or in college. We will have to figure out on our own when it's ok to use them. If we practice that now with clear rules for class time, we will be more ready than if there is just a total ban and we never get to choose.

Some people think kids will break the rule and use their phones in class anyway. I get that, but the fix is to enforce the rule instead of punishing every student. Teachers could take the phone from anyone who uses it in class, and if someone keeps doing it they could lose the privilege. I think most kids would follow the rule if they knew they could check their phone at lunch.

Also Policy 2 would be really hard to enforce, and kids would probably just hide their phones. Policy 1 is fair and it would actually work. I hope you pick it.

Sincerely,
A Concerned Student
