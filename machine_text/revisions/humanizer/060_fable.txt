Dear Principal,

I heard the school might make a rule that you need a B average to play sports or do other activities. I think this is a bad idea.

A lot of kids have a C average and they are not lazy. Some of them study for hours and still get a C in math or science. Kicking them off the team is not going to make that grade go up. It just takes away the one thing they look forward to at school.

Sports also keep kids coming to school. My cousin told me the only reason he showed up to first period was because his coach checked attendance. If kids like him don't have practice they have less reason to come, and then their grades could get even worse.

You learn stuff in activities too, like being on time and working with people you don't like. You also learn how to lose and not quit.

I think it would be more fair to keep the C rule and give tutoring to players who are slipping.

Sincerely,
A student
