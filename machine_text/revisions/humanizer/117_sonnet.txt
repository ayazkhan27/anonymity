Almost everybody has a phone these days, and most of us pick it up dozens of times a day. That is why phones in cars is such a big issue. A car weighs about two tons and moves fast, so the person driving it has to pay full attention. I believe drivers should not use cell phones at all while they are driving.

The main reason is how our attention works. People think they can multitask, but research on divided attention says the brain just switches back and forth between tasks, and every switch costs a moment of awareness. If a driver looks at a text for five seconds on the highway, the car travels about the length of a football field while nobody is watching the road. Even experienced drivers can't get around that.

Some people say hands-free calling is a good compromise because both hands stay on the wheel. But the danger is not only about hands. A conversation takes up your mind too. Studies of drivers on hands-free calls found they reacted slower to hazards and missed things right in front of them. A driver can be looking at the road and still not really see it. A total ban is also easier to enforce than a partial one. If all phone use is banned, a police officer doesn't have to decide which kind of use is okay.

Other people say you need your phone for directions or emergencies. Those are fair points, but they are easy to solve. You can type in the route before you start the car, or mount the phone and let it give spoken directions so you never touch it. In an emergency, you can pull over somewhere safe and call. Waiting a few minutes to stop costs very little compared to a crash.

Driving also affects other people. A distracted driver puts passengers, pedestrians, cyclists, and other drivers in danger, and none of them agreed to take that risk. We already have laws against drunk driving and running red lights because they threaten other people on the road. Using a phone while driving belongs with those.

Changing a habit is hard, but people can do it. Drivers can turn off notifications, switch on do-not-disturb, or put the phone somewhere they can't reach it. Every text can wait until the car is parked. A person's life is worth a lot more than a reply.
