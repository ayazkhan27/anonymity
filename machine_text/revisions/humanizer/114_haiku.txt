Cell phones are part of everyday life, and lots of people check them without even thinking about it, including while they drive. I believe drivers should not be allowed to use cell phones at all while they are behind the wheel. Driving takes full attention, and a phone takes some of that attention away in ways people don't notice.

Even hands-free calls make a driver listen, talk, and think about a conversation. Studies on distracted driving keep showing that this slows reaction time and makes drivers less aware of traffic, people walking, and road conditions that change. Texting, scrolling, and checking notifications are even worse because they take your eyes off the road and one hand off the wheel at the same time.

Some people say a full ban is unrealistic and that responsible drivers can handle a quick look at a map or a message. The problem is that distraction is hard to predict. One second of not paying attention at highway speed can be the difference between a close call and a deadly crash. Laws that allow some phone use are also hard to enforce, because police cannot easily tell if a driver is dialing, listening, or reading.

A clear rule with no exceptions is simple for drivers to follow and protects everyone else on the road. The phone can wait until the car is parked. Most texts and notifications can wait a few minutes without anything bad happening. Waiting is a small inconvenience, and it is worth it if the rule saves lives.
