Last winter my cousin broke her leg and missed three weeks of school. When she came back she was lost in every class. If her school had online lessons she could have kept up from the couch. I think students should be able to attend class from home, as long as it is only an option.

The main reason is that things happen that have nothing to do with school. Kids get sick, families move, the bus breaks down, or it snows and the roads close. Right now every one of those is a lost day. With a video class, a student with a small fever could still hear the lesson, and he wouldn't give his germs to thirty other people.

It would also help kids who learn better by themselves. Some people in my classes are too shy to ask a question in a loud room, but they would type it in a chat box. A recorded lesson can be paused and watched again too. A teacher can't do that when twenty other kids are waiting.

I know there are problems. It's easy to get distracted at home, and some families don't have fast internet or a quiet room. Students also need friends, and I don't think you can really make friends through a screen. That's why I wouldn't want online classes every day.

But I don't see who it hurts to have the choice. If a school has both, a student who can't show up in person can still keep learning.
