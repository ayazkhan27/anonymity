I agree with my principal that every student should have to do at least one extracurricular activity. School teaches us facts, but activities teach us stuff like working with people and managing our time.

First, activities teach teamwork. If you play soccer, you find out pretty fast that you can't win by yourself. You have to pass the ball and trust the other players. Yearbook is the same way because the writers and photographers have to get their parts done on time or there is no yearbook. Adults need to do this at their jobs too.

Second, activities help with time management. When I joined debate club I had practice three days a week after school. I was worried my grades would go down, but they didn't. I started planning out my homework and I stopped wasting my whole night on my phone. I think a lot of kids actually focus better when they are busy.

Also, activities help kids figure out what they like. A shy kid could try drama and find out they are good at acting. Someone on student council might decide they want to be a lawyer.

Some people say students are already too busy. But it's only one activity, and you get a lot out of it. That's why I agree with the principal.
