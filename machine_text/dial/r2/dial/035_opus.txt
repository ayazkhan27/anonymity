Dear Principal,

I am writing about whether our school should make community service required. I think every student should have to do it, as long as the hours are reasonable and students get to pick how they help.

First, community service teaches things you can't learn in a classroom. My friend Maya tutors second graders at Lincoln Elementary two times a week. She told me that explaining fractions to a seven year old made her understand them better herself. She also learned patience and how to talk to kids who get upset. She will use those skills at any job later.

Second, service connects students to the town they live in. Last spring a group of us picked up trash along the creek behind the park. It was hard and dirty, but we had a great time doing it together. We filled twenty bags in one afternoon. Now when I walk by that creek, I notice when trash shows up and I pick it up. I didn't care about that spot before we worked on it.

I know some students say they are already busy with homework, sports, and jobs. That is fair. But twenty hours a year is less than half an hour a week, and that is not much. The school could also count things students already do, like coaching a little league team or helping at a church food drive. That way it would feel normal instead of like extra work.

Requiring service would help our town and help students grow. Please think about making it part of graduating from our school.

Sincerely,
A Concerned Student
