Dear Principal,

I am writing about the idea of making every student do community service. I think students should not be forced to do it. Service is fine when a person picks it, though.

Some of my friends tutor little kids at the elementary school on Tuesdays. One of them told me a second grader finally read a hard word, and she said it was the highlight of her week. Another friend picks up trash by the creek behind our school. He says the creek looks different after he is done. They both signed up on their own, so they show up on time and do the work.

If the school makes it a rule, that will change. A lot of students already have jobs after school to pay for bills. Others watch their younger brothers and sisters, or they have practice and homework every night. I know kids who are really tired already, and a required number of hours would be one more thing on a long list. When people are forced, they usually do the bare minimum. They sign the sheet and leave early. That does not help anybody, and some kids will end up hating volunteering. It would be a problem for the school too, since teachers would have to check all the hours.

My idea is to make it easy to join. The school could have a sign-up table with local groups, run projects on weekends or at lunch, and read the names of volunteers at assemblies. More students would sign up if they were invited instead of pushed. I do not think a rule is needed for that.

Thank you for reading my letter.

Sincerely,
A concerned student
