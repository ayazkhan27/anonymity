Dear Principal,

I am writing about whether our school should make every student do community service. I do not think it should be a rule. I think the school should offer it and make it easy to join.

Some of my friends volunteer. One of them tutors third graders in reading after school. She says the kids get really excited when a hard word finally makes sense. Another friend joins a cleanup at the creek near our neighborhood on Saturdays. They both like it, and I can see why. Service teaches you to be patient and responsible. It also makes you feel part of something bigger.

But a requirement would be unfair to a lot of students. Many of us have full schedules. Some have part-time jobs to help pay the bills at home. Others watch their younger brothers and sisters, or they have practice for a team every day. My friend Marcus works at a grocery store four evenings a week. Making him find extra hours for service is a lot to ask, and his grades could drop.

Another thing is the reason people do it. When something is forced, most people do the minimum and watch the clock. A student who has to sign a log sheet might not care at all. Service that comes from your own choice means more to the student and to the people getting help. I have seen this happen with school clubs too.

Here is what I suggest instead of a rule. The school could hold a volunteer fair where local groups like food banks, libraries, and animal shelters explain what they need. We could do service projects during lunch or on school grounds, so students with busy days do not need extra travel. We could also give volunteers a certificate or a shout out at assemblies. When students see classmates getting honored, I think more of them will want to join. However, it has to be easy, or hardly anybody will sign up.

I believe this plan would get more students involved, and it would work better than a rule. Thank you for reading my opinion.

Sincerely,
A Concerned Student
