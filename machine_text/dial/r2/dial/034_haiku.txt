Dear Principal,

I think students should have to do community service. Some of my friends tutor little kids at the elementary school, and others pick up litter in the neighborhood parks. These jobs show students how our town works outside of class, and they are a useful way to spend time.

Some kids say service takes time away from homework, sports, or jobs. That is a problem, and it can be hard to fit in. But it is only a few hours a month, so most students can plan around it. Service is not always fun, but it teaches patience, teamwork, and responsibility. Those skills will be useful at school and at work later.

Requiring service also makes things fair. When everyone helps, nobody is left out. Students get to know people they would never meet otherwise, and the school becomes a place where people look out for each other. I hope you will approve this requirement. Students will learn a lot from it.

Sincerely,
A Concerned Student
