Dear Principal,

I think Policy 1 is the best choice. Letting students have phones at lunch and free time, as long as they are off in class, is a great way to keep the good parts of phones and keep school fun and happy.

First, phones are a really good safety tool. If there is a late bus or a change in plans, students can reach their families right away, and parents feel safe and happy. Second, Policy 1 teaches responsibility. Students get to decide when a phone is okay to use. Learning to put a phone away and focus is a very useful skill for college and for work, and I love that we get to practice it now.

Third, the rule is simple and fair. Phones are off during class, and teachers can spend their time teaching, which is great for everybody. Lunch is also the one time we can relax, check on family, and laugh with friends. That nice rest helps us do well in the afternoon.

For these reasons, I hope you will choose Policy 1. Under a full ban, teachers would need a lot of time to check every bag in every room. Thank you for reading my letter.

Sincerely,
A Concerned Student
