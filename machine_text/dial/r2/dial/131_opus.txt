When you have a hard decision, asking more than one person for advice can help you make a better choice. Everybody sees things through their own life, so one opinion is not always enough.

For example, say a girl is trying to pick between the soccer team and the school play. Her coach might push soccer because he knows she is really talented and would love to have her. Her drama teacher might say the play will build her confidence. Her older brother, who did both, might tell her that the practices are at the same time on Tuesdays and Thursdays. Each person gives her part of the picture, and together they show her what matters to her.

Getting more opinions also helps you catch mistakes. One friend might say a used car is a good deal. A second friend who knows engines could look under the hood and spot a leak the first one missed.

Last, hearing different views makes you think for yourself. You don't just obey one voice. You have to weigh the reasons and pick what fits your life. That is why I think you should talk to a few people before you decide something big.
