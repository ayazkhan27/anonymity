Dear Principal,

I am writing about the plan to make every student do community service. I do not think it should be required. The school can still tell us about it and post the sign up sheets.

My first reason is that service only works when the person wants to be there. A girl in my class named Dana tutors second graders in reading two times a week. She knows which kids are stuck on which sounds. She brings shark books for one boy because sharks are the only thing he will read about. Now think about a student who got sent there and just stares at the clock. A seven year old can tell. The kid gets a really bad tutor, and the student starts to think of it as one more chore, the same as detention.

My second reason is time. Some kids in my class have nothing to do after school. Others stock shelves at a grocery store until nine, or they go home and watch their little brothers and sisters until a parent gets off work. Those students are already serving somebody. They come to school so tired. Forty hours would be very hard for them, and it is not fair to make it cost them more than everybody else. Some of them would have to pick between a paycheck and a diploma. A school should never force that on a kid.

The third problem is the places we would get sent to. The food pantry by my house only has two workers who get paid. If sixty teenagers show up needing signatures, those two people will lose the whole afternoon making up jobs and filling out forms. That does not feed anybody who is hungry. The litter crews on my street work because ten people show up on Saturday with gloves and bags, and nobody had to drag them there.

There is another way to do this. You could make service a class that counts toward graduation. Clubs could do projects during the school day. Students who put in a lot of hours could get their names read at the ceremony. You could also count the work kids already do for their families. Then nobody gets punished because they do not have the time.

Thank you for reading a student's view before you decide.

Sincerely,
A concerned student
