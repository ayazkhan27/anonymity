Dear Principal,

I am writing about the phone policy. I think you should pick Policy 1, which lets students bring phones to school and use them at lunch and in free time, as long as they are turned off in class. A full ban sounds simple, but it is not going to work the way people expect.

First, a ban is hard to enforce. Almost every kid has a phone. Lots of them would put it in a backpack or a pocket and keep it there. Then teachers would spend half of class looking for hidden phones and writing up kids instead of teaching the lesson. With Policy 1 the rule is easy to follow. If a student knows they can check their phone at lunch, they have no reason to sneak a look during math.

Second, there is the safety part. Some of us have parents who work late. Some of us have to pick up a little brother or sister after school. Some students have medical stuff they need to keep track of. If something happens, a student should be able to call home fast. Under Policy 2 you would have to walk to the office and ask to use the school phone, and that takes a long time when it is not an emergency you can wait on. With Policy 1 the phone is right there in the bag.

Third, it teaches us what to do on our own. In a few years we will be in high school, then college and jobs, and nobody is going to take our phones away there. We have to learn to put the phone down when it is time to work. A ban does not teach that. It just pushes the lesson off until later. Policy 1 lets us show every day that we can be trusted.

Some people worry about cheating, bullying, and kids staring at screens. Those worries are bad, but Policy 1 already keeps phones off in class, so the main problem is handled. The school can also give clear punishments, like losing phone time for a week. That way the kids who break the rules get the consequence, and everyone else is not punished for it.

So I hope you choose Policy 1. It keeps class time for learning, it treats students like they can handle responsibility, and I think most of us would really enjoy proving that. It would be so great, and we will not let you down.

Sincerely,
A Concerned Student
