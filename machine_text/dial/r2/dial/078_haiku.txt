Dear Principal,

I think you should not make students keep a B average to be in sports and other activities. A grade rule might seem like it helps students study more, but it would mostly hurt the kids who need help the most.

A lot of students with a C average are only struggling in one or two classes. For them, sports, band, or debate is often the reason they come to school. If you take those activities away, you also take away the structure that could help their grades get better.

Activities also teach things that classrooms do not always test, like teamwork and handling pressure. Students who learn these things often go back to homework with more focus. Some kids are very stressed about grades, but a team can help them feel less alone.

Instead of cutting students off, the school could offer tutoring, study halls, or check-ins for anyone whose grades drop. That would help them instead.

Please think about this.

Sincerely,
A Concerned Student
