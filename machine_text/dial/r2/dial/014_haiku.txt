Dear Principal,

I think we should choose Policy 1. It lets students bring phones to school and use them at lunch and other free times, as long as they are off during class. A total ban might seem like the easy choice, but it would hurt students more than it helps.

First, phones help students stay safe. Many families use them to confirm pickup times or check in when a bus is late. Under Policy 2, those students would have no way to reach home during the school day.

Second, a ban treats students like they cannot follow a simple rule. Policy 1 already sets a clear limit: phones stay off during class. Students who follow that rule keep their phones, and students who break it can lose the privilege. This teaches responsibility, which students will need after they leave school.

Third, free time matters. Lunch and passing periods are when students eat, talk with friends, and rest. A phone is often how they read the news or answer a family message. But taking that away does not make those minutes better. It only makes them more crowded, and some students feel worried and alone.

Finally, a full ban would need constant checking, lockers, and confiscations. Those tasks take staff time away from students. Policy 1 is easier to watch because the rule is simple.

I respectfully ask you to choose Policy 1. Without it, many students would spend the day feeling stuck, isolated, and really lonely.

Respectfully,
A Concerned Student
