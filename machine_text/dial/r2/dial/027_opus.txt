Dear Principal,

I think our school should make every student do some community service, as long as the number of hours stays reasonable.

Some of my friends tutor second graders at Lincoln Elementary after school. At first they only went because a teacher asked them to, but now they talk about "their kids" all the time and say it is fun. One of them even wants to be a teacher. She never would have figured that out if nobody pushed her to try it.

Other friends spent a Saturday picking up trash along the creek behind the park. They filled twenty bags. The next week people were walking their dogs there again. A few hours of work changed the place for the whole neighborhood.

I know some kids think service will take time away from homework, sports, or jobs. That makes sense, but ten or fifteen hours a year with lots of choices of when and where is not a big deal for anyone.

Service teaches responsibility and helps our town, but it only works if the school makes it count. If we don't require it, most kids won't sign up. If we do, I think a lot of them will end up glad they went.

Sincerely,
A concerned student
