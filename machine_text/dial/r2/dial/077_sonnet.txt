Dear Principal,

I am writing about the new rule that says students need a B average to play sports or join clubs. I know you want kids to get good grades, but I think this rule is unfair and hurts a lot of students.

First, many students have a C average. A C is a passing grade. Those students are doing okay in their classes. They would still be removed from the teams and clubs they love. For some kids, sports are the only reason they come to school. If we take that away, they might stop trying in class, and then their grades could get worse.

Second, activities teach things you can't learn in a classroom. A team teaches you to work together and show up on time. The yearbook staff and the drama club teach responsibility. A student might struggle in math, but that same student can be a great musician or a strong leader on the field. Those students have talents too.

Third, activities help kids with their grades. Many athletes keep a schedule because practice is right after school. Coaches and advisors check in on their students and tell them to study. Without that, some students would have nobody pushing them.

Instead of a B average, I think a C average is a fair rule. The school could also offer free tutoring for students who need it. That way grades still count, and students get help from the school instead of getting shut out.

Please think about all of the students before you decide. Thank you for reading my letter.

Sincerely,
A concerned student
