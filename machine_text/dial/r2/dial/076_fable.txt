Dear Principal,

I am writing about the idea to make students have a B average to be in sports and other activities. I get why people want it, since school comes first. I still think the rule should stay at a C.

First, a C is passing. A kid with a C average is doing what the school asks. A lot of us study every night and that is where we end up, mostly in classes like algebra or science. The grade is only a C, but the work behind it is hard and honest.

Second, activities are what keep some kids going in class. My friend plays soccer and he does his homework on the bus, because the coach checks grades every Friday. He says math is boring, but he would hate to miss a game, so he does it. If you took soccer away, he would go home and sit on his phone.

Third, you learn things on a team that you do not get in a classroom. You have to show up on time. You have to work with people you did not pick. You have to finish the season even when the scores go the other way.

Also, so many students have a C that the teams and clubs would get small. Some of them would not have enough people to keep going. That is not fair to the kids who do have a B, because they would lose their team too.

I think there is another plan. Keep the C rule, and add tutoring after school for students whose grades are low. Coaches could let players leave practice to go. It would take time and it might not fix every grade, but kids would still have a reason to come to school. Please think about it before you change the rule.

Sincerely,
A concerned student
