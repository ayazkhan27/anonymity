Dear Principal,

I am writing about community service. I think every student should do it, and I think it is a good plan when the rules are fair and kids get nice choices.

Some of my friends already volunteer, and it makes them happy. One friend tutors second graders in reading every Thursday. She loves it. At first she did it for club hours, and now she talks about her cute students and plans fun lessons on her own. Another friend joined a weekend group that cleans the creek behind our school. He says it feels great to see the creek look pretty, and he is proud to pick up each wrapper he sees. They found these good things because somebody gave them a push.

I know some kids think it is a bad idea. We have sports, jobs, and family chores, and homework takes a lot of time. Nobody wants a hard rule that feels like a problem. The school can ask for a small number of hours, maybe ten each semester, and let kids count the nice things they already do, like helping at a church food drive or watching a little cousin. A flexible plan is fair, easy for busy students, and friendly.

Community service also teaches good lessons that books can teach only a little. Students learn to be on time, to work with all kinds of people, and to be kind and helpful. Those are great habits for college and for any job. The town gets a lot of help too, like clean parks and happy kids who love to read.

So I hope you will pick a plan with fun choices and flexible hours. Some students may feel lazy at the start, and that is fine. Most of them will feel glad and proud, and they will be happy they tried. I would love to help make a good program for our school.

Sincerely,
A Concerned Student
