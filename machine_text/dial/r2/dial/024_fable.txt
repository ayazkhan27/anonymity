Dear Principal Morris,

I heard you are deciding if every student should have to do community service. I think it is a good idea and you should require it. Keep the hours small, maybe ten a semester.

My friend Dana tutors second graders at Lincoln Elementary on Tuesdays. Her church group asked her to. At first she hated it and complained for a month, and she said it was boring and a waste of her time. Now she loves it. She is proud of a boy named Marcus, who struggled with one page in September and happily finished a whole chapter book in March. That is a great win for both of them, and she is glad someone pushed her to sign up. A lot of us are like Dana. Nobody asks us, so we stay home.

I know the other side. Some of my friends work after school or watch their little brothers and sisters. They are tired and worried about losing free time. That is fair. It is why the rule should be small and easy to fit in. Ten hours is one Saturday morning a month. Students could also count kind things they already do, like coaching a youth soccer team or helping at a food pantry.

There is plenty to do close by. Right now the park behind our school is ugly. It is covered in dirty trash and broken glass. A team of friends could clean it in one sunny morning.

Please make service a rule and let us choose how we do it. Helping is good for us, and it is fun with friends. I hope you say yes.

Sincerely,
A student
