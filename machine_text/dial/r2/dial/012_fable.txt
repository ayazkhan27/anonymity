Dear Principal,

I think you should pick Policy 1. It lets students bring phones to school and use them at lunch and free periods, as long as they are off in class.

My first reason is safety. A lot of us walk home, ride the bus, or stay late for practice. Plans change, and it is good when parents can reach us. Some people say the office phone works fine. It is not much help when the office closes at five.

My second reason is responsibility. We will have phones at every job, so it is smart to learn now when to put them away. Policy 2 just moves that lesson to later.

Also, Policy 2 would not be easy to enforce. Teachers would have to check backpacks, and kids would hide phones anyway. That is really unfair to teachers.

Policy 1 keeps class time safe for learning and gives us free time to use phones. Students who do not follow the rule should hand over the phone for the day.

Sincerely,
A Student
