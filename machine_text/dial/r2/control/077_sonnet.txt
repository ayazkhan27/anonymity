Dear Principal,

I am writing about the new policy that says students have to have a B average to play sports or be in clubs. I understand that you want kids to get better grades, but I think this rule will hurt more students than it helps.

First of all, a lot of students have a C average, and a C is still a passing grade. These kids are doing ok in their classes, but they would get kicked off the teams and clubs that they love. For some students sports is the only reason they even want to come to school. If we take that away from them they might stop trying and their grades might get even worse.

Second, activities teach you stuff that you cant learn in a classroom. Being on a team teaches you how to work with other people and be disciplined. Yearbook and drama club teach you responsibility and how to manage your time. Someone might be bad at math but really good at music or being a leader. This policy ignores what those students are good at.

Third, activities can actually help students get better grades. A lot of athletes keep their schedules organized because practice is right after school. Coaches and advisors also check on their students and tell them to study. If they dont have that, kids who are struggling might not have anybody pushing them to do better.

Instead of a B average, I think a C average would be more fair, and the school could give free tutoring to students who fall behind. That still shows that grades matter, but it helps students instead of punishing them.

I hope you think about this before you decide. I really think it would be a better choice for our school. Thank you for listening to the students.

Sincerely,
A concerned student
