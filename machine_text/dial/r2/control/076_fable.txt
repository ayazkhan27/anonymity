Dear Principal,

I heard you are thinking about changing the rule so that students need a B average to play sports or be in other activities. I understand why you would want that, since school is supposed to come first. But I think this rule would hurt way more kids than it would help, so I am asking you to please keep it at a C.

First of all, a C is a passing grade. If you have a C average you are doing what the school asks, even if it isn't great. A lot of us study really hard and still get C's, especially in hard classes like algebra and science. If you take away the team or band from those kids, your basically punishing them for trying.

Another reason is that activities are the reason some kids even try in class. My friend is on the soccer team and he does his homework on the bus because the coach checks grades every Friday. If he got kicked off the team he would not start studying more. He would just go home and play video games and his grades would get even worse.

Also, activities teach you things you don't learn in class. You learn to show up on time and work with people you didn't pick. You learn how to lose and not quit. The kids who have trouble in class probably need that more then anybody.

One more thing is that a lot of students have a C average, so the teams and clubs would get a lot smaller. Some of them might not even have enough people to keep going.

I think a better idea would be to keep the C rule and add tutoring for kids who are close to failing. That way grades could go up without taking away the thing that makes us want to come to school in the first place.

Sincerely,
A concerned student
