Dear Principal,

I am writing to ask you to not make every student do community service. I think community service is a good thing and the school should encourage it, but I dont think it should be forced on people.

I have a few friends who tutor little kids at the elementary school and they really like it. One of them told me the best part of her week is when a second grader finally sounds out a hard word. Another friend picks up trash by the creek behind our school and he feels proud when he sees how much cleaner it is. It works for them because they picked it themselves. They actually care about the kids and the creek so they try hard.

If it was a requirement it would be different. Some of my friends work after school to help pay bills. Others have to watch their little brothers and sisters, or they have sports and homework. A required number of hours would just be one more thing in a day thats already full. When people are mad about an assignment they just do the least they can, like sign the sheet and leave early. That doesnt help anybody, and it might make them never want to volunteer again.

I think a better idea is to make service easy and fun. The school could set up a sign-up fair with different groups, have projects on weekends and at lunch, and give shout-outs to volunteers at assemblies. More kids would join if they felt invited instead of forced.

Thank you for reading my letter.

Sincerely,
A concerned student
