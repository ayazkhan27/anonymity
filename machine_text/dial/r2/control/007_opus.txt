Dear Principal,

I heard your thinking about changing the cell phone policy and I think Policy 1 is better. Students should get to bring phones to school and use them at lunch and free periods, as long as there off in class.

My first reason is safety. Alot of parents want to be able to reach there kids during the day and kids need a way to call home if something changes. Like if practice gets canceled or you feel sick you can just text your mom. If there was a real emergency like a lockdown or a bus crash, a phone could let your family know your ok. If you ban phones all the way parents would be way more worried.

My second reason is it teaches us to be responsible. In a few years were going to be in college or have jobs and nobody is going to take our phones away. We have to figure out on our own when to put them down. If we can use them at lunch but have to keep them off in class, thats good practice. A total ban just teaches kids to sneak them.

Third, a full ban would be really hard to enforce. Kids are still going to bring phones in there pockets and backpacks, and teachers will waste time looking for them and taking them instead of teaching. With Policy 1 the rule is clear and fair so most kids will actually follow it. Teachers only have to do something if somebody takes a phone out in class.

Also phones can help during free time. You can check your grades and assignments, look stuff up for a project, or text your group for a project. Lunch is supposed to be a time to relax to, and listening to music or texting your mom for a couple minutes doesn't hurt anybody.

Some people think phones will distract students. I get that but Policy 1 already fixes that because phones have to be off when we are learning. If someone breaks the rule they can get in trouble and everyone else still gets the good stuff.

For all these reasons I really hope you pick Policy 1. It keeps students safe, it teaches responsibility, and its easier to enforce. Thank you for reading my letter.

Sincerely,
A Concerned Student
