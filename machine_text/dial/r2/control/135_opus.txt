When people have a hard decision to make alot of them ask more than one person for advice. Some people think that is just a way to put off choosing but I think it actually helps you make a better choice. When you ask a bunch of people you get more information, you don't get stuck with one persons bias, and you get to think about your own ideas before you decide.

My first reason is that different people know different things. Nobody has done everything. For example, say a senior in high school is trying to pick between a state college and a community college. Her guidance counselor can tell her about transfer credits and how hard it is to get in. Her older cousin went to community college first so she can tell her what it was really like to save money while all her friends moved away. Her mom or dad can tell her what the family can actually pay for. Every person knows a different part and she only gets the whole picture if she talks to all of them. If she only asked one person she would be missing alot of important stuff.

My second reason is that everybody giving advice is kind of biased, even if they are trying to help. Like a coach might tell a really good athlete to focus on sports because the team needs him. But his music teacher might tell him to practice piano more. Neither of them is lying, they just see him in there own way. When he hears both of them he can see that they want different things and he can ask himself what he wants. Having alot of opinions kind of works like a balance. If only one person is pushing you, you might end up doing what they want instead of what you want. With more people you can find the middle and see things better.

My third reason is that when you ask alot of people you have to explain your problem over and over, and that helps you think. Lets say a guy wants to buy a used car from somebody. The first time he tells his friend about it he might not even say that the car has alot of miles on it. But by the third time somebody asks him about the miles and he figures out thats what is really bugging him. So the advice is important but just telling the story is important too. Every time he talks about it he thinks of a question he didn't think of before. That is why talking to more people is good even if they don't say anything that smart.

Of course you can ask to many people. If you ask like twenty people you might be more confused then before, and it's still your decision at the end. Your not supposed to let a whole crowd decide for you. The point is to get enough opinions so you aren't rushing into something you will regret later.

In conclusion, asking for more than one opinion helps people make better choices. It fills in the stuff they don't know, it keeps them from getting pushed around by one person, and it helps them figure out what they really care about. A good decision usually doesn't come from just one person. It comes from listening to a few people and then really thinking about what they said before you make up your mind.
