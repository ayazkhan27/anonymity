Dear Principal,

I am writing about whether students should have to do community service. I think we should, but only if the rule is fair and lets students pick what they do.

A lot of my friends already volunteer and I have seen how it changes them. My friend Maya helps second graders with reading every Thursday after school. At first she only did it cause her club needed hours. Now she talks about her kids all the time and she even makes up her own lessons. Another friend, Jake, picks up trash by the creek behind our school on weekends. He told me he never noticed how much garbage was there until he started picking it up. Now he wont even drop a wrapper on the ground. They probably would not have found this stuff if nobody made them try.

I know some people disagree. Lots of us have sports, jobs, or have to take care of family, and we already have a ton of homework. Community service could feel like one more thing to do. But I think this problem can be fixed if the school has a good plan. The school could ask for ten hours each semester, which is not that much when its spread out. Students should be able to count things they already do, like helping at a church food drive or watching their little brother or sister. That way the rule is fair for busy kids.

Also, community service teaches things you cant get from a textbook. You learn to show up on time, work with people who are different from you, and see that what you do matters to somebody else. Those skills will help us in college and at any job. The community gets something too, like cleaner parks and kids who read better.

For these reasons, I ask you to make community service a requirement with flexible hours and lots of choices. Some students might complain at first, but I think most of them will end up feeling proud of what they did.

Sincerely,
A Concerned Student
