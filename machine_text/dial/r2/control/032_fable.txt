Dear Principal,

I heard the school might make every student do community service. I don't think you should make it a rule. Service is good and you can still tell kids to do it, but forcing us is a bad idea.

My first reason is that helping people only works if you want to be there. My friend Dana tutors second graders in reading two times a week. She knows which kids are stuck on which sounds. One boy will only read about sharks so she brings him shark books every time. Now think about a kid who got sent there because he has to. He would be looking at the clock the whole time and little kids can tell when you don't care about them. So the second grader gets a bad tutor and the older kid just learns that helping people is one more boring thing to get done.

My second reason is time. Not everybody has the same amount. Some kids in my class have nothing to do after school. But other kids have to go home and watch there little brothers and sisters until their mom or dad gets off work. Some of the older kids I know work at the grocery store until nine at night. They are already helping somebody and their family needs them. If you make everyone do forty hours it is way harder for them then for a kid with free afternoons. That isn't fair at all.

Also I worry about the places we would get sent to. The food pantry by my house only has two people working there. If sixty kids showed up and all of us needed a signature, those two people would spend the whole day making up jobs for us and signing papers. They wouldn't get to do there real work. My friends pick up litter on Saturdays and it works because only about ten people come and they all want to be there.

I think there is a better way to do this. You could make service a class we can pick, or let clubs do projects during school. You could give awards to kids who do a lot of hours. You should also count the stuff kids already do for their families, because that is helping too.

Thank you for listening to what a student thinks.

Sincerely,
A concerned student
