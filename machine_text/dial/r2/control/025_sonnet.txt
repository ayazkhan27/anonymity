Dear Principal,

I am writing about making all students do community service. I don't think it should be required, but I think the school should make it easier for kids to volunteer if they want to.

I understand why people like the idea. Some of my friends tutor little kids at the elementary school and they always come back happy. Some other kids pick up trash at the park on Saturdays and the park looks a lot better after. Those kids really care about our town.

But when you force people to do it, its different. Lots of kids in my grade already have homework, sports, jobs, and little brothers and sisters to watch. My friend works at night at a grocery store so her family can pay the rent. If she had to do service hours too, it would just be one more thing and she would probably be mad about it. Also, kids who only volunteer to get the hours done usually just do the bare minimum and don't really try.

There is another problem which is how it would work. Tutoring and cleanups need adults to plan them and watch everybody. If hundreds of students all need a spot at the same time, the school and the groups in town could get overwhelmed. Some kids would end up with jobs that are pointless and don't help anyone.

I think a better idea is to encourage service instead of forcing it. The school could have a volunteer fair where local groups come and students can sign up. You could also give awards at assemblies to students who do service, and let kids count the service they do at school events. If students find something they like doing, they will keep doing it even after they graduate.

Thank you for reading my letter. I hope our school can get students excited to help the community instead of just making them do it.

Sincerely,
A Concerned Student
