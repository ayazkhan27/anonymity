Dear Principal,

I heard your trying to decide between two cell phone policys. I think you should pick Policy 1, where we can bring our phones and use them at lunch and free time but we have to keep them off during class.

My first reason is safety. Alot of parents want to be able to get ahold of their kids during the day. Sometimes students need to call home too, like if practice gets canceled or there ride doesnt show up. If phones were totally banned we couldnt do that. And if there was ever a real emergency it would be really important to call our family fast.

My second reason is Policy 1 still keeps class time for learning. The phones have to be off in class anyway so teachers still get our attention. Taking our phones away at lunch doesnt help anything in the classroom. It just punishes kids on there own time.

Another reason is that a ban would be really hard to enforce. Kids are still going to sneak phones in their pockets and backpacks, trust me. Then teachers would waste time looking for phones instead of teaching us. If the rule is fair more students will actually follow it.

Last, Policy 1 teaches us to be responsible. When we get older and go to college or get a job nobody is going to take our phones away. We have to learn when to put them down on our own. Letting us practice at school helps get us ready for that.

So for all of these reasons I think you should choose Policy 1. It keeps classes focused and its fair to students too.

Sincerely,
A Concerned Student
