Dear Principal,

I am writing about if students should have to do community service. I think every student should have to do it, but only if the hours arent crazy and we get to pick what we do.

First, community service teaches you stuff you cant learn in class. My friend Maya tutors second graders at Lincoln Elementary two times a week. She said teaching fractions to a little kid made her actually understand them better. She also learned to be patient with the kids who get mad when they dont get it. You need stuff like that for pretty much any job when your older.

Second, it makes you care more about where you live. Last spring me and my friends picked up trash by the creek behind the park. We filled like twenty bags in one afternoon. Now when I walk by the creek and I see trash I pick it up. Honestly I never cared about that place until we cleaned it.

I know some kids are going to say they are already to busy with homework and sports and stuff, and some of them have jobs. Thats true and its fair. But if it was only like twenty hours a year, thats not even half an hour a week. Plus the school could count stuff kids already do, like coaching a little kids team or helping with a food drive at church. That way it wouldnt feel like extra work.

Making community service required would help our town and it would make us better people. Please think about making it something you have to do to graduate from our school.

Sincerely,
A Concerned Student
