Dear Principal,

I dont think you should make the rule that students need a B average to play sports or do activities. I get that you want kids to try harder in school but I think this rule would hurt the kids its supposed to help.

First, alot of kids at our school have a C average and a C is still passing. Those kids are doing there work. If you take away the thing that makes them want to come to school they might care even less about grades, not more. For some kids practice after school is the only reason they want to get up in the morning. My friend is like that and if he couldnt play basketball I honestly dont know if he would come.

Second, sports and clubs teach you stuff that isnt on your report card. When your on the soccer team or in drama club you learn how to work with other people and be responsible and how to lose without getting mad. You also have to manage your time because you have homework and practice on the same night. You need those things for a job someday and just for life. A kid with a C needs them just as much as a kid with an A, maybe even more.

Third, coaches and club advisors actually care about the students. Alot of times a coach is the one who notices when a kid is having a hard time and tells them to go get help. If you kick those kids off the team they lose that person to.

Instead of a B rule I think you should keep the C rule and start a tutoring program for players whos grades start dropping. That way kids can still play and get help so there grades go up. Some of the older players could even be the tutors.

Thank you for asking what students think. I hope you let every student who is passing keep doing there activities.

Sincerely,
A Concerned Student
