Dear Principal,

I think Policy 1 is the better choice. Letting us have our phones at lunch and in our free time, as long as they are off in class, is fair and the school can still run fine.

First of all, its about safety. If theres an emergency or the bus is late or my plans after school change, I need to be able to text my mom. If phones are banned all day parents are going to worry alot more.

Another reason is it teaches us responsibility. We have to decide when its ok to use a phone and when to put it away. We will need to do that in high school and at a job someday so we should get to practice now.

Also a full ban would be really hard to enforce. Teachers would waste time looking for phones and arguing with kids instead of teaching. Policy 1 is simple. Phones are off in class, and if you break the rule you lose your phone.

Lunch is the only time all day we can relax, talk to our friends, or check in with our family. I think that break helps us pay attention better in the afternoon classes.

So for these reasons, I hope you pick Policy 1.

Sincerely,
A Concerned Student
