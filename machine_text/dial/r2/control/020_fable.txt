Dear Principal Morales,

I heard that you are thinking about making every student do community service. I think you should do it. But it shouldn't be a ton of hours and we should get to pick what we do.

My first reason is that most kids are never going to try community service unless somebody makes them. Last spring my friend Dana started tutoring second graders at Lincoln Elementary. She only went because her older sister made her. She thought it was going to be so boring. Then she spent ten weeks helping a boy named Marcus sound out his words, and by May he was reading chapter books to her! Now Dana says she wants to be a teacher. She never would of known that if she didn't go, and I bet a lot of us are like that too.

My second reason is that it helps the town and you can see it with your own eyes. Some people in my neighborhood pick up trash on Creek Road one Saturday a month. Before they started, the ditch was full of bottles and fast food bags and it was gross. Now families go for walks there. Our school has around nine hundred students. If everybody did just twenty hours a year, that is eighteen thousand hours of tutoring and cleaning up and visiting nursing homes. Our town doesn't have the money to pay people for all that.

I know some kids are against this because my own friends told me. They have sports and jobs and little brothers and sisters to watch. They also have homework that keeps them up late already. I think that is fair, and it's why the rule has to be flexible. Twenty hours in a whole school year is only like half an hour a week. Kids should be allowed to finish in the summer if they want to. Also the school should count a lot of different things, like helping at a church food pantry or coaching little kids in soccer. If somebody babysits every day after school, they could do one weekend project and be done with it.

Some people also say that if you are forced to do it then it isn't real service. I don't agree with that. We are forced to take math, and nobody says the stuff we learn in math is fake. The rule just gets us in the door. After that it is up to us what we do with it.

I hope you will say yes to this and keep it flexible for us.

Sincerely,
An eighth grade student
