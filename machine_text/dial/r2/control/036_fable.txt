Dear Principal,

I heard that you might make community service required for every student. I think that is a bad idea. Helping people is good but if you force us to do it then it is just like more homework.

My friend Dana tutors second graders in reading every Thursday. She does it because she likes those kids a lot, and they can tell. But what if somebody only goes because he needs his form signed? He would probably just look at the clock the whole time. The little kid would know he didn't want to be there and then nobody gets anything out of it.

Also some kids don't have time. There are kids in my class who have jobs after school because their family needs the money. Other kids have to watch their little brothers and sisters until their mom gets home at like seven. They don't have extra hours so the rule would be hardest on them, and that isn't fair.

I think you should just make it easier to volunteer instead. You could put up a list of places that need help and give credit to the people who do it. Then a lot of us would sign up anyway.

Sincerely,
A student
