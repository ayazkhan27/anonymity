Dear Principal,

I am writing about if every student should have to do community service. I think our school should make it a rule, but it should be flexable because kids are already really busy.

Some of my friends already volunteer and I seen what it does for them. My friend Maya helps second graders with reading every Tuesday after school. At first she only did it because her mom made her. Now she talks about "her kids" all the time and she gets super excited when one of them finishes a chapter book. She is more patient now and she isnt as scared to talk in front of people. My other friend Daniel goes with a group on weekends that picks up trash in the creek behind the shopping center. He said he never knew how much trash was in our town until he spent a whole morning pulling bottles and plastic bags out of the water. Now he picks up trash even when nobody is looking. Stuff like that teaches you responsibility in a way sitting in class cant.

But I also get why some kids dont like the idea. Alot of us have jobs, sports practice, music lessons, or little brothers and sisters to babysit. Homework already takes forever every night. If the school made us do like fifty hours a semester some kids would fall behind or not get enough sleep, and it would feel like a punishment instead of helping. That kind of ruins the whole point.

Thats why I think the rule should be reasonable. Maybe twenty hours a year would be enough, and students could pick when and how they do it. If someone works on weekdays they could help at a food bank on Saturday. If someone plays a sport they could coach a little kids team in the off season. The school could even count stuff kids already do, like helping at a church event or reading to people at the hospital. If students get to choose then the hours would actually mean something and not feel forced.

Making it required would also help kids who would never volunteer on there own just because they dont know how to start. Alot of people only find out they like helping after they try it.

Our community gives us alot, like parks, libraries, and teachers. A small flexable requirement would teach students to give back. I hope you think about it.

Sincerely,
A concerned student
