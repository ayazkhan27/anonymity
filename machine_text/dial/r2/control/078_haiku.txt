Dear Principal,

I think we should not require a B average to be in sports and other activities. Some people say it will make students study more, but I think it would hurt the kids who need help the most.

A lot of students with a C average are only struggling in one or two classes. They are not failing everything. For those kids, sports, band, or debate is usually the reason they come to school and keep trying. It is where they make friends with teachers and other students. If you take that away, you also take away the thing that could help their grades get better.

Activities also teach things that tests do not really measure, like working on a team, managing your time, and staying calm when things get stressful. A lot of students come back to their homework with more focus after learning those things.

Instead of kicking students out of activities, the school could offer tutoring, study halls, or check-ins with teachers for anyone whose grades start to drop. That would help the struggling students instead of shutting them out.

Thank you for listening to what students think.

Sincerely,
A Concerned Student
