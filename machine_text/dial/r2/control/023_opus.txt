Dear Principal Morgan,

I am writing to you about if every student should have to do community service. I think you should make it required because it helps students and it helps our town to, and I think thats worth the time.

First, community service teaches you stuff you cant learn in class. Last spring my friend Danielle started tutoring second graders at Lincoln Elementary two times a week. She told me that explaining subtraction to a seven year old actually made her better at math, and she got alot more patient too. My friend Marcus joined a group that picks up trash at Riverside Park. He said he never really thought about where trash goes until he pulled like forty plastic bottles out of the creek in one afternoon. Stuff like that changes how kids think about being responsible. Alot of kids would never try something like that unless they had to.

Second, our town really needs the help. The elementary school has alot of kids who are behind in reading and the teachers cant help all of them one on one. The food bank on Main Street doesnt have enough volunteers on the weekends. If every student at our school did even a few hours a semester that would be thousands of hours of help. That would make a big difference for the people who need those places.

I know some of my friends dont like this idea. They have homework and sports and jobs and stuff they have to do for there families, and they are worried they wont have time. I get that, but I think there is a way to fix it. The rule could be small, like ten hours a semester, and kids could pick projects that work with there schedules. Some of it could even be during school, like reading to little kids during a free period. That way its a little bit for everyone but not to much for anyone.

Last, if its required then service becomes part of our school. When everybody does it its not just a few kids anymore, its something we can all be proud of together.

So thats why I hope you will make community service required for all students. A few hours from each of us can teach us things we will remember and it can help the people in our town who need it the most.

Sincerely,
Jordan Ellis
