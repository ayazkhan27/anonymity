I agree with the principal that every student should have to do at least one extracurricular activity. Some people say it will take time away from homework or make kids do stuff they dont like. But I think it will help students way more than it hurts them. Activities teach you things you cant learn in class, they make you feel like you belong at school, and they help you figure out what you actually like.

First, activities teach you stuff you cant really get from a textbook. Like if you play soccer you learn you have to go to practice even when its freezing out and nobody wants to run. If you do yearbook you learn about deadlines because the pages have to be done by a certain day no matter what. Kids in student council learn how to talk in front of people and listen when people complain and also compromise when not everybody agrees. Colleges and jobs want people who can do that stuff. A lot of kids would never practice it if they just went to class and then went home.

Second, activities make students feel like they belong. A big school can be really lonely, especially if your new or shy. When you join something like chess club or drama, all of a sudden there are people who expect to see you and they notice if your not there. My cousin moved to a new town in ninth grade and didnt know anybody. She only joined cross country because a teacher kept bugging her about it, but by October she had a whole group of friends to sit with at lunch. Also kids who feel connected to there school usually get better grades and dont skip as much.

Third, if its required, kids might find out they like things they never even thought about. Alot of teenagers dont try new things because there nervous or they think they will be bad at it. But if everyone has to pick something, a kid who thinks he hates writing might find out he loves taking pictures for the school newspaper. That could even change what he wants to do when hes older.

But the rule should be kind of flexible. Some students have jobs or have to help out there family, so they should get options that fit there schedule. And there should be enough choices so everybody can find something they like. If the school does that, then I think the principal made a good choice and I hope other students support it to.
