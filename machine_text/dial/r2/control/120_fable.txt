I think you should ask more then one person before you make a big choice. I know this because of my cousin Dana. She is a nurse and last year she had to decide if she was going to quit her job and take a travel nurse job in Arizona. She asked like five people what they thought. At first I thought she was wasting time and was just scared to pick. But then I saw it really helped her so I changed my mind.

My first reason is one person doesn't know everything. Dana's mom told her to stay because the hospital has good benefits. Thats true but her mom never did a travel job. A lady Dana works with did two of them. She told Dana stuff nobody else knew, like you have to pay taxes on the housing money and its hard to make friends when you move every thirteen weeks. So everybody only knew a little part and Dana needed all of it.

My second reason is people want different things. Her mom wanted her to stay close to home. The recruiter gets paid more if Dana signs up. Her best friend said she missed her already and she didn't even leave yet. They were not lying, but they all had there own reason for saying what they said. If Dana only listened to the recruiter she would only hear the good stuff. When she heard all five of them she could tell what was a fact and what was just what that person wanted.

My third reason is if people who don't agree on anything all say the same thing, then it is probably right. Almost everybody told Dana to read the part in the contract about canceling. She read it and it said the company could end her job with only one week notice. She made them change it to two weeks.

Also asking people is not the same thing as letting them decide for you. Dana still picked for herself. She didn't even do what her mom said. She took the job and she kept her old license just in case. She came back eight months later and she had money saved up and she was happy she did it.

So in conclusion one opinion is kind of like a guess. A lot of opinions is more like a map, because you can see more of where your going.
