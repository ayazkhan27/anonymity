Dear Principal,

I heard you might change the cell phone policy at our school. I think you should go with Policy 1, the one where we can bring our phones and use them at lunch and free time but they have to be off in class. Here is why I think that.

First, kids need phones to talk to their parents. A lot of us don't go straight home after school. I have practice until five some days and my mom's work hours change every week, so she doesn't always know when she can pick me up. If practice gets cancelled or my ride can't come I have to tell her somehow. I know there is a phone in the office but its only one phone and there are hundreds of kids here. Parents want to reach us too. If you ban phones we would still have to figure all that out, it would just be way harder.

Second, I don't think Policy 2 would even work. Phones are small. Kids who want them are going to hide them in a pocket or in their locker. Then teachers have to spend time looking for phones and taking them away and arguing with kids, when they could be teaching. Also if almost everybody is breaking a rule then kids start to think rules don't really matter. Policy 1 is fair so most kids will follow it. Then teachers only have to deal with the few kids who don't.

Another reason is Policy 1 still keeps phones out of class. The whole problem with phones is they distract people during lessons. Both policies stop that. With Policy 1 if a teacher sees your phone or hears it in class they can take it till the end of the day. Nobody wants that because then you don't have it at lunch.

My last reason is we need to learn when to put our phones away on our own. When we get jobs or go to college nobody is going to lock up our phones for us. We will just have to know to turn it off in a meeting and check it on a break. With Policy 1 we can practice that now, while there are still teachers around to tell us when we mess up. I think that is better than never learning it.

I know you want students to pay attention in class and I want that too. Policy 1 does that without taking away something our families really need. Please think about it before you decide.

Sincerely,
A student
