I think when you have a hard choice to make you should ask more then one person for advice. Everybody sees things different because of there own life, so one person's opinion is only part of it.

Like say a girl is trying to pick between soccer and the school play. Her coach would probably tell her to do soccer because he knows she is good at it. Her drama teacher would say the play will make her more confident. But her older brother did both and he might tell her the practices are at the same time alot and she will be super tired. Everybody tells her something different, and when she puts it all together she can see what actually matters to her.

Asking more people also helps you catch mistakes. If your friend says a used car is a really good deal, another friend who knows about engines might see a leak the first one didnt even notice.

Last, hearing different opinions makes you think for yourself. You cant just do what one person says. You have to look at the reasons and figure out what works for you and your life. Thats how you make a smart choice and not regret it later.
