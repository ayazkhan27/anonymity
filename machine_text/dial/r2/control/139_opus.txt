When people have a hard decision to make, most of them just ask one person they trust, like their mom or their best friend. That is okay, but I think it is better to ask a few different people. One person only sees things one way, and other people might know stuff they don't know.

My first reason is that everybody knows different things. For example, say a senior in high school is trying to pick a college. Her parents will probably talk about how much it costs and how far away it is. Her teacher might know which school is good for what she wants to study. And if she has an older friend who already goes there, the friend can tell her what the dorms and classes are really like. None of them knows everything, but if she talks to all of them she gets way more of the picture.

My second reason is that asking more people helps you tell when someone is biased. People give advice based on what happened to them. If a guy lost a lot of money opening a restaurant, he is probably going to tell everyone not to start a business. But if his friend opened one and it went great, he might make it sound really easy. If you only listened to one of them you would get the wrong idea. If you listen to both of them, you figure out that it depends on things like planning and luck.

My third reason is that it matters if people agree or not. If three doctors all tell you to get the same treatment, you can feel pretty sure it is the right one. If they all say different things, then you know it is not an easy choice and you should ask more questions before you decide. Either way you learn something you wouldn't have learned from only one doctor.

Last, explaining your problem to different people actually helps you too. Every time you tell somebody about it, you have to get your thoughts in order. Sometimes you find out what you really want just from talking about it, or from noticing which advice makes you feel better and which advice makes you nervous.

Asking other people doesn't mean they get to make the choice for you, though. In the end it is still your decision because you are the one who has to live with it. But if you listen to a few people first, you will probably make a better choice than if you only asked one person. That is why I think asking more than one person is the smart thing to do.
