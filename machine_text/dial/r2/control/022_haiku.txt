Dear Principal,

I think students should have to do community service before they graduate. I know that sounds like a lot, but I really think it would be good for everyone. Helping other people teaches us stuff we can't always learn in class.

Like, think about the kids in my class who tutor at the elementary school. They have to be patient, and they have to explain things that they used to find hard. Then they realize they can actually teach somebody, which is kind of cool. Students who pick up trash at the city park learn that what they do affects the places other people live and play. These experiences help us become more responsible and more caring. Colleges and employers look for that kind of stuff.

I know some people worry that service takes time away from homework, sports, jobs, and sleep. But a requirement of about twenty hours spread over four years is not very much. Students already spend tons of hours on things that only help themselves, so a little time for others would not ruin anybody's schedule. A lot of kids also end up liking the thing they thought they would hate the most.

To make it fair, the school could give us lots of choices, like tutoring, food drives, park cleanups, or volunteering at the hospital. That way everyone can find something that fits what they like and what they are good at.

Thank you for thinking about this. I believe a community is stronger when its young people learn to give back, and I think our school is a good place to start teaching that.

Sincerely,
A Concerned Student
