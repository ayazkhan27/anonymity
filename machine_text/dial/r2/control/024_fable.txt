Dear Principal Morris,

I heard you are trying to decide if all students should have to do community service. I think you should make us do it, but not a ton of hours. Maybe like ten a semester.

My friend Dana tutors second graders at Lincoln Elementary every Tuesday. She only started because her church group asked her to, and she complained about it for like a month. But now she is always talking about this boy Marcus. In September he couldn't even read a whole page and in March he finished a chapter book. She never would of met him if nobody made her sign up. I think alot of kids are like that. Nobody asks us to volunteer so we don't, and then we just figure it would be boring.

I know some people don't agree with me. Some of my friends have jobs after school or they have to watch their little brothers and sisters, so they say they don't have time. I get that, and thats why it should be a small amount. Ten hours is only like one Saturday morning a month. Also kids could count stuff they already do, like coaching little kids soccer or helping at a food pantry.

Another reason is the park behind our school. It has trash all over it and nobody ever cleans it up. If every student did a few hours there it would look way better by spring.

So please make community service required, but let us pick what we do.

Sincerely,
A student
