Dear Principal,

I am writing to ask you to pick Policy 1. This policy says students can bring their phones to school and use them at lunch and in their free time, but the phones have to be off during class.

The first reason is that a ban would be really hard to enforce. Pretty much every kid has a phone, so a lot of people would just hide theirs in their backpack or their pocket. Then the teachers would waste class time trying to find the phones and hand out punishments instead of teaching us. If students know they can check their phones at lunch, they probably won't try to sneak them out in the middle of a lesson.

Another reason is safety. Lots of students have parents who work late, or little brothers and sisters they have to pick up, or a health problem they need to keep track of. If something bad happens, a student should be able to call their family right away. With Policy 2 a student would have to go to the office to find a phone, and that could take a long time. With Policy 1 the phone is right there when you really need it.

Also, Policy 1 helps students learn to be responsible. One day we will have jobs or go to college, and phones will be everywhere and nobody is going to take them away from us. Learning to put your phone away when it's time to focus is something we need to practice now. A ban just puts off that lesson until later. Policy 1 lets us show every day that we can be trusted.

I know some people are worried that phones cause distractions, cyberbullying, and cheating. Those are real problems, and that is why Policy 1 keeps phones off in class. The school could also make clear punishments for kids who misuse their phones, like losing their phone privileges for a week. That way only the students who break the rules get in trouble, and everybody else doesn't.

For these reasons, I hope you will choose Policy 1. It keeps class time for learning, but it also respects what students need and helps us learn to be responsible.

Sincerely,
A Concerned Student
