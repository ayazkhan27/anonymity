Dear Principal,

I think our school should make every student do community service, but only if it isnt to many hours.

Some of my friends tutor second graders at Lincoln Elementary after school. At first they only went because a teacher told them to. Now they talk about "their kids" all the time and one of them even wants to be a teacher when she grows up. She never would of found that out if nobody made her try it.

Some other friends spent a Saturday picking up trash by the creek behind the park. They filled like twenty bags! The next week people were walking there dogs there again. Just a couple hours of work made it better for the whole neighborhood.

I know some kids are worried that service will take time away from homework or sports or jobs. That makes sense. But if it was only ten or fifteen hours a year and you could pick when and where you do it, nobody would get overloaded.

Community service teaches kids to be responsible and it also helps our town. So please make it part of our school.

Sincerely,
A concerned student
