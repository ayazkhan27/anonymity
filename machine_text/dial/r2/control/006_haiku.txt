Dear Principal,

I think we should pick Policy 1. That policy lets students bring phones to school and use them at lunch and during free periods, but phones have to stay off during class. I know a full ban seems easier to do, and I get why people like it. But if we make kids leave their phones at the door, we never learn how to use them the right way, and that is something we will need for the rest of our lives.

First, phones help students stay in touch with their families. A lot of kids have parents who work late, or brothers and sisters at a different school, or a ride home that changes at the last minute. My friend missed the bus one time and had to call her mom from the front office. She could only do that because she had her phone. Under Policy 2, kids like her would have no real way to reach home during the day.

Second, a ban does not make phones go away. It just hides them. People would stick phones in their bags, in the bathroom, or in their lockers. Everybody would spend more time sneaking around than they would spend on their phones at lunch. Policy 1 is a simple rule that is easy to understand. Phones are away in class and free to use outside of it, so nobody has to guess.

Third, free time is when most students talk to friends, check the news, or look up their schedule. Those things are normal. If phones stay in our pockets during lunch, kids can choose how to spend their break. It also shows the school trusts us to act responsibly instead of treating us like we are going to cause trouble.

Finally, I think the school should teach us how to manage our phones instead of just taking them away. With Policy 1, teachers can talk to us about phone rules in class. They can talk about how much screen time is too much, and they can step in when a phone is distracting someone. A total ban shuts down that conversation before it can even start, and then nobody learns anything about it.

So I think Policy 1 is the better choice. It keeps students connected to their families, and it is easier to enforce than a ban that people will just work around. It also helps us get ready to use technology with good judgment. I hope you will think about it and talk with the other teachers before you decide.

Respectfully,

A Student
