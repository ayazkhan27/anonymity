Dear Principal,

I think you should go with Policy 1. Thats the one where we can bring our phones to school and use them at lunch and free periods, but they have to be off during class.

My first reason is safety. Alot of kids walk home or ride the bus, and some of us stay after for practice. Sometimes plans change and our parents need to get ahold of us. One time last year my ride forgot to pick me up and I couldn't even call my mom. A phone sitting at home doesn't help with that at all.

Another reason is that Policy 1 teaches us to be responsible. We need to learn when to put our phones away, because someday we will have jobs and have to do it there too. If you just ban phones then we never learn that.

Also Policy 2 would be really hard to do. Teachers would have to check backpacks instead of teaching, and kids would probably just hide there phones anyway.

Please give us a chance to show you we can be trusted.

Sincerely,
A Student
