When my cousin was in tenth grade she broke her leg and missed almost five weeks of school. Her teachers sent home packets of worksheets, but nobody ever explained the chemistry to her. By the time she came back she was so far behind that she ended up retaking the whole class in summer school. If her school had let her join class by video, she probably would have missed only a couple of days. That is the biggest reason I think students should have the option to attend classes from home.

Notice that I said option. I don't think every student should be on a laptop all year, because that would be bad for a lot of people. I just think the choice should be there for the kids who need it. Some students get sick for a long time or get hurt like my cousin. Some live far out and ride the bus an hour each way, which is two hours a day of pretty much nothing. There are also students with really bad anxiety, and sitting in a loud, crowded cafeteria makes it hard for them to focus the rest of the day. For students like these the choice isn't really home or school. It's learning at home or not learning at all.

Learning from home also lets students work in the way that fits them. In a regular class the teacher explains something once and moves on. A lot of online lessons are recorded, so if you didn't get a math problem you can watch it again and you don't have to pretend you understood. Some students have jobs after school to help their families, and they could do their work at night. It could help small schools too. A small school might not have a teacher for the third year of a language or for advanced physics, but its students could take that class online with a teacher from somewhere else.

Some people say that students at home will get distracted or lonely. I think they are partly right. It is hard to pay attention when your bed and your phone are right next to you, and you can't really make friends through a screen. But all that proves is that online class is wrong for some students. It doesn't prove that nobody should be allowed to do it. Schools could take attendance, make students keep their cameras on during discussions, and require a certain grade to stay in the program. If a student starts failing, then they have to come back to the building.

In conclusion, school is supposed to be about learning, not about which chair you are sitting in. My cousin lost a whole summer because of a broken leg, and that didn't need to happen. Letting students attend from home would help more of them keep learning when something in their life goes wrong.
