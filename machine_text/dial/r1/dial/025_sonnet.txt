Dear Principal,

I am writing about whether all students should have to do community service. I think it should not be mandatory, but the school should make it easier for students to volunteer.

I get why a requirement sounds like a good idea. Some of my friends tutor kids at the elementary school on Tuesdays. Other people spend a Saturday picking up trash at the park. The park looks cleaner after that, and I can see those students are doing something for the town.

But the same service is different when students are forced to do it. A lot of my classmates already have homework, sports, jobs, and little brothers or sisters to watch. One girl I know works nights at a grocery store so her family can pay rent. If the school adds a number of hours she has to finish, it is one more thing on her list, and she would probably not be happy about it. When someone only volunteers to check a box, they usually do the minimum. The people they are helping can tell if a student is not there on purpose.

Another thing is the planning. Tutoring and cleanups need adults to organize them. If hundreds of students all need a place to volunteer at the same time, the school and the local groups might not be able to handle it. Some students would end up doing busywork that does not help anyone.

I think a better plan is to encourage service instead of ordering it. The school could have a volunteer fair where local groups come and sign up students. Teachers could mention the students who volunteer at the assemblies. Service could also count if it is done at school events, so students don't have to find extra time. If a student finds something they like, they will probably keep doing it after they graduate.

Thank you for reading my letter. I hope the school can get more students to volunteer without making it a rule.

Sincerely,
A Concerned Student
