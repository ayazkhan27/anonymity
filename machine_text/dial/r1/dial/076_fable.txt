Dear Principal,

I am writing about the idea to make students have a B average to be in sports and other activities. I get why people want it, since school comes first. I still think the rule should stay at a C.

First, a C is passing. A kid with a C average is doing what the school asks. A lot of us study every night and that is where we end up, mostly in classes like algebra or science. The grade might be a C, but the effort is real and it is honest work.

Second, activities are what keep some kids going in class. My friend plays soccer and he does his homework on the bus, because the coach checks grades every Friday. If you took soccer away from him, he would go home and sit on his phone. He does the homework for the team.

Third, you learn things on a team that you do not get in a classroom. You have to show up on time. You have to work with people you did not pick. You have to finish the season even when the scores go the other way.

Also, so many students have a C that the teams and clubs would get small. Some of them would not have enough people to keep going.

I think there is a better plan. Keep the C rule, and add tutoring after school for students whose grades are low. Coaches could help by giving players time to go. Grades would go up, and kids would still have the thing they love about school. I hope you will think about it, because I believe it would be good for the students and great for our school.

Sincerely,
A concerned student
