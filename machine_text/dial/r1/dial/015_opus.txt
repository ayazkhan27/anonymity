Dear Principal,

I know you are trying to pick between two cell phone policies, and I think Policy 1 is the best choice. Students should be allowed to bring phones and use them at lunch and free time, as long as they are turned off in class.

The first reason is safety. Lots of parents want to be able to reach their kids during the day. Students also need a way to call home if practice gets canceled, if their ride falls through, or if something bad happens. Banning phones would take that away. In a real emergency, being able to call your family fast is really important, and nobody wants to be stuck and scared with no help.

Second, Policy 1 already protects class time, which is the whole point of a ban. If phones have to be off during class, teachers still get our attention. Taking them away at lunch does not help learning at all. It just punishes students during time that is supposed to be ours, and that feels unfair.

Third, a full ban would be hard to enforce. Kids are still going to sneak phones in their pockets and backpacks. Then teachers would waste time searching for phones instead of teaching, which is a big problem. A simple rule that makes sense is easier to follow, and students will actually respect it.

Last, Policy 1 teaches responsibility. After we graduate, nobody is going to take our phones away at a job or in college. We have to learn when to put them down ourselves. Practicing that at school is a good way to get ready for the real world.

For all these reasons, please choose Policy 1. It keeps class focused and still respects our safety and freedom.

Sincerely,
A Concerned Student
