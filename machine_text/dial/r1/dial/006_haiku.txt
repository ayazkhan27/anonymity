Dear Principal,

I think you should choose Policy 1. It lets students bring phones to school and use them at lunch and other free periods, as long as the phones stay off during class. A full ban sounds simple, but it is a bad idea. Students love their phones, and taking them away at the door does not teach anyone anything useful.

First, phones help students stay in touch with their families. Many kids have parents who work late, brothers or sisters at other schools, or a ride home that changes at the last minute. A phone lets a student confirm a pickup, call a parent after a scary moment, or tell family they are safe. Under Policy 2, those students would have no easy way to reach home during the school day, and that is a real problem.

Second, a ban does not really get phones off campus. It just hides them. Students would stash devices in bags, bathrooms, and lockers, and all that sneaking would be much worse than a few minutes of fun scrolling at lunch. Policy 1 is a clear rule that is easy to follow: phones away in class, free time outside it.

Third, free time is when students hang out, read the news, or check their schedules. Those are good habits. Keeping phones in pockets during lunch lets students pick how to spend their break, and it shows that we are responsible kids, not criminals.

Finally, schools should teach students how to handle their devices, not just hide the problem. Policy 1 gives teachers a great chance to talk about phone rules in class, talk with students about screen habits, and step in when a phone gets distracting. A total ban shuts that conversation down before it even starts.

For these reasons, I believe Policy 1 is the better choice. It keeps students close to their families, it is easier to enforce than a ban that students will hate and sneak around, and it helps us grow into people who use technology with good judgment. I truly hope you will say yes, because this would make our school a happier and more trusting place.

Respectfully,

A Student
