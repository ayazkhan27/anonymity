Dear Principal,

I think students should have to do community service. Some of my friends tutor little kids at the elementary school, and others help clean up litter in the neighborhood parks. These jobs matter because they help people and show students how our town works outside of class.

Some kids say service takes time away from homework, sports, or jobs. That is a problem, and it can be hard to fit in. But it is only a few hours a month, and most students can plan it around their schedules. Along the way they build patience, teamwork, and responsibility, which will help them at school and at work later.

Requiring service also makes things fair. When everyone helps, nobody gets left out, and the school becomes a place where people look out for each other. I hope you will approve this requirement, because a caring school is a fair and happy school.

Sincerely,
A Concerned Student
