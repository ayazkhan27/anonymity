Dear Principal,

I am writing to ask you not to make every student do community service. I think service is a good idea, but it should be up to each person.

Some of my friends tutor little kids at the elementary school. One of them told me that when a second grader finally read a hard word, it was the best part of her week. Another friend picks up trash by the creek behind our school. He likes seeing the creek look clean after. They both signed up on their own, so they show up on time and do the work.

If the school makes it a rule, that will change. A lot of students already have jobs after school to pay for bills. Others watch their younger brothers and sisters, or they have practice and homework every night. I know kids who are really tired already. A required number of hours would just be one more thing on a long list, and it would be a problem for them. When people feel forced, they usually do the bare minimum. They sign the sheet and leave early. That does not help anybody, and some kids might hate volunteering after that.

A better plan is to make it easy to join. The school could have a sign-up fair with local groups, run projects on weekends or at lunch, and recognize volunteers at assemblies. More students would sign up if they felt invited instead of pushed.

Thank you for reading my letter.

Sincerely,
A concerned student
