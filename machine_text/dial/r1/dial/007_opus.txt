Dear Principal,

I am writing about the cell phone policy you are thinking about changing. I think Policy 1 is the better choice. Students should be able to bring phones to school and use them at lunch and during free periods, as long as the phones stay off in class.

My first reason is safety. A lot of parents want to be able to reach their kids during the day, and kids need a way to call home when plans change. If practice gets cancelled or someone feels sick, one text fixes it. If there is a real emergency, like a lockdown or a bus accident, a phone could let a family know their kid is okay. Taking phones away completely would take that away too.

My second reason is that Policy 1 teaches responsibility. In a few years we will be in college or at jobs where nobody takes our phones. We will have to decide on our own when to put them down. Using phones at lunch and keeping them off in class gives us practice with that kind of self-control. A total ban just teaches kids to sneak.

Third, a full ban would be hard to enforce. Kids will still bring phones in their pockets and backpacks, and teachers would waste time searching for them. With Policy 1 the rule is clear, so most students will follow it. Teachers only have to step in if a phone comes out during class.

Also, phones can help during free time. Students can check assignments online, look things up for a project, or text their group partners. Lunch is a time to relax, and a few minutes of music or a message from your family does not hurt anyone.

Some people say phones will distract students. Policy 1 already handles that by keeping phones off when we are learning. Students who break the rule can get in trouble, and everyone else still gets to use their phones the right way.

For these reasons, I really hope you pick Policy 1. It keeps us safe, it helps us grow up, and it is very fair and easy to follow. I think students would be so happy with it, and I know we would show you we can handle it. Thank you for listening to us!

Sincerely,
A Concerned Student
