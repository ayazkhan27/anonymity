Dear Principal,

I am writing to share my thoughts on community service. I think every student should do it, and I think it is a great idea if the rules are fair and kids get good choices.

Some of my friends already volunteer, and I can tell it makes them happy. One friend tutors second graders in reading every Thursday. She loves it. At first she only did it for her club hours, and now she is always talking about her cute students and making fun lesson plans on her own. Another friend joined a weekend group that cleans the creek behind our school. He says it feels great to see the creek look so nice, and now he is proud to pick up every wrapper he sees. Neither of them would have found these good things if nobody had given them a push.

I know some kids think it is a bad idea. We have sports, jobs, and family chores, and our homework is already a lot. Nobody wants a hard rule that feels like a problem. That is not a reason to skip the whole plan, though. The school can ask for a small number of hours, maybe ten each semester, and let kids count the nice things they already do, like helping at a church food drive or watching a little cousin. A flexible plan is fair and easy for busy students, and it is not scary at all.

Community service also teaches good lessons that books can't teach. Students learn to be on time, to work with all kinds of people, and to be kind and helpful. Those are great habits for college and for any job. The town gets happy help too, like clean parks and kids who love to read.

So I hope you will pick a plan with fun choices and flexible hours. Some students may not love it at first, and that is fine. I think most of them will feel glad and proud, and they will be happy they tried. I would love to help make it a good program for our school.

Sincerely,
A Concerned Student
