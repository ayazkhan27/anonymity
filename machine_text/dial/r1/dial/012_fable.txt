Dear Principal,

I think you should pick Policy 1. It lets students bring phones to school and use them at lunch and free periods, as long as they are off in class.

My first reason is safety. A lot of us walk home, ride the bus, or stay after for practice. Plans change, and parents need to reach us. Some people say the office phone works, but it is a problem when the office is closed at five.

My second reason is responsibility. We will have phones at every job, so we should learn now when to put them away. A ban just moves that lesson to later.

Also, Policy 2 would be really hard to enforce. Teachers would have to check backpacks, and kids would hide phones anyway.

Policy 1 keeps phones out of class and still lets us use them on our own time. However, students who break the rule should lose the phone for the day.

Sincerely,
A Student
