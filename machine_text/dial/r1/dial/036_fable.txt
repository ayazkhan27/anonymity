Dear Principal,

I am writing to ask you not to make community service a rule for every student. Service is good, but it turns into a chore when it is forced.

My friend Dana tutors second graders in reading every Thursday. She goes because she wants to, and the kids can tell. Now think about a student who only goes to get a form signed. He watches the clock the whole hour. The kid he is with sees that, and then the hour is a waste for both of them.

Time is the other thing. Some of my classmates work after school so their families can pay bills. Others watch their little brothers and sisters until a parent gets home at seven. They are already really tired. A set number of hours would hit them the most, because they have the least room in their week.

You could post openings and give credit to people who sign up. Some of us would do it, but a rule would only make the rest of us resent it.

Sincerely,
A student
