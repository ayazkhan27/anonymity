Dear Principal,

I think you should not make students have a B average to be in sports and other activities. A grade rule might seem like it helps people study more, but it would mostly hurt the students who need help the most.

A lot of students with a C average are only struggling in one or two classes. For them, sports, band, or debate is often the reason they come to school and stay connected with teachers and friends. If you take those activities away, you also take away the structure that could help their grades get better.

Activities also teach things that classrooms do not always test, like teamwork, time management, and how to handle pressure. Students who learn these things often go back to homework with more focus.

Instead of cutting students off, the school could offer tutoring, study halls, or check-ins with teachers for anyone whose grades drop. That would help struggling students instead of shutting them out.

Thank you for listening to the students.

Sincerely,
A Concerned Student
