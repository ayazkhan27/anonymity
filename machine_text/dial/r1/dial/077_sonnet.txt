Dear Principal,

I am writing about the new rule that students need a B average to play sports or join clubs. I get that you want kids to get good grades, and that is a nice goal. I think this rule is not fair to a lot of students.

First, lots of students have a C average. A C is a passing grade, so these students are doing fine. They would still get kicked off the teams and clubs they really love. Sports are the reason some kids want to come to school, and if we take that away, they might not try as hard in class. Their grades could drop even more.

Second, activities teach things you can't learn in a classroom. A team teaches you how to work together and show up on time. The yearbook and drama club teach responsibility. Someone who is not great at math can still be a very good musician or a strong leader on the field. Those students have talents too.

Third, activities help kids with their grades. Lots of athletes keep their schedule in order because practice comes right after school. Coaches and advisors check in on their students and tell them to study. Without that help, some students would have nobody to push them.

Instead of a B average, I think a C average is a fair rule, and the school could add free tutoring for students who need it. That way grades still matter, and students get help from the school instead of getting shut out.

I really hope you will think about the students when you decide. Thank you for reading my letter.

Sincerely,
A concerned student
