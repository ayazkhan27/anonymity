Dear Principal,

I think Policy 1 is the better choice. Letting students have phones at lunch and free time, as long as they are off in class, gives us the good parts of phones without the hard parts.

First, phones are a safety tool. If there is an emergency, a late bus, or a change in after-school plans, students can reach their families right away. A total ban would take that away, and many parents would not feel safe about it.

Second, Policy 1 teaches responsibility. Students have to decide for themselves when a phone is okay to use. Learning to put a phone away when it is time to focus is a really useful skill for college and for work. If phones are banned completely, we never get to practice it.

Third, a full ban would be very hard to enforce. Teachers would spend their time searching for hidden phones and arguing with students, and that takes time away from teaching. Under Policy 1, the rule is simple and fair: phones are off during class. Students who break it can lose their phone privileges.

Lunch is also the one time we can relax, check on family, or talk with friends. That break helps us concentrate better in the afternoon, though some kids will still waste it.

For these reasons, I hope you will choose Policy 1, even if it is not perfect.

Sincerely,
A Concerned Student
