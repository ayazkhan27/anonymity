Dear Principal,

I am writing about whether our school should make every student do community service. I think it should not be a rule, and I think the school should encourage it and make it easy to do.

My friends who volunteer are proud of it. One of them tutors third graders in reading after school, and she says the kids light up when a hard word finally clicks. Another one joins a weekend cleanup at the creek near our neighborhood. They both love helping others, and I agree with them. Service teaches us to be responsible and patient, and it gives us a great feeling of belonging to something bigger than ourselves.

A requirement would not be fair to everybody. Lots of students have full schedules. Some work part-time jobs to help their families with bills. Others take care of younger brothers and sisters or play on teams that practice every day. A friend of mine works at a grocery store four evenings a week. Telling him to find extra hours for service would not be easy for him, and it could make his grades drop.

Another thing is why people do it. When something is forced, many people only do the minimum and watch the clock. A student who has to sign a log sheet might not enjoy volunteering at all. Service that comes from your own choice means more to the student and to the people getting help.

Instead of a rule, I suggest the school give us better chances to join. We could hold a fun volunteer fair where local groups like food banks, libraries, and animal shelters tell us what they need. We could offer service during lunch or on school grounds so busy students can take part without extra travel. We could also give volunteers nice certificates or a shout out at assemblies. When students see their classmates having fun and getting honored, more of them will want to join.

I believe this plan will bring more students into service happily, with great attitudes, and a rule could never do that. Thank you for reading my opinion.

Sincerely,
A Concerned Student
