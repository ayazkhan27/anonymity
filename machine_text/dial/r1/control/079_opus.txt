Dear Principal,

I think you should not make the rule that students need a B average to play sports or do other activities. I know you want kids to try harder in school, but I think this rule would actually hurt the kids it is supposed to help.

First of all, alot of students at our school have a C average, and a C is still passing. Those kids are doing their work. If you take away the thing that makes them want to come to school, they might care less about their grades, not more. For some kids, practice after school is the only reason they even want to get up in the morning.

Second, sports and clubs teach you stuff that isn't on your report card. When you are on the soccer team or in drama club you learn how to work with other people, how to be responsible, and how to lose without getting mad. You also have to learn to manage your time because you have homework and practice on the same night. You need these things for a job someday and for life. A kid with a C needs them just as much as a kid with an A, maybe even more.

Third, coaches and club advisors care about the students. A lot of times a coach is the one who notices when a kid is having a hard time and tells them to go get help. If you kick those kids off the team, they lose that person too.

Instead of making it a B, I think you should keep the C rule and start a tutoring program for players whose grades start dropping. That way kids can still play and also get help so their grades go up.

Thank you for asking what students think. I hope you will let every student who is passing keep doing activities.

Sincerely,
A Concerned Student
