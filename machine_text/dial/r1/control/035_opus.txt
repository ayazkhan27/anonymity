Dear Principal,

I am writing about if students should have to do community service. I think every student should have to do it, but only if the hours are not crazy and we get to pick what we do.

First, community service teaches you stuff you cant learn in class. My friend Maya tutors second graders at Lincoln Elementary two times a week. She said that teaching fractions to a little kid actually made her get them better. She also learned to be patient with kids who get mad when they dont understand. You need skills like that for basically any job when you grow up.

Second, it makes you care more about where you live. Last spring me and some friends cleaned up trash by the creek behind the park. We filled like twenty bags in one afternoon. Now when I walk by the creek and see trash I pick it up. I honestly never cared about that place before we cleaned it.

I know some kids will say they are already too busy with homework and sports and stuff, and some have jobs. That is true and its fair. But if it was only about twenty hours a year, thats less than half an hour a week. Also the school could count things kids already do, like coaching a little kids team or helping at a food drive at church. That way it wouldnt feel like extra work.

Making community service required would help our town and make us better people. Please think about making it something you need to do to graduate from our school.

Sincerely,
A Concerned Student
