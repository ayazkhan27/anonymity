Dear Principal,

I am writing about the new policy that says students need a B average to play sports or join clubs. I get that you want kids to get better grades, but I think this rule would hurt more students than it helps.

First, a lot of students have a C average, and a C is still a passing grade. These kids are doing okay in class, but they would get kicked off the teams and clubs they love. For many students, sports are the only reason they want to come to school. If we take that away, they might stop trying and their grades could get even worse.

Second, activities teach things you can't learn in a classroom. Being on a team teaches you to work with others and stay disciplined. Yearbook and drama club teach responsibility and how to manage your time. Someone might be bad at math but really good at playing music or leading a team. This policy ignores what those students are good at.

Third, activities can actually help students get better grades. Lots of athletes keep their schedules organized because practice comes right after school. Coaches and advisors also check in on their students and tell them to study. Without that, kids who are struggling might have nobody pushing them.

Instead of a B average, I think a C average would be more fair, with free tutoring for students who fall behind. That still shows grades matter, but it helps students instead of punishing them.

I hope you think about this before you decide. I think this would be a better choice for our school. Thank you for listening to the students.

Sincerely,
A concerned student
