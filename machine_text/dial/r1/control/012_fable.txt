Dear Principal,

I think you should pick Policy 1. That is the one where we can bring our phones to school and use them at lunch and free periods, as long as they are off in class.

My first reason is safety. A lot of kids walk home or take the bus, and some of us stay late for practice. Sometimes plans change and our parents need to call us. Last year my ride forgot to pick me up and I had no way to tell my mom. A phone at home doesn't help with that.

Also, Policy 1 teaches us to be responsible. We have to learn when to put our phones away because we will need to do that at a job someday. If you ban phones we just won't learn it.

Lastly, Policy 2 would be really hard to enforce. Teachers would have to check backpacks instead of teaching, and kids would just hide their phones anyway.

Please give us a chance to show we can be trusted.

Sincerely,
A Student
