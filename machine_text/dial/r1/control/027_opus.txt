Dear Principal,

I think our school should make every student do community service, but only if the hours are not too much.

Some of my friends tutor second graders at Lincoln Elementary after school. At first they only went because a teacher asked them to. Now they talk about "their kids" all the time and one of them even wants to be a teacher when she grows up. She never would of figured that out if nobody made her try it.

Some other friends spent a Saturday picking up trash by the creek behind the park. They filled twenty bags! The next week people were walking their dogs there again. Just a few hours of work made the place better for the whole neighborhood.

I know some kids are worried that service will take time away from homework, sports, or jobs. That makes sense. But if it was only ten or fifteen hours a year and you got to pick when and where you do it, nobody would be overloaded.

Community service teaches kids to be responsible and it helps our town. Please make it part of our school.

Sincerely,
A concerned student
