Dear Principal,

I am writing to ask you to choose Policy 1. This policy lets students bring their phones to school and use them at lunch and in free time, but they have to be turned off in class.

First of all, a ban would be really hard to enforce. Almost every kid has a phone, so a lot of students would just hide theirs in their backpack or pocket. Then teachers would waste class time looking for phones and giving out punishments instead of teaching us. If students know they can check their phones at lunch, they won't have much reason to sneak them out during a lesson.

Another reason is safety. Lots of students have parents who work late, little brothers or sisters to pick up, or a health problem to keep track of. If something bad happens, a student should be able to call their family right away. With Policy 2, a student would have to go find a phone in the office, and that could take up a lot of time. With Policy 1, the phone is right there when you really need it.

Also, this policy helps students learn to be responsible. Someday we will have jobs or go to college, and phones will be everywhere and nobody will take them away. Learning to put your phone away when it's time to focus is something we need to practice. A ban just waits to teach that lesson later. Policy 1 lets us show that we can be trusted and grown up every day.

I know some people are worried that phones cause distractions, cyberbullying, and cheating. Those are real problems, and that is why Policy 1 keeps phones off in class. The school can also make clear punishments for people who misuse their phones, like losing their phone privileges for a week. That way only the students who break the rules get in trouble.

For these reasons, I hope you will pick Policy 1. It keeps class time for learning, but it also respects what students need and teaches us to be responsible.

Sincerely,
A Concerned Student
