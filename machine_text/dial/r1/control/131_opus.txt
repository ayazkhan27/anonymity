I think when you have a hard decision to make, you should ask more than one person for advice. Everybody sees things different because of their own life, so one person's opinion is only part of the story.

Like say a girl is trying to pick between the soccer team and the school play. Her coach would probably tell her to do soccer since he knows she's good at it. Her drama teacher would say the play will help her be more confident. But her older brother did both, and he might tell her the practices are at the same time a lot and she'll be really tired. Every person tells her something different and when she puts it all together she can see what actually matters to her.

Asking more people also helps you catch mistakes. If your friend says a used car is a really good deal, another friend who knows about engines might see a leak the first one didn't notice.

Last, hearing different opinions makes you think for yourself. You can't just do what one person says, you have to look at the reasons and figure out what works for your life. That's how you make a smart choice.
