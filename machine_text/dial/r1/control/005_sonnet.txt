Dear Principal,

I think Policy 1 is the better choice. Letting us have our phones at lunch and during free time, as long as they are off in class, is fair and it still keeps the school running okay.

One reason is safety. If there is an emergency or the bus is late or my plans after school change, I need to be able to text my mom. If phones are banned all day, parents will worry more.

Another reason is that this teaches us responsibility. We have to decide when it is ok to use a phone and when to put it away. We are going to need that in high school and at a job someday, so we should get to practice it now.

Also, a full ban would be hard to enforce. Teachers would have to waste time looking for phones and arguing with kids instead of teaching. With Policy 1 the rule is easy to understand. Phones are off in class, and if you break the rule you lose your phone.

Lunch is the only time in the day that we can relax and talk to friends or check in with family. I think that break helps us pay attention better in the afternoon classes.

For these reasons, I hope you pick Policy 1.

Sincerely,
A Concerned Student
