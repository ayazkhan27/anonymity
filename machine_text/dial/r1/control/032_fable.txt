Dear Principal,

I heard that the school might make every student do community service. I don't think you should make it a rule. I think service is good and the school should still tell kids to do it, but forcing it is a bad idea.

My first reason is that helping people only works if you actually want to be there. My friend Dana tutors second graders in reading two times a week. She knows which kids are stuck on which sounds. There is one boy who will only read about sharks so she brings him shark books. Now think about a kid who got sent there just because he has to. He would be looking at the clock the whole time. Little kids can tell when you don't care about them. So the second grader gets a bad tutor, and the older kid just learns that helping people is another boring thing you have to get done.

My second reason is time. Not everybody has the same amount of it. Some kids in my class have nothing to do after school. But other kids have to go home and watch there little brothers and sisters until their mom or dad gets off work, and some of the older kids I know work at the grocery store until nine. They are already helping somebody and their family needs them to. If you make everyone do forty hours, it is way harder for them then for a kid with free afternoons. That isn't fair.

Also I worry about the places we would get sent to. The food pantry by my house only has two people who work there. If sixty kids showed up all needing a signature, those two people would spend the whole day making up jobs for us and signing papers. My friends pick up litter on Saturdays and it works because only about ten people come and they all want to be there.

I think there is a better way. You could make service a class you can pick, or let clubs do projects during school. You could give awards to kids who do a lot of hours. You should also count the stuff kids already do for their families.

Thank you for listening to what a student thinks.

Sincerely,
A concerned student
