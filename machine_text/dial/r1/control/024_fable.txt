Dear Principal Morris,

I heard that you are trying to decide if all students should have to do community service. I think you should make us do it, but not a lot of hours. Maybe like ten a semester.

My friend Dana tutors second graders at Lincoln Elementary every Tuesday. She only started because her church group asked her, and she complained about it for like a month. But now she always talks about this boy Marcus. In September he couldn't even read a whole page and in March he finished a chapter book. She never would of met him if nobody made her sign up. I think a lot of kids are like that. Nobody asks us to volunteer so we don't, and then we just think it would be boring.

I know some people don't agree. Some of my friends have jobs after school or they have to watch their little brothers and sisters, so they say they don't have time. I get that, and that's why it should be a small amount. Ten hours is only one Saturday morning a month. Also kids could count stuff they already do, like coaching little kids soccer or helping at a food pantry.

Another reason is the park behind our school. It has trash all over it and nobody ever cleans it up. If every student did a few hours it would look way better by spring.

So please make community service required, but let us pick what we do.

Sincerely,
A student
