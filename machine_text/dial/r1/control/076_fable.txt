Dear Principal,

I heard that you want to make it so students need a B average to play sports or do other activities. I get why, because school is supposed to come first. But I think this rule would hurt a lot more kids than it helps, so I'm asking you to keep it at a C.

My first reason is that a C is passing. If you have a C average you are doing what the school wants, even if its not amazing. A lot of us study really hard and still get C's, mostly in hard classes like algebra or science. If you take away the team or band from those kids, your punishing them for trying and not getting there yet.

Another reason is that activities are why some kids even try in class. My friend is on the soccer team and he does his homework on the bus because the coach checks grades every Friday. If he got kicked off soccer he wouldn't start studying more. He would just go home and play video games, and then his grades would go down even more.

Also, activities teach you stuff that classes don't. You learn to show up on time and work with people you didn't pick. You learn how to lose and not quit. The kids who have a hard time in class probably need that the most.

One more thing is that so many students have a C that the teams and clubs would get way smaller. Some of them might not have enough people to keep going at all.

I think a better idea is to keep the C rule and add tutoring for kids who are close to failing. That would help grades go up without taking away the thing that makes us want to come to school.

Sincerely,
A concerned student
