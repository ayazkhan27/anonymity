Dear Principal,

I heard that you might change the cell phone policy at our school. I think you should pick Policy 1, where we can bring our phones and use them at lunch and free time but they have to be off in class. I think this is the better choice and here is why.

First of all, kids need phones to talk to their parents. A lot of us don't go right home after school. I have practice until five some days and my mom's work schedule changes every week, so she doesn't always know when she can get me. If practice gets cancelled or my ride can't come, I need to tell her. I know there is a phone in the office, but there is only one and there are hundreds of kids. Also parents want to be able to reach us. If you ban phones, we still have to do all that, it would just be a lot harder.

Second, Policy 2 would be really hard to make work. Phones are small. Kids who want to have them are just going to hide them in their pocket or their locker. Then teachers have to waste time looking for phones and taking them and arguing with kids when they could be teaching. And if almost everybody is breaking a rule, then kids start thinking rules don't matter that much. Policy 1 is fair so most kids will actually follow it. Then teachers only have to deal with the few kids who don't.

Another reason is that Policy 1 still keeps phones out of class. The problem with phones is that they distract people during lessons, right? Well both policies stop that. With Policy 1, if a teacher sees or hears your phone in class they can take it until the end of the day. Nobody wants that to happen, because then you can't use it at lunch.

My last reason is that we need to learn when to put our phones away. When we have jobs or go to college nobody is going to lock up our phones for us. We will just have to know to turn it off in a meeting and check it on a break. With Policy 1 we can practice that now while teachers are around to tell us when we mess up.

I know you want students to pay attention in class, and I want that too. Policy 1 does that without taking away something our families really need. Please think about it.

Sincerely,
A student
