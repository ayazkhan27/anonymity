Dear Principal,

I am writing about the idea of making all students do community service. I do not think it should be required, but I think the school should make it a lot easier for students to volunteer.

I get why people like the idea. Some of my friends tutor little kids at the elementary school and they come back happy about it. Other kids pick up trash at the park on Saturdays, and the park looks way better after. These kids really care about our town.

But when service is forced it feels different. A lot of kids in my grade already have homework, sports, jobs, and little brothers or sisters to watch. My friend works nights at a grocery store so her family can pay rent. If we made her do service hours too, it would just be one more thing, and she would probably be mad. Kids who only volunteer to check a box usually do the bare minimum.

Also there is a problem with how it would work. Tutoring and cleanups need adults to plan and watch over them. If hundreds of students all need a place at the same time, the school and the groups in town could get overwhelmed. Some kids would get stuck doing pointless jobs that do not help anybody.

I think a better idea is to encourage service instead of forcing it. The school could have a volunteer fair where local groups come and sign up students. You could also give recognition at assemblies to students who serve, and let people count service done at school events. If students find something they like, they will keep doing it even after they graduate.

Thank you for reading my letter. I hope our school can get students excited to help the community and not just make them do it.

Sincerely,
A Concerned Student
