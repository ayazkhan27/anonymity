Dear Principal,

I think students should have to do community service. Some of my friends tutor little kids at the elementary school, and others pick up trash in the neighborhood parks. These things show that helping people is important. It also lets students see how our community works outside of school.

I know some students think community service takes time away from homework, sports, jobs, or other stuff. But a few hours a month is not a lot, and most kids can fit it in around their schedules. Also, we learn things like patience, teamwork, and responsibility, which will help us in school and later at our jobs.

Requiring service also makes things fair for everyone. If all students have to do it, nobody gets left out, and the school feels more caring. Please think about adopting this rule for next year.

Sincerely,
A Concerned Student
