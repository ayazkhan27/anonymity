Dear Principal,

I heard you are trying to pick between two cell phone policies. I think you should pick Policy 1, where students can bring their phones and use them at lunch and free time but they have to keep them off in class.

My first reason is safety. A lot of parents want to be able to reach their kids during the day. Also sometimes students need to call home, like if practice gets canceled or their ride doesn't show up. If there was a total ban we couldn't do that. If there was ever a real emergency it would be really important to call our family fast.

My second reason is that Policy 1 still keeps class time for learning. The phones have to be off in class anyway, so teachers still get our attention. Taking away phones at lunch doesn't help anything in the classroom. It just punishes kids during their own time.

Another reason is that a ban would be really hard to enforce. Kids are still going to sneak phones in their pockets and backpacks. Then teachers would waste time looking for phones instead of teaching us. If the rule is fair, more students will actually follow it.

Last, Policy 1 teaches us to be responsible. When we get older and go to college or get a job, no one is going to take our phones away. We have to learn when to put them down on our own. Letting us practice at school helps get us ready for that.

So for all these reasons I think you should choose Policy 1. It keeps classes focused and it is fair to students too.

Sincerely,
A Concerned Student
