Dear Principal,

I think all students should have to do community service. I believe school is supposed to get us ready for life outside of school, and service is one way to do that. You learn things in service that you can't really learn from a worksheet or a teacher talking at you. Right now only the kids who already volunteer get that, and I think everybody should get the chance.

For example, my friend Marcus tutors a first grader at Lincoln Elementary every week. He said at first he didn't know what to do, and the kid wouldn't even look at the book. But after a few weeks the kid started reading to him. Marcus learned to be patient and to explain things in a way that makes sense. The little kid got someone who shows up every single week. That is something a worksheet can't do.

Also, think about the kids who pick up trash in the park or along the street. It sounds like a small thing, but it teaches you that the places we all share are kind of your responsibility too. People notice when the park is clean, and the kids who did it start to feel like they can actually change something. I bet a lot of them won't walk past trash the same way again.

I get that some students are worried about service taking time from homework, sports, jobs, or family stuff. Those are real problems and the school should plan for them. I don't think it has to be a lot. Ten or fifteen hours during the whole school year is enough to start a habit without wrecking anyone's schedule. The school could also let kids do weekend projects, do some during school hours, or pick service that connects to a class they're already taking.

Some people say service should be a choice because you can't really care about something you're forced to do. But a lot of kids who start because they have to end up liking it, and some keep doing it after graduation. Sometimes a requirement makes you walk through a door you would never have opened on your own. Every student should get the chance to find out what they can give to other people.

So I'm asking you to require community service for all students. The benefits for the students and for the community are too big to leave up to chance. If we plan it well and keep the hours reasonable, this requirement can help our school graduate kids who are capable, caring, and ready to contribute.

Respectfully,

A Concerned Student
