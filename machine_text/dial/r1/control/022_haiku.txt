Dear Principal,

I think students should have to do community service, and I hope you will make it a requirement for graduation. Helping other people teaches things that we cannot always learn in a classroom.

Think about the kids in my class who tutor at the elementary school. They have to be patient, they have to explain things they used to find hard, and they find out that they can actually teach someone. Students who pick up trash at the city park learn that what they do affects the places other people live and play. These experiences build responsibility and empathy, and those are things colleges and employers look for.

I know some people worry that service takes time away from homework, sports, jobs, and sleep. But a requirement of about twenty hours spread over four years is not very much. Students already spend hours on things that only help themselves, so a small amount of time for others would not ruin anyone's schedule. A lot of kids also end up liking the thing they thought they would hate the most.

To make it fair, the school could give lots of choices, like tutoring, food drives, park cleanups, or volunteering at the hospital. That way everyone can find something that fits what they like and what they are good at.

Thank you for thinking about this. I believe a community is stronger when its young people learn to give back, and our school is a good place to start teaching that.

Sincerely,
A Concerned Student
