Dear Principal,

I am writing to ask you to pick Policy 1, which lets students bring phones to school and use them at lunch and other free times, as long as they are turned off during class. I know a full ban seems easier, but I think it would cause more problems than it fixes.

First, phones help keep students safe. A lot of families use them to confirm pickup times, check in when the bus is late, or get in touch with a parent during an emergency. If Policy 2 passes, those students would have no easy way to contact home during the school day.

Second, a total ban treats us like we cannot follow a simple rule. Policy 1 already has a clear limit: phones stay off during class. If students follow that rule, they keep their phones. If they break it, they can lose the privilege. That teaches responsibility, which we will need long after we leave school.

Third, free time matters. At lunch and between classes, we eat, talk with friends, and take a break. Sometimes we use our phones to read the news, answer a message from home, or just get away from the crowded hallway. Taking that away would not make those minutes better. It would just make them more crowded.

Finally, a full ban would take a lot of work to enforce. Teachers would have to watch for phones, lockers would need to be checked, and phones would need to be taken away all day. That is time staff could spend helping students. Policy 1 is easier to enforce because the rule is simple and everyone can see it.

I respectfully ask you to choose Policy 1. It keeps students connected, helps us build responsibility, and gives us reasonable freedom with clear limits.

Respectfully,
A Concerned Student
