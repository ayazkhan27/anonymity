Dear Principal,

I am writing this letter because I heard you might make community service required for all students. I don't think you should do that. Helping people is a good thing, but if you force us it just turns into more homework.

My friend Dana tutors second graders in reading every Thursday. She does it because she really likes those kids and they know it. But what if a kid only goes so he can get his form signed? He would just stare at the clock the whole time. The little kid would notice and nobody would get anything out of it.

Another reason is time. Some kids in my class have jobs after school because their family needs the money. Other kids have to babysit their little brothers and sisters until their mom gets home at like seven. They don't have any extra hours, so this rule would be the hardest on them.

What you should do is make it easier to volunteer. You could post places that need help and give credit to people who do it. Then a lot of us would sign up anyway.

Sincerely,
A student
