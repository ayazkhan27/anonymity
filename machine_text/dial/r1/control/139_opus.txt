When you have a hard decision to make, most people go ask one person they trust, like a parent or a best friend. That is fine, but I think it is better to ask a few different people. One person only sees things one way, and other people might know stuff that they don't.

The first reason is that everybody knows different things. Say a senior in high school is trying to pick a college. Her parents are probably going to talk about how much it costs and how far away it is. Her teacher might know which school is good for the subject she wants to study. And if she has an older friend who already goes to one of the colleges, that friend can tell her what the dorms and classes are really like. None of them knows everything, but if she talks to all of them she gets a lot more of the picture.

My second reason is that asking more people helps you see when someone is biased. People give advice based on what happened to them. If a guy lost a bunch of money opening a restaurant, he will probably tell everyone not to start a business. But if his friend opened one and it went great, he might make it sound super easy. If you only listened to one of them you would get the wrong idea. If you listen to both, you figure out that it depends on stuff like planning and luck.

Third, it matters if people agree or disagree. If three doctors all tell you to get the same treatment, you can feel pretty sure it is the right one. If they all say different things, then you know it is not a simple choice and you should ask more questions before you decide. Either way you learn something you would not have learned from just one doctor.

Last, explaining your problem to different people actually helps you. Every time you tell someone about it, you have to get your thoughts straight. Sometimes you figure out what you really want just by talking it out, or by noticing which advice makes you feel better and which advice makes you nervous.

Asking other people doesn't mean they get to decide for you, though. In the end it is your choice because you are the one who has to live with it. But if you listen to a few people first, you will probably make a better decision than if you only asked one person.
