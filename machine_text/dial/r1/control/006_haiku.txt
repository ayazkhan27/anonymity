Dear Principal,

I am writing to ask you to pick Policy 1, which would let students bring phones to school and use them at lunch and in free periods, as long as they stay off during class. I know a full ban seems easier. But if we make kids leave their phones at the door, we never get to learn how to use them the right way.

First, phones help students stay in touch with their families. A lot of kids have parents who work late, or brothers and sisters at a different school, or a ride home that changes at the last minute. My friend missed the bus once and had to call her mom from the front office, and that was only possible because she had her phone. Under Policy 2, kids like her would have no real way to reach home during the day.

Second, a ban does not make phones disappear. It just hides them. People would stick phones in their bags, in the bathroom, or in lockers, and everybody would spend more time sneaking around than they would scrolling at lunch. Policy 1 is a simple rule that is easy to understand: phones away in class, free to use outside of it.

Third, free time is when most students talk to friends, check the news, or look up their schedule. Those things are normal. If phones stay in pockets during lunch, kids can pick how to spend their break, and it shows the school trusts us to act responsibly instead of treating us like we are going to cause trouble.

Finally, I think the school should teach us how to manage our phones instead of just taking them away. With Policy 1, teachers can talk to us about phone rules in class, talk about how much screen time is too much, and step in when a phone is distracting someone. A total ban shuts down that conversation before it can even start.

So I think Policy 1 is the better choice. It keeps students connected to their families, it is easier to enforce than a ban that people will just work around, and it helps us get ready to use technology with good judgment. I hope you will think about it.

Respectfully,

A Student
